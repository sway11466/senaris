SUBJECT: A flight mark — ONE hare in full bounding stride, seen flat in profile, running to the RIGHT and rising slightly, its body laid along a diagonal from the lower left up to the upper right. The head is at the upper right, the nose pointing at the right edge of the frame; the two long ears lie flat back along the neck, pointing to the left. The front legs reach forward to the right, the hind legs kick far out behind to the lower left, so the body is fully stretched in one long leap with no leg touching anything. The hare is lean, bony and angular, a hunted thing bolting away and never turning to fight — NOT round, NOT fluffy, NOT cute, no big eyes. Only the outline carries it: no fur texture, no whiskers, no inner detail other than one flat eye. Nothing else is drawn: NO ground, NO dust, NO speed lines, NO motion trails, NO grass. DRAW IT BIG: the hare spans the full width of the square canvas, the tips of the hind feet touching or nearly touching the left edge and the nose close to the right edge, the ear tips and head reaching toward the top edge and the hind feet toward the bottom edge. Leave only a thin margin, a few percent of the canvas — do NOT float a small mark in a large empty background. Warm amber-gold, one light tone with at most one slightly darker tone of the same amber inside the body, hard flat edges — a branding-iron mark, NOT a rendered animal, NO shine, NO glow. Pure black background, a single flat color with no wood grain (for easy cutout).
