SUBJECT: A standoff mark — THREE heavy swords pointing inward at one another, seen flat, arranged like the three spokes of a wheel spaced evenly a third of a turn apart. The first sword's hilt sits at the TOP CENTRE of the frame and its blade points straight down toward the centre. The second sword's hilt sits in the BOTTOM-LEFT corner and its blade points up and to the right toward the centre. The third sword's hilt sits in the BOTTOM-RIGHT corner and its blade points up and to the left toward the centre. The three swords are the same size and shape, and the mark is left-right mirrored about a vertical axis through the centre. The three points stop short of one another: around the very centre of the frame is ONE clear, open gap of background, a small empty space the three points aim into — the blades never touch, never cross, never overlap. It is three foes holding one another at a distance, none daring to step in first. Each sword is broad, thick and crude: a wide straight blade tapering to a blunt point, a heavy straight crossguard set square across the blade and standing well out on both sides, a short grip and a round pommel at the outer end. Only the outline carries it: no fuller, no engraving, no wrapping, no gems. Nothing else is drawn: NO hands, NO shields, NO ring or circle joining them, NO sparks, NO clash, NO motion lines. DRAW IT BIG: the mark fills the square canvas, the top pommel touching or nearly touching the top edge, the two lower pommels close to the bottom-left and bottom-right corners. Leave only a thin margin, a few percent of the canvas — do NOT float a small mark in a large empty background. Warm amber-gold, one light tone with at most one slightly darker tone of the same amber on the crossguards and grips, hard flat edges — a branding-iron mark, NOT rendered steel, NO shine, NO glow. Pure black background, a single flat color with no wood grain (for easy cutout).
