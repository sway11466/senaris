SUBJECT: A revival mark — ONE plain upright cross seen flat from the front, symmetrical about both the vertical and the horizontal axis. Two thick bars of the same width crossing at the centre of the canvas: one vertical, one horizontal, each about a quarter of the canvas wide, with square flat ends; all four arms are the same length. A solid, simple shape in one flat amber, with at most a slightly darker amber as a plain narrow inner band along the bars. NO circle behind it, NO rays, NO flared or pointed ends, NO ornament, NO figure. A plain holy cross — the fallen stand again. DRAW IT BIG: the ends of the four arms touch or nearly touch the top, bottom, left and right edges of the square canvas. Leave only a thin margin, a few percent of the canvas — do NOT float a small mark in a large empty background. Warm amber-gold, one light tone plus at most one slightly darker tone of the same amber, hard flat edges — a branding-iron mark, NOT rendered metal, NO shine, NO glow. Pure black background (single flat color, for easy cutout).
