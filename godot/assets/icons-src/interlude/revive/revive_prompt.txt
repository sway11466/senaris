SUBJECT: A winged cross badge — a small holy emblem that brings the fallen back to the ranks. ONE upright cross of warm polished gold stands at the centre of the square canvas, symmetrical about its vertical axis: a thick vertical shaft, crossed at the upper third by a shorter horizontal bar of the same thickness, all four ends cut square and flat, with a plain bevel down the middle of each bar catching the light. From behind the horizontal bar, one pair of wings spreads out to the left and to the right, the same width on both sides, made of three or four large simple feather shapes each, in soft off-white shaded clearly grey along their undersides so they do not wash out against pale paper. The wings are held out level, their tips reaching the left and right edges of the canvas; they do NOT rise above the top of the cross. NO halo, NO rays, NO sparkles, NO chain or cord, NO face, NO figure, NO stone base, NO ground. It is a small badge held in the hand, not a monument standing in earth. DRAW IT BIG: the top of the cross touches or nearly touches the top edge, the foot of the cross the bottom edge, the wingtips the left and right edges. Leave only a thin margin, a few percent of the canvas.
