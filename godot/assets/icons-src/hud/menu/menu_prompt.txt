SUBJECT: A menu mark — ONE cogwheel seen flat from the front, symmetrical about both the vertical and the horizontal axis. A thick solid ring carries eight broad square teeth spaced evenly around its rim, each tooth as wide as the gap beside it, every tooth blunt and flat-topped; the ring's inner edge is a plain circle around one round hole in the centre, the hole about a third of the wheel's width. Exactly eight teeth: one pointing straight up, one straight down, one straight left, one straight right, and one in each of the four diagonals between. Nothing inside the ring but the hole: NO spokes, NO second ring, NO bolts, NO smaller gear. A plain, sturdy machine part. DRAW IT BIG: the wheel fills the square canvas, the tips of the top, bottom, left and right teeth touching or nearly touching the four edges of the frame. Leave only a thin margin, a few percent of the canvas — do NOT float a small mark in a large empty background. Warm amber-gold, one flat light tone with at most one slightly darker tone of the same amber inside the ring, hard flat edges — a branding-iron mark, NOT rendered metal, NO shine, NO glow. Pure black background (single flat color, for easy cutout).
