SUBJECT: Two claw gashes torn across the board. The long gash runs from the lower
left corner to the upper right corner, its two tips touching those corners so it
spans the whole square. It is a tapered wedge: wide and open in the middle,
narrowing to sharp points at both tips. A second, shorter gash of the same shape
runs parallel to it, set below and to the right, its length about half the long
one. Both gashes lean the same way, from lower left to upper right, and they do
not touch. Nothing else in the frame.
