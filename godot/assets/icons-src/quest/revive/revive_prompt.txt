SUBJECT: A heart with a heartbeat line running through it. The heart is one bold
rounded shape, wide and blunt, filling the square canvas from edge to edge with
only a thin margin. Across the middle of the heart a single heartbeat line is cut
out of the shape as an empty channel: it enters flat from the left side, dips
once, then jumps into one tall sharp peak that points straight up, and leaves
flat to the right side. The peak is the tallest part of the line and sits just
right of centre. The channel is thick and open so it stays readable when small.
Nothing else in the frame.
