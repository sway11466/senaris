STYLE は doc/art/units.md §3.4 の EFFECT STYLE を土台に1点だけ差し替えた（差し替えの記録）:
  ・向き＝「右へ向かう一撃」を外し、正面・直立・左右対称にする（飛ぶ絵でも振り抜く絵でもない）。
  ・構図＝正方形のまま（シールドウォールは盾の形に合わせて縦長にしたが、X字は幅と高さが
    釣り合うので外さない）。
  ・中心＝「面積の大きい絵は中心を空洞にする」も外さない。交差した細い刃なので、X の内側は
    背景のまま空き、下の駒が透ける。

意匠は特定のユニットに寄せない（紋章や固有の武器を写すと、出た駒によって「誰々の剣」に見える）。
無紋の直剣2本で描く。シールドウォールが盾ならカウンターは交差した剣＝守りと迎え撃ちが対になって読める。
火花・光は描かない（発動の時点ではまだ噛み合っていない＝光る理由が無い）。

SUBJECT: TWO plain straight SWORDS crossed in an X, seen straight from the FRONT, standing upright and perfectly left-right symmetrical — the sign that two soldiers have set themselves to strike back.

Both blades point UP and outward, their hilts down and outward at the bottom, crossing a little above the middle of the frame so the X is wide at the top and narrower at the bottom. No tilt, no perspective, no three-quarter view — flat and face-on, as if it were a badge. The two swords are identical and mirror each other exactly.

Each sword is a plain unmarked arming sword: a straight double-edged blade with a simple straight crossguard, a short grip and a round pommel. NO heraldry, NO crest, NO engraving, NO gem, NO letters.

Drawn in three flat tones only, with hard edges throughout: a pale blue-grey steel blade, a darker blue-grey for the crossguards, grips and pommels, and a thick dark outline around every shape. Nothing is drawn behind, beside or between the blades — the space inside the X stays empty background.

NO glow, NO shine streaks, NO sparks, NO clash, NO magic, NO aura, NO motion lines, NO damage or dents, NO hands, NO owner.

The pair fills the frame edge to edge with only a thin margin. Square 1:1 composition. Pure black background (single flat color, for easy cutout).
