SUBJECT: FIVE chibi clergy praying together — TWO clerics, TWO priests and ONE bishop — gathered in a close cluster on open ground, seen from a slightly low angle. This is a blessing, not an attack: the mood is warm, calm and uplifting.

All five are PRAYING in silence, eyes CLOSED and mouths CLOSED, heads bowed over the holy symbol each of them holds, faces serene and absorbed. Nobody sings, shouts, grimaces, strains or looks fierce; no open mouths. Heads are bowed at different depths and their bodies lean at slightly different angles — so the five read as a gathered congregation, not a row of copies.

The bishop stands at the center, slightly forward and taller than the rest, the focal point of the group: silver-white hair, a full grey beard, no hat of any kind, a blue-grey outer robe with wide gold-embroidered borders over a cream inner robe, a gold-trimmed stole across the shoulders and a small cross pendant at his chest. Each holds his cross-topped rod upright in both hands before the chest. Their uniform is identical to each other — separate them only by pose.

The cross is the light source, so all five are lit warmly from above and behind, their robe hems and hoods stirring gently in a soft updraft — everything about the picture should feel like a benediction settling over an army.
