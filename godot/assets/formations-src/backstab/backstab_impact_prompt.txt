SUBJECT: The mark left by a single silent strike into an enemy's back — TWO crossing slash marks and nothing else. Pure black background (single flat color, for easy cutout).

THE MARKS: one long main stroke sweeps from the UPPER LEFT down to the LOWER RIGHT, needle-sharp at both ends and widest near its middle, drawn as one clean hard-edged sliver with a slight curve. A SECOND stroke, about half as long and a little thinner, crosses it at a steep angle so the two form a narrow X — a quick second cut, not a symmetrical star. The crossing point sits slightly ABOVE the center of the frame.

Keep both strokes SLENDER: together they must cover only a small part of the frame, with plenty of empty background between and around them, so a game piece drawn underneath stays visible. NO ring, NO circle, NO shockwave, NO burst of lines, NO impact star, NO blood, NO droplets.

COLOR: silver-white core with a pale ice-blue edge, two or three flat tones only, hard outlines, no glow and no gradient. A few very small pale flecks may trail off the far ends of the strokes, drawn as flat hard shapes, never as sparkle or haze.

The two strokes fill the frame edge to edge with only a thin margin. Square 1:1 composition.
