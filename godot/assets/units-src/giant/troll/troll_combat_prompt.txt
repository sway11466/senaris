SUBJECT: The SAME troll as the troll board piece — a huge, slow, thick-hided cave brute (same tiny sunken head, same knotted grey-green hide, same stone-dust crust on the shoulders, same blunt tusks; this must read as the exact same creature). It is a MONSTER BEAST, not a person and not an orc: big and lumpen with a heavy stooping back, a tiny head sunk low between enormous rounded shoulders, gigantic thick arms hanging past its knees with heavy knuckles, a bloated barrel belly and short bowed legs with broad flat feet. Its hide is its armour: thick, knotted grey-green skin, mottled and leathery, with hard warty plates and a crust of grey stone-dust and lichen across the shoulders and back. Tiny dull red eyes under a heavy brow, a wide flat gash of a mouth with a few blunt tusks, small torn ears. It carries NO weapon and NO tool and wears NOTHING — no clothing, no armour, no belt, no ornament; keep the whole body covered by that knotted hide with no anatomical detail. It swings at the enemy on the LEFT with one massive arm hauled across in a heavy overhand smash while the other hand braces low, shoulders dropped into the blow, both broad feet still flat and planted — a ponderous, crushing swing from something that is strong but slow, NOT a quick jab and NOT a nimble lunge. Keep the mature, muted, limited palette: grey-greens, stone greys and dusty browns, with red only in the eyes.
