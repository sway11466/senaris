SUBJECT: A roller lancer — an ancient automaton that has kept running deep inside ruins for ages, a failed war machine that rolls in fast and stabs from just out of reach. It is a MACHINE in the shape of a tall, heavily built person: the SAME brass-and-rusted-iron build as the gear soldier (dark rusted iron plates held together by big rivets, trimmed with worn brass bands), but with adult proportions and a small head, NOT chibi — a thick, broad, barrel-chested torso with broad shoulders, clearly taller and bulkier than the gear soldier, a strong, chunky silhouette. Its head is a tall, narrow, upright cylinder, longer than it is wide, with ONE round eye lens glowing red in the middle of its face — the only red on it; no mouth, no teeth. It has NO legs: below the waist the body narrows into a sturdy fork that holds ONE big, thick iron wheel with a brass hub, directly under the body like a unicycle; seen from the front the wheel shows as a thick upright tyre standing on the ground. Its arms are thick, heavy and jointed and end in big, chunky iron hands with five thick jointed fingers, like armoured gauntlets. Both hands grip ONE long iron spear with worn brass fittings, held straight upright in front of the middle of the body, the shaft running from near the ground to well above the head, the spearhead pointing straight up; it carries NO shield and NO other weapon. Colours: mostly dark rust-brown iron, darker than a brown cave floor, with worn brass-yellow bands, rivets and the wheel hub catching the light to lift its outline, a dark outline, and the single red eye. Keep the mature, muted, limited palette.
