SUBJECT: A gear soldier — a small ancient automaton that has kept running deep inside ruins for ages, a failed war machine. It is a MACHINE in the shape of a short, stocky person: a squat barrel-shaped body of dark rusted iron plates held together by big rivets, trimmed with worn brass bands. In the middle of its chest is a round porthole window through which one big brass gear can be seen. Its head is a round, dented can-like dome with ONE round eye lens glowing red — the only red on it; no mouth, no teeth. Its arms are thick and short and end in heavy blunt iron lumps instead of hands; it carries NO weapon, NO shield and NO tools. Short thick legs with flat iron feet. Keep it small — about the size of a goblin grunt. Colours: mostly dark rust-brown iron, darker than a brown cave floor, with worn brass-yellow bands and rivets catching the light to lift its outline, a dark outline, and the single red eye. Keep the mature, muted, limited palette.
