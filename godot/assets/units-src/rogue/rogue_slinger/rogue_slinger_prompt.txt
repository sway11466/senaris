SUBJECT: A rogue slinger — a rank-and-file thug of the outlaw gang that traps monsters and sells them, the one who pelts stones from behind the others. He wears the SAME black-and-crimson bandit gear as the rogue (same cheap charcoal-brown tunic, same soft cloth hood, same deep-crimson cloth mask) and must clearly read as one of the same gang. He is a small-time crook, no better than the plain rogue: a nasty little nobody, NOT a trained fighter, NOT an archer, NOT a ranger, NOT an assassin, NOT a ninja, NOT a soldier. He is an ordinary HUMAN: normal human skin, NO tusks, NO fangs, NO horns, NO claws. Build him slight and unimpressive — a wiry, narrow-shouldered frame with thin arms — so the weakness comes from his size and his lack of armour, NOT from ragged clothing. His clothes are cheap and plain but perfectly INTACT and reasonably kept: a simple dark charcoal-brown tunic over grey-brown trousers, a plain leather belt, plain worn boots. NO rips, NO tatters, NO patches, NO frayed hems, NO holes, NO bandage-like rag wrappings — he is a working criminal, not a beggar. Keep the gear light and plain: NO armour plates, NO fitted leather armour, NO shoulder guards, NO rows of buckles, NO polished metal, NO uniform, NO cloak. A soft cloth hood is pulled up over his head and a deep-crimson cloth is tied over his nose and mouth as a mask, leaving only the eyes visible. THE SLING — his only weapon, drawn with correct sling mechanics: a shepherd's sling is a small oval LEATHER CRADLE (a shallow pouch) with TWO long cords running from its two ends. He holds BOTH cord ends TOGETHER in one raised fist at chest height in front of him, and steadies the cradle in his other hand just below it, a single round grey stone sitting in the cradle ready to be loosed — so the whole weapon hangs in one short, neat, nearly vertical line close to the body. CRITICAL — do NOT put one cord in each hand, do NOT draw a big drooping loop or circle of cord, do NOT let the stone sit in the middle of a loop: the cords are gathered in ONE fist and the stone is at the FAR END of them. This is NOT a lasso, NOT a bolas, NOT a whip, and NOT a Y-shaped catapult slingshot (there is no forked frame and no elastic band anywhere). Draw the cords bold and thick and the grey stone large and round so the weapon still reads at tiny size. Absolutely NO bow, NO crossbow, NO arrows, NO quiver, NO sword, NO staff, NO magic. At his hip hangs a fat bulging cloth bag of sling stones, drawn big and heavy so it reads as his ammunition. He also carries the gang's trade tool: a neat coil of rope over one shoulder. No priestly sign anywhere: no cross, no robe, no holy symbol. EXPRESSION — play him as a proper villain, not a victim: the eyes above the mask are narrowed to a hard, sly glare with the brows angled down and inward, wearing the smug, mean look of someone who enjoys picking on the weak from a safe distance. He is NOT frightened, NOT cowering, NOT sad, NOT pitiable; he stands upright and insolent. Keep the mature, muted, limited palette: charcoal, dark browns and greys, with the deep crimson mask as the only colour.
