SUBJECT: A rogue — a rank-and-file thug of the outlaw gang that traps monsters and sells them. He is a small-time crook, the weakest piece on the board: a nasty little nobody, NOT a trained fighter, NOT an assassin, NOT a ninja, NOT a soldier. He is an ordinary HUMAN: normal human skin, NO tusks, NO fangs, NO horns, NO claws. Build him slight and unimpressive — a wiry, narrow-shouldered frame with thin arms — so the weakness comes from his size and his lack of armour, NOT from ragged clothing. His clothes are cheap and plain but perfectly INTACT and reasonably kept: a simple dark charcoal-brown tunic over grey-brown trousers, a plain leather belt, plain worn boots. NO rips, NO tatters, NO patches, NO frayed hems, NO holes, NO bandage-like rag wrappings — he is a working criminal, not a beggar. Keep the gear light and plain: NO armour plates, NO fitted leather armour, NO shoulder guards, NO rows of buckles, NO polished metal, NO uniform, NO cloak. A soft cloth hood is pulled up over his head and a deep-crimson cloth is tied over his nose and mouth as a mask, leaving only the eyes visible. He carries the gang's trade tools: a neat coil of rope over one shoulder and a folded sackcloth bag at his hip. In one hand, held low in front of him, is his only weapon: a small plain knife with a worn wooden handle — modest and unremarkable, NOT a fine dagger, NOT a sword, NOT a bow, NO magic. No priestly sign anywhere: no cross, no robe, no holy symbol. EXPRESSION — play him as a proper villain, not a victim: the eyes above the mask are narrowed to a hard, sly glare with the brows angled down and inward, wearing the smug, mean look of someone who enjoys picking on the weak. He is NOT frightened, NOT cowering, NOT sad, NOT pitiable; he stands upright and insolent. Keep the mature, muted, limited palette: charcoal, dark browns and greys, with the deep crimson mask as the only colour.
