SUBJECT: The SAME matango as the matango board piece — a walking mushroom monster of the deep forest (same deep brick-red cap with dull moss-green spots, same cream stalk body, same calm face; this must read as the exact same character). It is a PLANT, not an animal and not a person in a costume: ONE single squat mushroom — a big rounded cap sitting low over a thick barrel-shaped stalk that IS its body, with two short stubby arms and two short stubby legs formed out of the same mushroom flesh (NO fur, NO scales, NO claws, NO hooves, NO animal limbs), standing on small earth-coloured mycelium roots. NO clothing, NO armour, NO weapon, NO sling, NO staff, NO bow, NO tools or ornaments of any kind. Chibi proportions, but SHORT and WIDE — the cap is the biggest shape in the silhouette, wider than the body is tall, so it reads as a low round lump rather than a standing figure; it must not look like the tall treant. Colours: the cap is a deep brick red with rounded dull moss-green spots scattered over it and a pale cream underside of gills; the stalk body and the stubby limbs are dull off-white cream mushroom flesh with soft grey-brown shading; the root feet are earth brown. Keep the reds and greens muted and earthy — deep brick and dull moss, NOT bright fairy-tale toadstool red, NOT vivid saturated green. Its face is on the front of the stalk, under the cap: a calm, rounded face with two large simple dark eyes and a small quiet mouth — patient and vacant rather than angry, sly, leering or menacing; NO lolling tongue, NO half-lidded smirk, NO fangs. The spore haze is pale ochre-grey, faint and low-contrast, with NO glow, NO rays and NO particles bright enough to bleed into the background. Keep the mature, muted, limited palette: deep brick cap, dull moss spots, cream flesh, earth-brown roots.
