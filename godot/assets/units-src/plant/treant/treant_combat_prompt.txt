POSE (root-brace): A rooted blocking stance, seen from the side — the treant plants itself square across the road facing the enemy on the LEFT, both heavy branch arms spread wide and low to either side to bar the way, shoulders squared and the trunk leaning very slightly into the threat, while its thick roots dig into the ground and spread out around its feet. It does NOT charge, does NOT lunge, does NOT wind up a blow and does NOT raise a guard in front of its face: it simply refuses to move, standing as a wall of wood the enemy has to get past.

SUBJECT: The SAME treant as the treant board piece — an ancient tree that walks, the forest's living wall (same sooty grey-brown bark, same calm hollow face, same dull red eye embers; this must read as the exact same character). It is a PLANT, not an animal and not a person in a costume: a single thick gnarled tree trunk as the body, two heavy branch arms, and a knot of thick roots for feet that grip the ground. NO fur, NO scales, NO animal limbs, NO clothing, NO armour, NO shield, NO helmet, NO weapon, NO tools or ornaments of any kind. Chibi proportions: a huge blunt trunk-head over a squat, extremely wide body and short thick root legs, so it reads as a heavy, immovable block. Its toughness is shown as mass and thickness: deep cracked slabs of bark, big knots and burls, a doubled trunk thickness at the shoulders, and a broad flat-topped silhouette — nothing sharp, nothing spiky. The bark is sooty grey-brown and charcoal, weathered and dry, NOT green: only a few small tufts of dry, dull, brown-green leaves at the shoulders and the ends of the branch arms, small in area and low in saturation, so the piece never blends into the green forest ground it fights on. Its face is part of the trunk — a calm, rounded face formed by two hollows and a wide seam of a mouth, kind and patient rather than angry, snarling or menacing; deep inside the eye hollows a dull red ember glows, and a few small dull-red berries hang among the leaves — the red hollows and berries are the ONLY colour on the piece. Its "feet" are roots, so instead of two clean boots on the ground it stands on a spread mat of thick roots. Keep the mature, muted, limited palette: sooty grey-brown bark, charcoal shadow, dry dull leaves, and the dull red eyes as the single accent.
