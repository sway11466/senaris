SUBJECT: A human cleric — the basic healer and objective-capturer of the steel-blue army. A WOMAN, with the same steel-blue faction palette and the same face-style family as the fighter, but a gentle support healer, not a soldier. A simple hooded robe in steel-blue and off-white cloth with small gold trim. In both hands, held up in front of the chest, she clasps a holy cross — a chunky, clearly readable hand cross / crucifix of wood and gold, the main role prop. NO staff and NO wand (a staff reads as a mage); the cross is what marks her as a cleric. Calm, kindly, devout expression. The plainest of the holy orders — no armor, no ornate mitre. Keep the mature, muted, limited palette.
