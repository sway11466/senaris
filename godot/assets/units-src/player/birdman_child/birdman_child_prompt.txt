SUBJECT: A birdfolk girl — a young girl of the winged cliff-dwelling people, the same winged-human family as the birdman, who has just been taken from her home and is frightened. She looks like a CHILD WITH WINGS: an ordinary human face and head (human eyes, nose and mouth — absolutely NO beak, NO bird head, NO feathered face), human arms and hands, human legs and bare human feet. Same face-style family as the fighter. She is frightened: her eyebrows are drawn up and together in the middle, and her eyes are opened wide. Her face is otherwise unchanged — the fear is carried only by the eyebrows and the opening of the eyes. The STYLE's lines about a calm, confident personality and a charming, heroic look with grit do NOT apply to this child. Her whole posture is small and closed: shoulders pulled up and in toward her neck, upper arms pressed against her sides, and both hands closed together in front of her chest. Her hands hold nothing, so the STYLE's instructions about weapons and role props do not apply here. Two feathered wings grow from her back, FOLDED CLOSED and pulled in tight against her back so they follow the line of her body and stay narrow. Wings in muted slate-grey and steel-blue with off-white tips, the same plumage as the birdman. Shoulder-length windswept hair and nothing else on the head. She wears one plain garment only: a simple sleeveless tunic of undyed off-white linen, gathered at the waist with a thin cord, with bare arms, bare shins and bare feet. She STANDS on the ground with both bare feet planted flat on it, a small soft oval shadow directly beneath her feet. Keep the mature, muted, limited palette.
