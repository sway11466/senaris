SUBJECT: A dwarf warrior — a miner turned axe-fighter who fights alongside the steel-blue army, the toughest wall in the line and a tireless walker of mountains. A dwarf: clearly SHORT and very WIDE — barrel-chested and thick as a keg, shoulders nearly as wide as he is tall, huge blocky hands, short stumpy legs and heavy boots. He is stout and heavy, NOT small and slim (he must never read as a halfling or a child). A long, thick, full beard spreads across his chest — plain and loose, hanging in one broad mass with a blunt squared-off end: NO braids, NO plaits, NO forked or split beard, NO beard rings, clasps or beads of any kind. Bushy brows and a broad nose above it; a plain riveted iron half-helm sits low on his head. Muted steel-blue army palette on a miner's kit: a heavy blue-grey padded coat under scarred iron plates on chest and shoulders, dark brown leather straps, a wide belt with a big square iron buckle, thick leather gauntlets. Working gear marks him as a man from the mines: a short miner's pick hooked at the belt and a small dented lantern hanging at his hip. He grips a broad single-bit battle axe upright in front of him with both hands, its head chunky, wide and oversized, the haft planted between his boots. NO shield of any kind (the great shield belongs to the knight). Stoic, gruff and unshakable, with a hint of dry good humour. Keep the mature, muted, limited palette.
