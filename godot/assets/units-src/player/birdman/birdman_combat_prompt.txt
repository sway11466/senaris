POSE (drift): A floating attack pose — the body hovers clear of the ground with no weight on any foot, drifting toward the enemy on the right with both hands (or the weapon) reaching ahead, the trailing hem or tail streaming out behind. For flying units.

SUBJECT: The SAME birdman as the birdman board piece — a winged cliff-dweller who fights alongside the steel-blue army (same face, same wings, same tunic, same club; this must read as the exact same character). He looks like a MAN WITH WINGS, not like a bird: an ordinary human face and head (human eyes, nose and mouth — absolutely NO beak, NO bird head, NO feathered face), human arms and hands, human legs and bare human feet. What makes him a birdman is only this: two large feathered wings growing from his back, sweeping back and open wide as he dives forward. Everything above the neck is plainly human: short windswept hair and nothing else — NO feathers stuck in the hair, NO feather crest, NO plume, NO headband or headdress of any kind. Wings in muted slate-grey and steel-blue with off-white and dark banded tips; a sleeveless muted steel-blue tunic, a blue sash at the waist, wrapped forearms, bare arms and shins. Nothing crosses the chest or shoulders — NO harness, NO baldric, NO shoulder strap, NO bandolier, NO belt running diagonally across the body; the chest stays clear. He swings a SMALL wooden club at the enemy on the right — a plain short cudgel of dark hardwood, slightly wider and knotted at the striking end, grip bound with cord, whipped around one-handed in a quick snapping blow while the free hand reaches ahead for balance. Keep the club SMALL and light: about the length of his forearm and no longer than from his chin to his waist, no thicker at the head than his own wrist. It must NOT be a huge two-handed maul, NOT a giant tree-trunk bludgeon, NOT taller than he is, NOT a heavy weapon — he is a light, quick flyer and the club should look easy to swing in the air. NO spear, NO blade, NO axe, NO claws. He hovers: no foot touches anything, the legs trailing behind him, the sash and hair streaming back. Fierce, watchful and noble — a free highland hunter, not a monster: clean, upright and dignified, NOT ragged, NOT bestial, NOT hunched, no tattered feathers. Note that the STYLE line about both feet planted does not apply — he is airborne. Keep the mature, muted, limited palette.
