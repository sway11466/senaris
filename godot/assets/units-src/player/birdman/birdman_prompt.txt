SUBJECT: A birdman — a winged cliff-dweller who fights alongside the steel-blue army, light enough to cross any cliff and one of the few who can reach an enemy in the air. He looks like a MAN WITH WINGS, not like a bird: an ordinary human face and head (human eyes, nose and mouth — absolutely NO beak, NO bird head, NO feathered face), human arms and hands, human legs and bare human feet. What makes him a birdman is only this: two large feathered wings growing from his back, spread WIDE and open so they fill out the silhouette. Everything above the neck is plainly human: short windswept hair and nothing else — NO feathers stuck in the hair, NO feather crest, NO plume, NO headband or headdress of any kind. Wings in muted slate-grey and steel-blue with off-white and dark banded tips. He wears light gear only, so nothing weighs down the wings: a sleeveless tunic of muted steel-blue, a blue sash at the waist, wrapped forearms, bare arms and shins. Nothing crosses the chest or shoulders — NO harness, NO baldric, NO shoulder strap, NO bandolier, NO belt running diagonally across the body; the chest stays clear. He holds a SMALL wooden club upright in front of him in one hand — a plain short cudgel of dark hardwood, slightly wider and knotted at the striking end, the grip bound with cord. Keep the club SMALL and light: about the length of his forearm and no longer than from his chin to his waist, no thicker at the head than his own wrist. It must NOT be a huge two-handed maul, NOT a giant tree-trunk bludgeon, NOT taller than he is, NOT a heavy weapon — he is a light, quick flyer and the club should look easy to carry in the air. NO spear, NO blade, NO axe, NO claws. He is AIRBORNE — the whole body floats clear of the ground with the legs hanging relaxed below him, and a small soft oval ground shadow is cast on the ground directly under him with a clear empty gap between his feet and that shadow; he is plainly hovering, NOT standing. Fierce, watchful and noble — a free highland hunter, not a monster: clean, upright and dignified, NOT ragged, NOT bestial, NOT hunched, no tattered feathers. Keep the mature, muted, limited palette.
