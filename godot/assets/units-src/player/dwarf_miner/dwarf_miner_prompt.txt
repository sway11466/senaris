SUBJECT: A dwarf miner — an ordinary mine worker, not a warrior, who has just fled up the tunnels from monsters. A dwarf: clearly SHORT and very WIDE — barrel-chested and thick as a keg, huge blocky hands, short stumpy legs and heavy boots; stout and heavy, NOT small and slim (he must never read as a halfling or a child). SAME chibi proportions, face-style family and muted palette as the game's other units. A reddish-brown beard of medium length, tied together near the end with a simple cord so it sits neatly on his chest; bushy brows and a broad nose. On his head, a small round leather miner's cap with a little brass oil lamp fixed on its front. He wears grimy, work-worn clothes: a faded steel-blue work shirt with the sleeves rolled up, sturdy brown trousers, a heavy brown leather apron smeared with dirt and dust, and scuffed work boots; smudges of soil on his face and hands. He holds a plain miner's pickaxe upright in front of him with both hands, gripping it tightly. His expression is startled and frightened — eyes wide and round, mouth open as if shouting a warning — but still the rounded, friendly face of the game, not grotesque. Keep the mature, muted, limited palette: dusty browns, faded steel-blue, brass, reddish-brown beard.
