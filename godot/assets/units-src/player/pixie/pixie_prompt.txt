SUBJECT: A pixie — the steel-blue army's tiny flying fairy, a featherweight scout who pins down ground enemies rather than killing them. CRITICAL: a big round oversized chibi head, about 2 to 2.5 heads tall total (NOT 3 heads), do NOT elongate the body. A very small young fairy girl with a short bob of pale flaxen hair and a single small leaf or flower tucked in it, a simple sleeveless tunic-dress of muted steel-blue cloth with off-white and pale sage-green accents, a thin blue sash, bare arms and legs, small bare feet. Two pairs of translucent insect wings (dragonfly-like, thin dark veins, faint blue-white sheen) spread WIDE and open from her back — the wings are the largest, boldest part of her silhouette. She carries one small wand: a short, slender rod like a conductor's baton, plain pale wood wrapped with a thin blue cord and capped with a tiny round bead at the tip, held upright in front of her chest with both hands, a small pinch of pale glowing dust gathered at the bead. The wand is SHORT — no longer than from her chin to her waist, clearly a little baton and NOT the wizard's long staff, and it must not be taller than she is. She is AIRBORNE — the whole body floats clear of the ground, legs relaxed with toes pointed down, and a small soft oval ground shadow is cast on the ground directly below her with a clear empty gap between her feet and that shadow; she is plainly hovering, NOT standing. EXPRESSION: bright, cheerful and fearless — a friendly closed-mouth smile with BOTH corners of the mouth lifted evenly, and a playful wink: one eye shut in a simple upward arc while the other stays wide, round and open. Both eyebrows stay relaxed, level and high on the forehead. She is simply cheerful and welcoming. Do NOT make her look smug, teasing, mocking or knowing: NO cocked or raised single eyebrow, NO half-lidded or narrowed open eye, NO lopsided smirk or one-sided grin, NO sideways glance, no sneer, no fangs, nothing wicked. The dust and wing edges may carry a gentle contained blue-white glow, but NO strong light, NO light beams, NO aura, NO scattered sparkle particles, NO glow bleeding into the background. Because this piece is drawn very small on the board, keep the read bold and simple: huge head, wide spread wings, floating gap under the feet. Keep the mature, muted, limited palette.
