SUBJECT: A wyrm — a serpent-dragon of the badlands, strong and fast enough that fleeing from it does not work. Body plan: one long, heavy, snake-like trunk with NO legs, NO arms, NO claws and NO hands — do NOT draw it as a lizard, a crocodile or a four-legged drake. The only limbs it has are WINGS: a pair of large ribbed membrane wings growing from the shoulders just behind the head, spread open on both sides so they fill out the silhouette, the membrane pale and translucent between dark spars, with a small clawed thumb at the leading edge. It is a DRAGON, not a mere snake, and that must read from the head and back: a broad blunt draconic skull with a heavy hinged jaw and rows of thick fangs, a pair of short backswept horns, a fringe of spiny whiskers at the jaw, and a ridged fin of dark webbing running from the skull down the spine to the tail tip. The trunk is python-thick and far bigger than the small rock serpent. It wears NOTHING — no saddle, no harness, no collar, no armour, no ornament. Chunky, stocky proportions with a big oversized head, like the game's other pieces. Seen head-on and symmetrical: the heavy body is stacked in a broad coil RESTING ON THE GROUND, the front of the trunk rears up out of the middle of that coil in an S-curve, and the head is held high facing the viewer with the jaws parted. This is a GROUND creature that hunts on the earth — the coil must sit solidly on the ground and touch its shadow, with no gap under it; it is NOT hovering and NOT airborne. Hide of bold overlapping scale plates in dark charcoal and slate with a deep bronze-olive sheen along the back and a paler ash belly. The eyes are dull red, the horns and fangs black. NO fire, NO smoke, NO crown of horns — it is the lesser cousin of the great red dragon and must never be mistaken for it. Fast and mean rather than grand. Keep the mature, muted, limited palette: charcoal, slate and bronze-olive, with red only in the eyes.

NOTE: the STYLE block's instructions about a person's head-to-body proportions, arms, hands, feet, "feet near the lower third" and held weapons do NOT apply to this piece — it is a limbless winged serpent. Keep only the STYLE's rendering, the straight-on symmetrical front view, and the centered composition with the coiled body sitting near the lower third of the frame and its small soft ground shadow.
