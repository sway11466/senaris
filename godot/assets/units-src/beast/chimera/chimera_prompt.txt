SUBJECT: A chimera — a big, heavy boss beast of the monster side, in the same sooty-and-ash coat and the same black claw-and-fang treatment as the griffon, a size larger and heavier than it. It is an ANIMAL, not a person: a heavy, deep-chested lion body on four short thick legs with wide black-clawed paws, with no hands. The body keeps the chunky, compact build of the game's other pieces, but the head is only somewhat larger than life — a predator's skull, not a mascot's head. It has a SINGLE lion head, in the centre, facing the camera straight on: a snarling face with the muzzle wrinkled up, the lips peeled back off black fangs and long canines, small narrowed bloodshot eyes under a heavy brow, the ears flattened; the mane is ragged, bristling and uneven, hanging in matted clumps. On the lion's forehead glows one small red alchemical sigil (the same simple circle-and-lines rune as the homunculus), the mark of the furnace that made it. Out of the middle of its back, directly above and behind the mane, bursts one goat head on a thick short neck, forced up through the lion's hide so that the skin around its base is stretched taut and the fur there is split open, showing the goat's pale hide underneath; the goat's face is gaunt, with a long grey beard, two curved black horns and blank, empty, staring eyes. Its tail is one snake as thick as the lion's leg, rising from the rump on the viewer's RIGHT side, scaled and muscular, reared up over the back with a flat viper's head, and biting down on one of the goat's horns. Each part keeps its own hide and they do not match: coarse matted lion fur, short dusty goat hair, dry scales, with the joins between them strained and wrong rather than neat. Coarse lion coat in sooty black shading to ash-grey and dusty brown, the mane darker charcoal; the goat head dirty ash-white with a grey beard; the snake in dark charcoal scales with a paler slate belly; horns, claws and fangs black; all the eyes dull red. It wears NOTHING — no collar, no chains, no saddle, no harness, no armour, no ornament of any kind. NO fire, NO smoke, NO glow from any mouth. Seen straight on and symmetrical, the body square to the viewer, standing squarely on the ground on all four paws, feet near the lower third, with a small soft oval ground shadow directly under it. Menacing and wrong — a thing that should not exist. Keep the mature, muted, limited palette: sooty blacks, ash-grey and dusty browns, with red only in the eyes and the forehead sigil.
