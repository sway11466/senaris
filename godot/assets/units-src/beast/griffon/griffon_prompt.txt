SUBJECT: A griffon — a wild predator beast of the monster side, a mid-weight flyer that stoops on its prey from above. It is an ANIMAL, not a person: an eagle's head, an eagle's broad feathered wings and eagle forelegs with talons, joined to a lion's hindquarters with heavy paws and a tufted tail. It walks on four legs; do NOT stand it upright on two legs like a man, and do NOT give it hands. Chunky, stocky proportions with a big oversized head and short thick limbs, the way the game's other pieces are drawn. Plumage on the head, chest and wings in sooty black shading to ash-grey and earth-brown, with a coarse dusty-brown lion coat behind; the hooked beak and every talon are black; the eyes are dull red under a heavy feathered brow; a few pale feather bars break up the wings. It wears NOTHING — no saddle, no harness, no collar, no reins, no armour, no ornament of any kind; it is a wild animal, not a mount. It is AIRBORNE — the whole body floats clear of the ground with the talons hanging beneath it and the wings spread WIDE and open on both sides so they fill out the silhouette, and a small soft oval ground shadow is cast on the ground directly under it with a clear empty gap between the talons and that shadow; it is plainly hovering, NOT standing on the ground. Fierce, alert and predatory, beak slightly parted. Keep the mature, muted, limited palette: sooty blacks, ash-grey and dusty browns, with red only in the eyes.
