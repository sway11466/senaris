POSE (drift): A floating attack pose — the body hovers clear of the ground with no weight on any foot, drifting toward the enemy on the left with both hands (or the weapon) reaching ahead, the trailing hem or tail streaming out behind. For flying units.

SUBJECT: The SAME harpy as the harpy board piece — a shrieking bird-woman of the monster side (same gaunt face, same feather mane, same sooty plumage, same black talons; this must read as the exact same creature). She is a MONSTER, not a person with wings: her ARMS ARE WINGS — long ragged feathered wings grow straight out of her shoulders where arms would be, ending in feather tips, so she has NO hands and NO fingers anywhere. From the knee DOWN her legs are a bird's: thin scaly shanks with heavy black talons. Everything else is a young woman's: head, neck, shoulders, a human torso with a normal waist and belly, and human thighs, in smooth warm-tan human skin — so that her human face sits on a human body, NOT on a bird's feathered barrel. Chunky chibi proportions with a big oversized head and a small slender body. Her face is an ordinary young woman's face, drawn in exactly the same friendly chibi style as the game's other unit pieces: a rounded face with large clear eyes in warm amber-brown and a small nose. Keep the face plain and unglamorous but NOT frightening — NO gaunt hollow cheeks, NO sunken or glowing eyes, NO heavy scowling brow, NO hooked witch's nose, NO bared fangs or pointed teeth, NO snarl or grimace, nothing ghoulish or corpse-like. Instead of hair she has a mane of pale straw-blonde feathers swept back off her head. Her feathers grow as her clothing: a thick feathered collar and mantle covering the chest and shoulders, and a short feathered skirt around the hips and upper thighs, with the human midriff and thighs bare between them. She must be decently covered by that plumage and NOT sexualised at all — chibi proportions, a flat childlike build, no cleavage, no exposed chest, no exposed hips, nothing suggestive. Feather colour: deep muted rust-red along the long wing feathers, warming to brick and dusty rose toward the tips, with paler sand-tan on the collar and skirt. She is a WARM-coloured bird, not a black one: NO sooty black plumage, NO crow or raven colouring. Apart from her own plumage she wears NOTHING and carries NO weapon and NO tool. She is stooping on her prey: the body hurtles toward the enemy on the left with both taloned feet swung forward at full reach and the claws spread to rake, the head thrust out and the mouth open in a short cry (lips parted only — no bared fangs, no gaping maw), the wings swept back and up behind her. No foot touches anything — she is entirely airborne. Keep the whole creature compact within the frame: wings swept BACK along the body rather than stretched out flat to the sides, so the piece is taller than it is wide. Wild and alert, like a wary wild bird. She reads as a monster because of the wings-for-arms and the taloned bird legs — NOT because of her face or body, which stay human and ordinary. Not seductive and not a cute fairy, but not scary-looking either. Keep the mature, muted, limited palette: muted rust-red and brick feathers over warm sand-tan, with darker rust in the shadows and black only in the talons. Rich and warm, but still desaturated and flat cel-shaded — NOT a bright hot-pink cartoon bird, NOT glossy, NOT painterly or photorealistic.
