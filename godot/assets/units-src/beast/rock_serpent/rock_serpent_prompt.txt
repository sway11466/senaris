SUBJECT: A rock serpent — a venomous snake that lives in the crevices of the badlands and comes at intruders in packs. It is an ANIMAL with NO limbs at all: one thick-bodied serpent, NO arms, NO legs, NO wings, NO hands, NO clothing, NO collar, NO ornament, NO weapon of any kind. It is small and unimpressive — the weakest of the beasts, dangerous only because there are many of them. Chunky, stocky proportions with a big oversized head on a short thick body, like the game's other pieces. Seen head-on and symmetrical: the body is piled in a low coil resting on the ground, and the neck rises out of the middle of that coil so the head is held up facing the viewer, jaws slightly parted to show two long curved fangs. A low ridge of blunt, rocky scutes runs down its spine and over the brow like a crest of chipped stone. Hide in dark stone-grey and sooty charcoal with dusty brown banding along the back, a paler ash belly, and the scales drawn as bold simple plates rather than fine detail. The eyes are dull red slits and the fangs are black at the base, whitening at the tips; a hint of dark venom beads at one fang. Keep it dangerous but plain — NOT a giant dragon, NOT hooded like a cobra, NO horns, NO frills, NO glowing markings. Keep the mature, muted, limited palette: stone greys, charcoal and dusty browns, with red only in the eyes.

NOTE: the STYLE block's instructions about arms, legs, feet, "feet near the lower third" and held weapons do NOT apply to this piece — it is a limbless snake. Keep only the STYLE's rendering, the straight-on symmetrical front view, and the centered composition with the coiled body sitting near the lower third of the frame and its small soft ground shadow.
