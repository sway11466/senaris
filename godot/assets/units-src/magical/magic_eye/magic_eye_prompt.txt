SUBJECT: A magic eye — an artificial creature grown in the same alchemist's flasks as the homunculus, a failed creation loosed from the ancient ruins that floats in the air and shoots magic rays from afar. It is a MAGICAL CREATURE: one big round floating eyeball with tentacles hanging beneath it. Seen head-on and facing the viewer: the ball is covered in the SAME skin treatment as the homunculus — completely hairless, smooth, pale ashen-white skin with a faint grey-lavender cast, smooth and simplified like a porcelain doll. On the front of the ball, a pair of heavy skin eyelids open onto ONE huge eye that fills most of the front: a pale grey eye-white, a large round iris glowing red with a dark pupil in its centre — the only red on it; the gaze is calm and steady, not angry. It has NO mouth, NO teeth, NO other eyes, NO sigil and NO ornaments. From the underside of the ball hang four or five thick, smooth tentacles of the same ashen-white skin, tapering to points and curling gently, roughly mirrored left and right. Like the homunculus's kin it is ASSEMBLED FROM PARTS: thin dark seam lines run around the base of each tentacle where it joins the ball. It is AIRBORNE — the whole creature floats clear of the ground, and a small soft oval ground shadow is cast on the ground directly under it with a clear empty gap between the tentacle tips and that shadow; it is plainly hovering, NOT resting on the ground. Keep it about the size of a goblin grunt. Keep the mature, muted, limited palette: ashen white, pale grey-lavender shadow, dark grey outline and seams, the red iris.
