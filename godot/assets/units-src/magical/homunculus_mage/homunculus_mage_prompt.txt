SUBJECT: A homunculus mage — the SAME homunculus as the homunculus board piece (same small smooth doll-like body, same pale ashen-white skin with a faint grey-lavender cast, same bald head, same small red alchemical sigil glowing on the forehead, same large solid glossy black eyes with a single white catchlight; this must read as the exact same kind of creature), but this one is casting a spell. It is a MAGICAL CREATURE in the shape of a small person, not a human child, with NO clothing, NO armour, NO weapon and NO tools or ornaments; the body is smooth and simplified like a porcelain doll, with no navel or other anatomical detail. Both short arms are raised up above its head, symmetrically, with the open palms turned upward and the empty hands spread a little apart, as if chanting an incantation; its small mouth is slightly open mid-chant, and its expression stays blank and quiet. The hands are empty and the spell itself is not drawn. Keep it small — about the size of a goblin grunt. Keep the mature, muted, limited palette: ashen white, pale grey-lavender shadow, dark grey outline, a small spot of red.
