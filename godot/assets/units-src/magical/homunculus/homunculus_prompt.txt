SUBJECT: A homunculus — a small artificial being grown in an alchemist's flask, a failed creation spilling out of ancient ruins. It is a MAGICAL CREATURE in the shape of a small person, not a human child: a small, smooth, doll-like body with pale ashen-white skin with a faint grey-lavender cast, completely bald, with NO clothing, NO armour, NO weapon and NO tools or ornaments — the body is smooth and simplified like a porcelain doll, with no navel or other anatomical detail. On its forehead glows one small red alchemical sigil (a simple circle-and-lines rune) — the only red on it. The face is the calm, rounded face of the game's enemies, but its eyes are large, solid glossy black with a single white catchlight, and its expression is blank and quiet, which makes it read as not quite human. Keep it small — about the size of a goblin grunt. It stands with its short arms hanging loosely at its sides, empty-handed. Keep the mature, muted, limited palette: ashen white, pale grey-lavender shadow, dark grey outline, a small spot of red.
