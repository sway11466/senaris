SUBJECT: A slime — a lump of living ooze that collects in the damp hollows of a cave and dissolves whatever it settles on. It is a MAGICAL CREATURE made of thick ooze, not an animal and not a person: ONE heavy, sagging mass of gel that has slumped where it sits, wider at the bottom than the top. Make it deliberately UNEVEN and asymmetrical — one shoulder of the mass higher and fatter than the other, the lower edge drooping in slack folds with two or three thick strands of ooze hanging and pooling on the ground. It has NO arms, NO legs, NO hands, NO feet, NO fur, NO scales, NO shell, NO clothing, NO armour, NO weapon and NO tools or ornaments. Keep it small and low — the smallest piece on the board — but heavy and slack rather than bouncy: this is NOT a cute jelly, NOT a candy, NOT a mascot, NOT a rounded rice-ball shape, NOT a smooth symmetrical dome. It is drawn SOLID, not see-through, in FLAT cel-shading with hard-edged tone steps — NO soft airbrushed gradients, NO glass, NO glow, NO transparency. The gloss is only two or three short, hard-edged streaks of pale highlight on the upper surface; NO single large soft oval highlight. Colours: a murky, muddied moss green fouled with grey-brown, darker and browner toward the base where it pools, with a dark olive rim; the highlights are a dull off-white. The ooze is turbid, not clear: suspended in it sit a few grains of grit, a small pale sliver of bone and one or two half-dissolved scraps, plus one or two denser dark-green balls — the buds it splits off when it multiplies. It has NO face and NO mouth: the only features are two dark, unevenly placed nuclei drifting in the upper half of the mass, which read as eyes without any expression. NO eyebrows, NO smile, NO fangs, NO tongue, NO blush. This piece is not humanoid, so the style's head-count proportions, face, hands, feet and weapon instructions do not apply to it. Keep the mature, muted, limited palette: murky moss green, grey-brown silt, dark olive rim, dull off-white gloss.
