POSE (engulf): A smothering lunge, seen from the side — the slime hunches its mass back and then surges toward the enemy on the LEFT, the whole body stretching out and rearing up into a breaking wave that leans over the target, its leading edge thinning into a broad overhanging lip about to fall on them, while the trailing half is still dragged along the ground behind it. It swings nothing and holds nothing: the attack is its own body falling forward. Keep the bottom of the mass firmly in contact with the ground along its whole length (it never floats or hops).

SUBJECT: The SAME slime as the slime board piece — a lump of living ooze from the damp hollows of a cave that dissolves whatever it settles on (same murky moss-green ooze fouled with grey-brown, same hard-edged gloss streaks, same suspended grit and buds, same two dark nuclei and no face; this must read as the exact same creature). It is a MAGICAL CREATURE made of jelly, not an animal and not a person: ONE mass of gel with NO arms, NO legs, NO hands, NO feet, NO fur, NO scales, NO shell, NO clothing, NO armour, NO weapon and NO tools or ornaments of any kind. Keep it small and low — the smallest piece on the field, even while it rears — and heavy and slack rather than bouncy: this is NOT a cute jelly, NOT a candy, NOT a mascot, NOT a smooth symmetrical dome. Make the mass deliberately UNEVEN, with slack folds and two or three thick strands of ooze hanging from the underside and pooling on the ground. It is drawn SOLID, not see-through, in FLAT cel-shading with hard-edged tone steps — NO soft airbrushed gradients, NO glass, NO glow, NO transparency. The gloss is only two or three short, hard-edged streaks of pale highlight on the upper surface; NO single large soft oval highlight. Colours: a murky, muddied moss green fouled with grey-brown, darker and browner toward the base where it pools, with a dark olive rim; the highlights are a dull off-white. The ooze is turbid: suspended in it sit a few grains of grit, a small pale sliver of bone and one or two half-dissolved scraps, plus one or two denser dark-green balls — the buds it splits off when it multiplies. It has NO face and NO mouth: the only features are two dark, unevenly placed nuclei drifting in the upper half of the mass, which read as eyes without any expression. NO eyebrows, NO smile, NO fangs, NO tongue, NO blush. This piece is not humanoid, so the style's head-count proportions, hands, feet, weapon and "both feet visible" instructions do not apply to it; the whole underside is its foot. Keep the mature, muted, limited palette: mossy blue-green gel, dark teal outline, pale cool highlights.
