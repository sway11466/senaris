SUBJECT: A skeleton archer — the ranged skirmisher of the undead horde. A walking human skeleton like the skeleton foot soldier, but noticeably lighter and finer-boned: a slender frame, narrow shoulders, quick-looking limbs. SAME bleached bone white-grey bones and SAME bare skull with empty eye sockets lit by a COLD BLUE-GREEN glow as the skeleton (the sign that the corpse moves) — it must read as the same kind of creature, a lighter cousin of the skeleton. Its gear is what it wore in life, now rotted: cracked dark leather straps, a tattered scrap of cloth at the waist, a few tarnished and rusted metal fittings — this is once-proper equipment left to decay, NOT scavenged junk. Grave-earth stains on the bones. It holds a short bow made of pale bone upright and ready in front of the body, with a quiver of cursed arrows slung on its back — the arrowheads faintly touched with the same cold blue-green. It carries NO blade of any kind. IMPORTANT — the body is square to the camera and completely frontal: skull, shoulders, hips and both feet all face the viewer straight on, weight even on both legs, planted and still. Light, quick and quietly menacing — not comical, not cute. Keep the mature, muted, limited palette: bone white-grey, rot brown, rusted metal, with the cold blue-green glow ONLY in the eye sockets and the arrowheads (small and contained). NOT bright cartoon colors.
