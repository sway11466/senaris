SUBJECT: A ghost — the flying spirit of the undead horde. It is an EMPTY BURIAL SHROUD that has stood up and drifted off the grave: one single continuous piece of hanging cloth with NOTHING inside it, not a figure wearing a sheet. Its whole shape is that drape — a rounded dome at the top where a head would be, the cloth falling and widening below it, ending in a torn, wavy, scalloped hem that lifts and curls. It has NO arms, NO hands, NO shoulders, NO neck, NO legs and NO feet, and carries NO weapon and NO object of any kind; nothing breaks the outline of the cloth. Its face is only what the cloth has torn open: TWO dark ragged eye-holes, narrow and tall and not quite matching, each with a small COLD BLUE-GREEN glow burning deep inside it — the SAME cold blue-green glow as the skeleton's eye sockets (the sign that the corpse moves). Below them a third, smaller dark tear where a mouth would be, ragged and lopsided. CRITICAL — these openings must NOT be cute: NO big round friendly eyes, NO shiny highlights or catchlights in them, NO smile, NO open cartoon "o" mouth, NO eyebrows, NO blush, NO cheerful expression of any kind. They are holes in cloth with something cold looking out. The cloth is grave cloth, not a clean bedsheet: a dull pale grey-green, dirt and rot staining it browner toward the bottom, small tears and loose frayed threads along the hem, hanging in a few heavy soft folds. IMPORTANT — IT FLOATS: the whole shroud hovers clearly ABOVE the ground with an obvious gap of empty air under the trailing hem, and a small soft oval shadow is cast on the ground below it. Reading as airborne at a glance is essential. IMPORTANT — the shroud is square to the camera and completely frontal, symmetrical, not turned or leaning to either side; the tears and the hem may be irregular, but the overall form is not. Faintly see-through at the very edge of the hem, but keep the body of the cloth solid enough that the silhouette reads clearly at a small size; do NOT make it so transparent that the shape disappears. Sorrowful, cold and quietly unsettling — a dead thing, not a mascot; not comical, not cute, not a Halloween decoration. Keep the mature, muted, limited palette: dull pale grey-green cloth, rot brown at the stained torn hem, with the cold blue-green glow ONLY inside the eye-holes (small and contained). NOT bright cartoon colors, NOT pure clean white.

NOTE — this piece is not a person: it has no limbs, no visible body and no face of its own, so the style block's instructions about head-to-body proportions, expressive eyes, a calm confident personality, feet on the ground and weapon props do NOT apply here. Draw only the hanging cloth.
