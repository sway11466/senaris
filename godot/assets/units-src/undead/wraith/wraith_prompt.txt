SUBJECT: A wraith — the greater spirit of the undead horde, the ghost's stronger elder. It is plainly the SAME kind of hanging grave cloth as the ghost board piece, but grown darker, heavier, larger and armed. Where the ghost is an empty shroud with nothing but torn holes for a face and no arms at all, the wraith's shroud has pulled itself into a shape: it has gathered at the top into a DEEP POINTED HOOD, and it has put out arms. Under the hood there is NO face at all — only black emptiness with TWO small COLD BLUE-GREEN eye-lights burning in it, the SAME cold blue-green glow as the skeleton's eye sockets (the sign that the corpse moves). This faceless hood is the single most important read of the design. The cloth is a heavy burial shroud and mantle, once-proper grave cloth now rotted, far darker and thicker than the ghost's — deep charcoal grey shot with cold grey-green, the hem torn into long ragged streamers. It has NO legs and NO feet: below the waist the body frays away into those tattered streamers and a thin curl of mist. Its hands are bare bone — the SAME bleached skeletal hands as the necromancer — and BOTH of them grip a rusted reaping scythe held straight upright in front of the body, the shaft vertical and centred, the curved blade sweeping up from its top; the blade is pitted, notched and blackened with rust, once a proper tool left to decay. Keep the scythe thick and bold so it still reads at a tiny size, and keep it upright and centred so the piece stays perfectly symmetrical — do NOT tilt it or swing it to either side. IMPORTANT — IT FLOATS: the whole figure hovers clearly ABOVE the ground with an obvious gap of empty air under the trailing streamers, and a small soft oval shadow is cast on the ground below it. Reading as airborne at a glance is essential. IMPORTANT — the body is square to the camera and completely frontal: hood, shoulders and hips all face the viewer straight on, not turned or leaning to either side. Faintly see-through at the frayed hem and the mist, but keep the body and the scythe solid enough that the silhouette still reads clearly at a small size; do NOT make it so transparent that the shape disappears. Cold, deliberate and menacing rather than sorrowful — the ghost grieves, this one hunts. Not comical, not cute. Keep the mature, muted, limited palette: deep charcoal grey-green shroud, rot brown at the torn hem, black-rusted iron on the blade, bleached bone hands, with the cold blue-green glow ONLY in the two eye-lights (small and contained). NOT bright cartoon colors.
