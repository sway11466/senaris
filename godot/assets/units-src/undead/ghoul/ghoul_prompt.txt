SUBJECT: A ghoul — the predator of the undead horde, the one that runs down the living. A gaunt corpse-eater: ash-grey desiccated flesh stretched tight over bone so the ribs and joints show through, a bald skull-like head, and a mouth split far too wide with a row of needle teeth. Its sunken eyes are lit by a COLD BLUE-GREEN glow — the SAME cold blue-green glow as the skeleton's eye sockets (the sign that the corpse moves). It wears almost nothing: a few cracked dark leather straps and a shred of rotted cloth at the waist, tarnished rusted fittings — once-proper equipment left to decay, NOT scavenged junk. Grave-earth stains on the skin. It carries NO weapon at all: it hunts with long hooked claws, and BOTH clawed hands are raised in front of the chest, mirrored left and right at the same height with the same bend — perfectly symmetrical. IMPORTANT — the body is square to the camera and completely frontal: head, shoulders, hips and both feet all face the viewer straight on, weight even on both legs, planted and still. This is NOT a three-quarter view, NOT a side view, NOT a running or lunging pose, and the body does NOT lean or turn to either side. It crouches low in a coiled, beast-like way, but the crouch is vertical only — the torso stays square to the camera. Starved, fast and vicious — not comical, not cute. Keep the mature, muted, limited palette: ash-grey flesh, rot brown, tarnished metal, with the cold blue-green glow ONLY in the eyes (small and contained). NOT bright cartoon colors.
