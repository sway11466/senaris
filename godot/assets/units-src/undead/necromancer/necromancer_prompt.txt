SUBJECT: A necromancer — the master of the undead horde and the boss of this campaign, the one whose will keeps every other corpse walking. A gaunt figure swallowed by a deep hood: the face inside is lost in shadow except for two points of COLD BLUE-GREEN light where the eyes would be — the SAME cold blue-green glow as the skeleton's eye sockets (the sign that the corpse moves). It wears long robes that were once fine and are now rotted: frayed hems, grave-earth stains, a heavy tarnished clasp at the throat, a few rusted fittings — this is once-proper vestment left to decay, NOT scavenged junk. Its hands are bare bone, the SAME bleached bone white-grey as the skeleton, showing at the cuffs. It holds a tall staff of knotted bone upright in front of the body, topped with a dull orb that holds a contained blue-green light inside it (a soft inner glow only — NO rays, NO aura, NO particles). IMPORTANT — the body is square to the camera and completely frontal: hood, shoulders and hips all face the viewer straight on, weight even on both legs, planted and still. Still, patient and commanding — the mind behind the horde, not comical, not cute. Keep the mature, muted, limited palette: deep faded robe cloth, bone white-grey, rot brown, tarnished metal, with the cold blue-green glow ONLY in the hood's eyes and inside the orb (small and contained). NOT bright cartoon colors.
