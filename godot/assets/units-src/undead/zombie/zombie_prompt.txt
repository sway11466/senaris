SUBJECT: A zombie — the shambling wall of the undead horde, the unit that soaks up blows while the rest of the horde closes in. A bloated, rotting human corpse: swollen greenish-grey flesh mottled with dark rot and grave-earth stains, the skin split open in a place or two to show bone underneath. Its face is slack and vacant, the eyes lit by a COLD BLUE-GREEN glow — the SAME cold blue-green glow as the skeleton's eye sockets (the sign that the corpse moves). It wears what it wore in life, now rotted to rags: a filthy torn tunic, cracked dark leather straps, a few tarnished and rusted metal fittings — this is once-proper clothing left to decay, NOT scavenged junk. It carries NO weapon at all: both swollen arms are raised straight out in front of the body, symmetrical and level, reaching for the living. It stands hunched slightly forward, heavy and slow, as if about to drag itself another step. Thick and hard to put down but clumsy — menacing in a dull, mindless way, not comical, not cute. Keep the mature, muted, limited palette: rot green-grey flesh, filthy brown rags, tarnished metal, with the cold blue-green glow ONLY in the eyes (small and contained). NOT bright cartoon colors.
