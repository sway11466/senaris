SUBJECT: A dullahan — the champion of the undead horde and the heaviest, most imposing silhouette in the faction. A HEADLESS heavy knight in full plate: broad pauldrons, a thick breastplate, heavy gauntlets and greaves — armour that was once proper knightly plate, now blackened and eaten through with rust, dented and grave-stained. Over it hangs the remains of a dark surcoat and cloak, torn to tatters at the hem. There is NO head and NO helmet above the collar: the empty neck is filled with a COLD BLUE-GREEN glow welling up out of the gorget — the SAME cold blue-green glow as the skeleton's eye sockets (the sign that the corpse moves). This missing head is the single most important read of the design. It holds a huge two-handed greatsword upright in front of the body, point up, the blade pitted and rusted. IMPORTANT — the body is square to the camera and completely frontal: shoulders, hips and both feet all face the viewer straight on, weight even on both legs, planted and still. Massive, silent and menacing — the widest and heaviest shape of the horde, not comical, not cute. Keep the mature, muted, limited palette: black-rusted steel, rot brown, deep faded cloth, with the cold blue-green glow ONLY at the open neck (small and contained). NOT bright cartoon colors.
