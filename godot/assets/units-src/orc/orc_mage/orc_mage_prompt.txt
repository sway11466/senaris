SUBJECT: An orc shaman — the horde's curse-worker, who hangs back behind the brutes and throws witchfire. He is the SAME kind of creature as the orc grunt, but the runt of the pack: same thick ash-grey hide with a brown-earth cast (definitely NOT green skin — green belongs to the goblins), same jutting jaw with two tusks pushing up from the lower lip, same flat broad nose, same pointed ears, same small deep-set dull-red eyes — but wiry and gaunt instead of hulking, hunched low with a bent back, long spindly arms, knobbly hands and a scrawny neck; balding, with lank black hair hanging in strings. His gear is superstition, not armour: a moth-eaten hide cloak over bare bony shoulders, strings of small bones, teeth and dark feathers hung around his neck, a rough hide wrap at the waist, crude red-ochre marks daubed on his face and chest, one small animal skull tied at his hip. He carries NO staff, NO wand and NO pointed hat — those belong to the enemy's human mages. Instead he holds a short crooked fetish of bound bone and hide upright in front of him with both hands, and a small crimson flame floats just above it, cupped between his palms. That flame is SMALL and contained — a soft, closed crimson-orange glow only, with NO strong light, NO light beams, NO aura, NO sparks or flying embers, NO glow bleeding into the background. NO bow (the bow belongs to the goblins), NO blade. Sly, muttering and malicious. Keep the mature, muted, limited palette: sooty blacks, ash-grey hide and dark hides, with the crimson confined to the little flame and the ochre marks.
