SUBJECT: An orc grunt — the rank-and-file brute of the monster horde, the enemy footsoldier that comes in numbers. A hulking, misshapen brute, NOT a tidy human soldier: thick ash-grey hide with a brown-earth cast (definitely NOT green skin — green belongs to the goblins), a heavy hunched build with a bull neck, sloping shoulders, long thick arms and short bowed legs. A blunt heavy jaw juts forward with two tusks pushing up from the lower lip, a flat broad nose, small deep-set eyes glowing dull red under a heavy brow, and pointed ears; coarse black hair tied back in a rough knot. Crude scavenged war-gear in blackened iron and dark leather: mismatched iron plates lashed over the chest with rope, a studded belt, ragged dark cloth at the waist, heavy wrappings on the shins, and one deep-crimson rag tied at the arm as the horde's colour. He grips a big, heavy chopping blade upright in front of him with both hands — a crude wide cleaver of pitted dark iron with a nicked edge and a wrapped haft, chunky and oversized. NO bow (the bow belongs to the goblins), NO magic. Brutish, mean and eager for a fight, but not comical. Keep the mature, muted, limited palette: sooty blacks, ash-grey hide and dark iron, with crimson only as a small accent.
