SUBJECT: One DRAGON'S NEST on a volcano — a ring of great boulders piled
around a hoard of gold and silver treasure, with one large gemstone crowning the
hoard. Drawn as ONE object standing alone on a flat pure white background
(#FFFFFF) — no ground, no floor, no lava, no cast shadow on the ground, no other
objects.

VIEWING ANGLE — seen from the front and moderately above, about 35 degrees down
from the horizon: high enough that the inside of the ring and the hoard in it
are clearly seen, low enough that the boulders stand up as a wall around it.

SQUARE TO THE VIEWER — the nest is set straight in front of the viewer, not
turned, and its left and right halves are roughly mirror images of each other.
This is NOT an isometric view and NOT a three-quarter view.

DESIGN (follow exactly, every count and position):
- One ring of piled boulders around a hoard. NOT a bird's nest: NO branches, NO
  twigs, NO straw, NO sticks, NO woven material. NO building, NO wall of cut
  stone, NO pillars, NO arch, NO steps, NO door.
- THE RING: a closed oval ring of about ten large, rough, rounded boulders,
  heaped two high in places, seen as an oval about twice as wide as it is deep.
  The boulders at the BACK of the ring are the tallest; the boulders at the
  FRONT are low, no higher than a third of the back ones, so the hoard behind
  them is not hidden. The boulders touch each other; there is no gap or opening
  in the ring.
- THE HOARD: one broad, low, rounded heap of treasure filling the middle of the
  ring, its top standing a little above the front boulders. It is mostly gold
  coins with silver coins mixed in, and a few larger pieces half-buried in it:
  one gold goblet, one silver plate standing on its edge, and one small open
  chest spilling coins — each piece large and simple enough to read at
  thumbnail size. Nothing spills out over the boulders.
- THE GEMSTONE: one large faceted gemstone set upright on the very top of the
  hoard, in the centre, facing the viewer square-on. It is an oval cut, about
  one fifth of the ring's width across and a quarter taller than it is wide,
  with a few large flat facets. It is the only gemstone in the picture — no
  other jewels, no gems in the goblet or the chest. Its colour is ONE UNIFORM
  COLOUR — the game repaints it to show who holds the nest — so no pattern, no
  inner picture, no sparkle star; its facets differ only by flat light and
  shade of that one colour.
- COLOURS: the boulders are a cool, ash grey rock, base #6F6A66, each boulder a
  slightly different flat tone between #807B76 and #55514E. The gold is a
  muted, warm old gold, base #B8913A, shade #7E6128, light #D4B060. The silver
  is a muted grey silver, base #9A9C9E, shade #6C6E70, light #B8BABB. The
  gemstone is a colourless, greyish, unlit stone of hex #8F9498, shade
  #6C7175, light #A9AEB1 — clear but dull, NOT white, NOT glowing.
- THE GROUND IT WILL SIT ON: the game draws its own cooled lava field behind
  this picture — a very dark warm grey-brown crust, hex #3A322D, with glowing
  orange cracks, hex #E0702A. The boulders must read clearly against it by being
  LIGHTER and COOLER — do NOT make them dark, black, brown, reddish or
  charred, and do NOT paint any orange, red or glowing cracks on them.

SIZE — the object sits in the MIDDLE of the picture. In a 1024-pixel picture the
ring is about 700 pixels wide, and the whole nest from the front boulders to the
top of the back boulders or the gemstone, whichever is higher, is about 520
pixels tall, leaving at least 140 pixels of white on every side. It must NOT
touch any edge and must NOT be cut by any edge.

LIGHT — dim light from above, short soft shadows that fall only on the nest
itself. NOTHING casts a shadow onto the background. NO glow, NO shine, NO
sparkle, NO light of any colour anywhere in this picture; the gold and the
gemstone do not light up.

THE BACKGROUND — everything around the nest is PURE FLAT WHITE, #FFFFFF, with
NO texture, NO shading, NO gradient, NO ground, NO lava, NO drop shadow. This
white is going to be deleted and the game will draw its own ground there, so the
outline of the nest must be a hard, clean, crisp edge against it. Nothing in the
nest itself may be white or near-white.

Everything muted, in the same clean stylized cel-shaded look as the game's
terrain and unit art: bold flat shading, clear dark outlines, a mature, slightly
muted, limited color palette (NOT bright saturated, NOT painterly photorealism).

NOTHING ELSE — no dragon, no eggs, no people, no creature, no bones, no skull,
no scales, no claw marks, no weapons, no armour, no crown, no sword, no scroll,
no candle, no torch, no fire, no smoke, no steam, no lava, no embers, no
crystals growing from the rock, no plants, no moss, no banner, no flag, no
lettering, NO magic, NO particles. Read at thumbnail size: a few large legible
shapes, no fine detail. NO border, NO frame around the picture itself, NO
vignette. Square 1:1.
