STYLE: A map tile object for a fantasy tactics game, in the same clean stylized
cel-shaded look as the game's terrain and unit art: bold flat shading, clear
dark outlines, a mature, slightly muted, limited color palette (NOT bright
saturated, NOT painterly photorealism). Drawn like the world map of a classic 2D
role-playing game, seen from DIRECTLY OVERHEAD — a flat lay with NO perspective,
NO isometric or three-quarter view, no horizon, no sky, no depth, nothing
standing up and nothing leaning towards the viewer. Read at thumbnail size: a
few large legible shapes, no fine detail. Square 1:1.

SUBJECT: ONE rectangular raised vegetable bed — a plot of garden soil boxed in
by low wooden planks — seen from straight above, sitting alone on a plain white
background so that it can be cut out and laid over the game's own ground.

SIZE AND PLACEMENT — the bed is a single landscape rectangle in the MIDDLE of
the picture. In a 1024-pixel picture it is about 660 pixels wide and 500 pixels
tall, leaving about 180 pixels of white to its left and right and about 260
pixels of white above and below. It must NOT touch any edge and must NOT be cut
by any edge. There is exactly ONE bed: no second plot, no row of plots, no path,
no plants outside the frame of the bed.

THE FRAME — four straight wooden boards laid on edge around the plot, seen from
directly above, so the frame reads as a rectangular band about 40 pixels thick
in a 1024-pixel picture. Weathered mid-brown timber, hex #7A5C3C, with the grain
running along each board. Square corners, straight sides. NO posts sticking up,
NO fence, NO trellis, NO gate, NO label or sign.

INSIDE THE BED — dark turned soil, hex #4E3B2A, with THREE or FOUR low ridges
running the long way across the plot, evenly spaced and parallel. Along the crest
of each ridge sits a row of small leafy vegetables — cabbages, onion tops,
carrot tops, bean leaves — one kind per ridge and a different kind from the
ridge beside it, each plant about 45 pixels across in a 1024-pixel picture, set
along the row with small irregular gaps. The plants are a muted mid green, hex
#7E9660. Some bare soil shows at the ends of the rows. Do NOT draw fruit,
flowers, tall stalks, stakes, trellises, poles or vines.

THE BACKGROUND — everything outside the wooden frame is PURE FLAT WHITE,
#FFFFFF, with NO texture, NO shading, NO gradient, NO speckles, NO grey, NO
grass, NO soil, NO drop shadow. This white is going to be deleted, so the
outside of the frame must be a hard, clean, crisp edge against it. Nothing in
the bed itself may be white, near-white or pale.

NO SHADOWS — the bed casts nothing onto the white, and there is NO light
direction in this picture at all. This tile gets rotated and mirrored when it is
laid on the map, so a baked light direction would end up pointing several
different ways across the board.

Nothing else: no scarecrow, no tools, no baskets, no watering can, no well, no
water, no people, no animals, no buildings, no stones, no signboards, no
lettering. NO border, NO frame around the picture itself, NO vignette, NO
caption, NO logo.
