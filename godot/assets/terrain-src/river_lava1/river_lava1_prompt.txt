SUBJECT: The surface of a LAVA POOL in a volcanic crater, seen from DIRECTLY
OVERHEAD, filling the entire frame edge to edge. Nothing but the lava surface:
NO bank, NO rim, NO rock shore, NO ash beach, NO smoke, NO fire, NO sparks, and
nothing floating on it. This is the open middle of the pool, nowhere near its
edges.

WHAT IT LOOKS LIKE: MOLTEN LAVA is the whole ground of the picture — a
continuous glowing surface that covers the entire frame, with NOWHERE a
person could stand.

COLOUR: the molten lava is a deep glowing orange, hex #E0702A, with broad
softer swells of a hotter pale yellow-orange, hex #F2B04A, drifting across it
in no particular direction. Keep these colours: do NOT push the glow toward
pink or magenta. The tile must read as BRIGHT ORANGE. Drawn the game's way —
crisp, flat, stylized shapes — NOT a photograph, NOT soft blurred glow, NOT
smoke.

NO DIRECTION — CRITICAL: the surface must look the same whichever way the
picture is turned, because the same lava is laid across the board and is
rotated and mirrored tile by tile. NO flow lines, NO streaks, NO current, NO
long parallel bands, and no mark of any kind that shows a direction.

NO LANDMARKS — the single worst failure: any single shape wider than a tenth
of the frame reads as a feature and shows up as an obvious repeat when this
lava is cut into tiles and laid across the board. NO single bright pool, NO
single focal thing anywhere. Keep the large-scale colour even across the
whole frame: the swells vary, the balance of bright and deep does not.

Lit only by its own glow, evenly across the frame, with NO cast shadow, NO
light direction, and NO reflection of anything. Flat surface seen from
directly overhead. Square 1:1.
