SUBJECT: The inside of a small birdfolk village — a backdrop that stands
behind the fighting in a battle window. Clean stylized cel-shaded look: bold
flat shading, clear dark outlines, muted limited palette.

CONTENT, left to right, in one shallow row: a nest-hut raised on poles, a
second nest-hut raised on poles, a gap of open ground, a third smaller
nest-hut set a little back, and one lookout perch at the far right. Nothing
overlaps: the perch stands clear of the huts with white showing between its
platform and the nearest roof. The perch platform rises only a little above
the roofs.

- Nest-hut: a rounded basket of woven branches, warm brown #9A7C58 (shade
  #6C5539), the weave read as a few broad bands and not as fine basketwork,
  with one large round doorway facing the viewer and no door leaf in it.
  Conical roof of thatch in ONE flat colour, base #8C8272, shade #6E6657,
  light #A89E8C — no patchwork, no stripes, no moss, no part darker than
  #6E6657. No window, no chimney, no stone.
- Each hut is RAISED on three plain lashed poles of the same brown wood, so
  its floor stands well above the bottom of the picture and the doorway is
  out of reach from the ground — these people fly home. No steps, no stairs,
  no ladder, no ramp.
- Lookout perch: one straight bare pole with a small round platform of lashed
  sticks on top and nothing on the platform. No ladder, no rope, no flag, no
  banner, no streamer, no lantern.
- Nothing else: no perch rail, no fence, no people, no birdfolk, no birds, no
  feathers, no eggs, no animals, no pots, no baskets, no nets, no washing
  lines, no smoke, no magic, no lettering.

FRAMING — square picture. The whole row sits in the lower half, centred,
about 900 pixels wide in a 1024-pixel picture, with at least 60 pixels of
empty white on the LEFT and RIGHT of it. NOTHING is cut by the left, right
or top edge: every hut and the whole perch are completely inside the picture.
Only the bottom edge cuts: the support poles and the perch pole run straight
down to the bottom edge and are cut flat by it — no ground, no shadow, no
strip of background along the bottom.

Seen from slightly above and in front, huts facing the viewer, shallow depth:
the row does not run diagonally into the distance.

BACKGROUND — everything else is pure flat white #FFFFFF, no texture, no sky.
It will be deleted, so all outer edges must be crisp. Nothing in the village
may be white or near-white. Square 1:1.
