SUBJECT: A small BIRDFOLK VILLAGE of woven nest-huts raised on poles, drawn as
ONE compact block that stands upright and faces the viewer, the way a piece
stands on a game board. Drawn alone on a flat pure white background (#FFFFFF)
— no ground, no rock, no cliff, no path, no cast shadow on the ground, no
other objects.

VIEWING ANGLE — seen from the front and moderately above, about 35 degrees
down from the horizon: high enough that every roof is fully in view, low
enough that the doorways and walls of the huts stand clearly. The village is
set straight in front of the viewer, not turned. This is NOT an isometric
view and NOT a three-quarter view.

DESIGN (follow exactly, every count and position):
- Exactly THREE nest-huts and ONE lookout perch. Nothing else stands in the
  village.
- The lookout perch stands at the CENTRE of the block, behind the huts: one
  straight bare pole with a small round platform of lashed sticks on top, and
  nothing on the platform. It rises above all three roofs and is the highest
  thing in the picture. NO ladder, NO rope, NO flag, NO banner, NO streamer,
  NO lantern, NO bird on it.
- The three huts stand around the perch: one hut at the LEFT, one hut at the
  RIGHT, and one smaller hut at the FRONT centre, just in front of the pole.
  All three roofs are fully visible from this angle — the game recolours the
  roofs to show who holds the village, so no roof may be hidden by another
  hut or by the pole.
- Each hut is a rounded basket of woven branches, warm brown wood #9A7C58,
  shaded #6C5539, with one large round doorway facing the viewer and no door
  leaf in it. The weave is visible as a few broad bands, not as fine
  basketwork. Nothing else on the walls. NO window, NO stone, NO plaster, NO
  chimney, NO smoke, NO nesting material sticking out.
- Each hut is RAISED on three lashed poles of the same brown wood, so its
  floor sits clearly above the perch rail at the front and the doorways are
  out of reach from the ground — these people fly home. The poles are plain
  and straight. NO steps, NO stairs, NO ladder, NO ramp.
- Each hut has ONE conical roof of thatch that overhangs the wall all round.
  All three roofs are the SAME colour: base #8C8272, shaded side #6E6657, lit
  side #A89E8C. Every part of a roof is the same colour as its neighbours —
  do NOT alternate lighter and darker thatch, NO patchwork, NO stripes, NO
  moss, NO leaves lying on the roof, NO decorative bands, and NO part of any
  roof darker than #6E6657. Shading may follow only the slope of the roof,
  never individual strands.
- Across the FRONT of the block, in front of the front hut and below it, runs
  one low perch rail: a single straight branch resting on two short posts,
  the same brown as the huts, with one open gap in its middle. The rail is
  lower than the floors of the huts. NO gate, NO fence panels, NO weaving
  between the posts, NO torches.

THE FOOT — the bottom ends of the poles and of the two rail posts all stop on
one clean, straight, level line across the bottom of the object. That line is
where the game sets the village on the ground. Draw nothing below it.

SIZE AND SHAPE — a chunky block, about as wide as it is tall and never more
than 1.3 times as wide as tall. It is the only object in the picture; it sits
in the middle with clear space on all four sides and touches no edge. In a
1024-pixel picture the block is about 700 pixels tall, leaving at least 140
pixels of white on every side.

COLOR — the wood and the roofs stay muted and greyish; do NOT make them
brighter, yellower or more saturated than the numbers above, and do NOT make
them darker either. NO ochre, NO orange, NO gold, NO green.

LIGHT — soft light from above, short soft shadows that fall only on the
village itself. NOTHING casts a shadow onto the background. NO glow, NO shine,
NO light of any colour anywhere in this picture.

THE BACKGROUND — everything around the village is PURE FLAT WHITE, #FFFFFF,
with NO texture, NO shading, NO gradient, NO ground, NO drop shadow. This
white is going to be deleted and the game will draw its own ground there, so
the outline of the village must be a hard, clean, crisp edge against it.
Nothing in the village itself may be white or near-white.

Everything muted, in the same clean stylized cel-shaded look as the game's
terrain and unit art: bold flat shading, clear dark outlines, a mature,
slightly muted, limited color palette (NOT bright saturated, NOT painterly
photorealism).

NOTHING ELSE — no people, no birdfolk, no birds, no feathers, no eggs, no
animals, no washing lines, no pots, no baskets on the ground, no nets, no
weapons, no well, no cart, no flag, no rope bridge, no lettering, NO magic,
NO particles. Read at thumbnail size: a few large legible shapes, no fine
detail. NO border, NO frame around the picture itself, NO vignette.
Square 1:1.
