SUBJECT: The low PERCH RAIL of the birdfolk village — a long branch resting
on short posts, the rail the villagers land on — lying across the FOREGROUND
of a battle scene: a band that runs along the very bottom of the battle
window. The soldiers stand behind it and it hides their feet, so this is a
frame at the very front, not scenery in the distance.

THE RAIL — waist-high, one straight horizontal branch carried on short
upright posts, with a second thinner branch running below it and lashed to
the posts with cord. The posts are flush with the top rail, nothing pokes
above it. It is a plain run of rail: no gate, no gap, no posts taller than
the rail, no weaving between the posts, no plants growing on it, no flowers,
no moss, no lettering, and nothing standing on it or leaning against it.

LAYOUT — in a 1792 x 1024 picture the rail is a band about 320 pixels tall
resting on the BOTTOM edge. Everything above it is EMPTY WHITE. The top of
the rail is a straight, clearly readable horizontal line — that line is the
top of the band, and the only strong line in it. Below the top rail the band
is mostly open: the lower branch, the posts, and empty white between them.

THE TWO ENDS ARE DIFFERENT.
- LEFT end: the rail runs straight off the left edge of the picture and is
  cut by it. Nothing tapers and nothing is rounded off there.
- RIGHT end: the rail STOPS well inside the picture, a FIFTH of the
  picture's width in from the right edge — in a 1024-pixel picture that is
  about 200 pixels of empty white to the right of it, a plainly visible gap
  and not a sliver. The end is plain: one last upright post, with the two
  branches finishing at it. Do not let it fade out and do not run it off the
  right edge.

KEEP THE FACE QUIET — the wood is flat and even: the same straight branches
repeating, no knots, no bark texture, no leaves, no twigs, no pattern breaks,
no shading and no dirt. Numbers and bars are drawn on top of this band in the
game, so it must stay calm enough to read them against. Keep it a shade
darker than the huts of the village, leaning towards a silhouette: the wood
is a dark muted brown, base #6C5539, lashings and undersides #4E3C29, with
only a faint lighter #8A6E4C along the top of the rail.

NO LIGHT DIRECTION and no cast shadows: no shadow under the rail, none along
the bottom, none on the ground (no ground is drawn at all). The band is
mirrored left to right when the game lays it down.

THE BACKGROUND — everything above and between the wood is PURE FLAT WHITE
#FFFFFF, with no texture, no shading, no gradient. The white is deleted, so
the top line of the rail must be a hard, clean, crisp edge against it.
Nothing in the rail may be white or near-white.

STYLE NOTE — bold flat shapes, clear dark outlines, muted limited palette,
the same clean cel-shaded look as the rest of the game's terrain.
