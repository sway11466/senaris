SUBJECT: A low stone parapet of an old footbridge, seen from the front and a
little above, as a WIDE horizontal band for the foreground of a battle scene.

SHAPE: a very wide picture -- the parapet crosses the ENTIRE frame from the
left edge to the right edge and runs off both sides, one continuous straight
wall with no ends, no posts at the ends, no gap, no gate. The wall is LOW: the
band of wall fills only the lower part of the frame, and EVERYTHING above the
coping line is fully TRANSPARENT (alpha), not sky and not background. The
bottom edge of the wall touches the bottom edge of the frame.

THE WALL: heavy weathered stone blocks with thin dark joints and a flat coping
course on top. Big simple shapes, readable at a glance -- this band sits in
front of the fighting troops, so keep it QUIET: dark, low-contrast, leaning
toward silhouette, with only a hint of the block joints. NO carving, NO moss,
NO ivy, NO lantern, NO fine texture, NO bright highlight anywhere.

COLOR: the same dry grey stone family as the bridge deck (around hex #8C8A82)
but noticeably DARKER, as a shaded foreground -- nothing in the band may be
brighter than a mid grey, and nothing may be pure black.

Lit dimly and evenly, with NO visible light direction and NO cast shadow.
