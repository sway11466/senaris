STYLE: A side-on rock texture for a fantasy tactics game, in the same clean
stylized cel-shaded look as the game's terrain and unit art: bold flat shading, a
mature, slightly muted, limited color palette (NOT bright saturated, NOT
painterly photorealism). Viewed straight from the FRONT, face-on — a flat lay
with NO perspective, NO horizon, NO sky, NO ground line, NO floor. The texture
fills the ENTIRE square frame edge to edge, with NO border, NO frame, NO
vignette, and no single big focal object. It must tile SEAMLESSLY LEFT TO RIGHT:
the left and right edges continue into each other. CRITICAL: vary the stones
RANDOMLY and UNEVENLY — never a repeated motif, never the same shape twice,
never in columns of equal width. Avoid large, high-contrast patches (they repeat
visibly when tiled); keep large-scale color even and low-contrast. Square 1:1.

SUBJECT: The front of a heap of SCREE — loose, angular broken stones piled on
top of each other, seen face-on. The stones are separate pieces of rock,
fist-sized to head-sized, sharp-edged and irregular, resting on one another at
every angle, with narrow dark gaps between them where they touch. Some gaps open
into small dark hollows where a stone is missing. The pile is the same broken
rock as the top of the scree field, seen from the side.

The stones reach the TOP EDGE of the frame evenly, with no skyline, no crest and
no single stone breaking the top: the heap has been cut off flat where the frame
ends. The bottom edge is cut off the same way — the stones simply continue past
it, with NO ground and NO base.

Dry warm grey-tan stone, hex #A89574 — the stones must use this exact colour and
keep it consistent. Do NOT drift cooler, bluer, more purple, more yellow or more
saturated than it, and do NOT make it lighter or darker either. Shade every facet
as ONE FLAT TONE — a lighter top facet, a mid side facet, a dark gap — flat cel
shading of the rock's own faces, NOT a cast shadow and NOT a light direction,
and no gap near-black: the gaps are dark grey-brown, hex #6B6255.

Read at thumbnail size: the whole face is only a narrow strip on screen, so keep
the stones CHUNKY and few — about eight to twelve of them across the width of
the frame — with no fine detail that would disappear. Spread them evenly from
top to bottom, with no single large feature that sits at one height only.

NOT masonry — NO stacked bricks, NO mortar lines, NO worked edges, NO tool
marks. NO vegetation of any kind — NO grass, NO moss, NO lichen, NO soil. NO
water, NO caves, NO objects of any kind.
