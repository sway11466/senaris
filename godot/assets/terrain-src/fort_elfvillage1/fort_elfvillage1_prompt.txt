SUBJECT: A small ELF VILLAGE in a forest clearing, drawn as ONE compact block
that stands upright and faces the viewer, the way a piece stands on a game
board. Drawn alone on a flat pure white background (#FFFFFF) — no ground, no
grass, no path, no cast shadow on the ground, no other objects.

VIEWING ANGLE — seen from the front and moderately above, about 35 degrees
down from the horizon: high enough that every roof is fully in view, low
enough that the doors and walls of the huts stand clearly. The village is
set straight in front of the viewer, not turned. This is NOT an isometric
view and NOT a three-quarter view.

DESIGN (follow exactly, every count and position):
- Exactly THREE round huts and ONE tree. Nothing else stands in the village.
- The tree stands at the CENTRE of the block, behind the huts: one
  broad-leaved tree with a thick straight trunk and one big round crown of
  leaves, deep muted green #4F6B3F, shaded #384D2C. Its crown rises above
  all three roofs and is the highest thing in the picture. NO second tree,
  NO bushes, NO hanging vines, NO lanterns in the tree.
- The three huts stand around the trunk: one hut at the LEFT, one hut at
  the RIGHT, and one smaller hut at the FRONT centre, just in front of the
  trunk. All three roofs are fully visible from this angle — the game
  recolours the roofs to show who holds the village, so no roof may be
  hidden by another hut or by the tree.
- Each hut: a round wall of upright split logs, warm brown wood #9A7C58,
  shaded #6C5539, with one arched plank door facing the viewer and one
  small round window beside the door. Nothing else on the walls. NO stone,
  NO plaster, NO chimney, NO smoke.
- Each hut has ONE conical roof of wooden shingles that overhangs the wall
  all round. All three roofs are the SAME colour: base #8C8272, shaded side
  #6E6657, lit side #A89E8C. Every shingle is the same colour as its
  neighbours — do NOT alternate lighter and darker shingles, NO patchwork,
  NO stripes, NO moss, NO leaves lying on the roof, NO decorative bands, and
  NO part of any roof darker than #6E6657. Shading may follow only the slope
  of the roof, never individual shingles.
- Across the FRONT of the block, in front of the front hut, runs one low
  fence of woven living branches (wattle), the same brown as the hut walls,
  with one open gap in its middle. The fence is lower than the doors of the
  huts. NO gate leaf, NO posts taller than the fence, NO torches.

THE FOOT — the base of the fence is a clean, straight, level line across the
bottom of the object. That line is where the game sets the village on the
ground. Draw nothing below it.

SIZE AND SHAPE — a chunky block, about as wide as it is tall and never more
than 1.3 times as wide as tall. It is the only object in the picture; it sits
in the middle with clear space on all four sides and touches no edge. In a
1024-pixel picture the block is about 700 pixels tall, leaving at least 140
pixels of white on every side.

COLOR — the wood and the roofs stay muted and greyish; do NOT make them
brighter, yellower or more saturated than the numbers above, and do NOT make
them darker either. Only the tree is green. NO ochre, NO orange, NO gold.

LIGHT — soft light from above, short soft shadows that fall only on the
village itself. NOTHING casts a shadow onto the background. NO glow, NO shine,
NO light of any colour anywhere in this picture.

THE BACKGROUND — everything around the village is PURE FLAT WHITE, #FFFFFF,
with NO texture, NO shading, NO gradient, NO ground, NO drop shadow. This
white is going to be deleted and the game will draw its own ground there, so
the outline of the village must be a hard, clean, crisp edge against it.
Nothing in the village itself may be white or near-white.

Everything muted, in the same clean stylized cel-shaded look as the game's
terrain and unit art: bold flat shading, clear dark outlines, a mature,
slightly muted, limited color palette (NOT bright saturated, NOT painterly
photorealism).

NOTHING ELSE — no people, no elves, no animals, no birds, no mushrooms, no
flowers, no grass, no path, no well, no cart, no banner, no flag, no rope
bridge, no ladder, no platform in the tree, no lettering, NO magic, NO
particles. Read at thumbnail size: a few large legible shapes, no fine
detail. NO border, NO frame around the picture itself, NO vignette.
Square 1:1.
