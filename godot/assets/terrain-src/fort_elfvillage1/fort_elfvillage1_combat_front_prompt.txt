SUBJECT: The low WATTLE FENCE of the elf village — a fence of woven living
branches — lying across the FOREGROUND of a battle scene: a band that runs
along the very bottom of the battle window. The soldiers stand behind it and
it hides their feet, so this is a frame at the very front, not scenery in the
distance.

THE FENCE — waist-high, made of thin supple branches woven horizontally in
and out between short upright stakes. The stakes are flush with the top of
the weave, nothing pokes above the fence. It is a plain run of fence: no
gate, no gap, no posts taller than the weave, no rope, no plants growing on
it, no flowers, no moss, no lettering, and nothing standing on it or leaning
against it.

LAYOUT — in a 1792 x 1024 picture the fence is a band about 320 pixels tall
resting on the BOTTOM edge. Everything above it is EMPTY WHITE. The top of
the weave is a straight, clearly readable horizontal line — that line is the
top of the band, and the only strong line in it.

THE TWO ENDS ARE DIFFERENT.
- LEFT end: the fence runs straight off the left edge of the picture and is
  cut by it. Nothing tapers and nothing is rounded off there.
- RIGHT end: the fence STOPS well inside the picture, a FIFTH of the
  picture's width in from the right edge — in a 1024-pixel picture that is
  about 200 pixels of empty white to the right of it, a plainly visible gap
  and not a sliver. The end is plain: one last upright stake, with the ends
  of the woven branches finishing at it. Do not let it fade out and do not
  run it off the right edge.

KEEP THE FACE QUIET — the weave below the top line is flat and even: the
same thin branches repeating, no thick branches, no knots, no leaves, no
holes, no pattern breaks, no shading and no dirt. Numbers and bars are drawn
on top of this face in the game, so it must stay calm enough to read them
against. Keep it a shade darker than the huts of the village, leaning
towards a silhouette: the wood is a dark muted brown, base #6C5539, joints
and undersides #4E3C29, with only a faint lighter #8A6E4C on the top of the
weave.

NO LIGHT DIRECTION and no cast shadows: no shadow under the top, none along
the bottom, none on the ground (no ground is drawn at all). The band is
mirrored left to right when the game lays it down.

THE BACKGROUND — everything above the fence is PURE FLAT WHITE #FFFFFF,
with no texture, no shading, no gradient. The white is deleted, so the top
line of the weave must be a hard, clean, crisp edge against it. Nothing in
the fence may be white or near-white.

STYLE NOTE — bold flat shapes, clear dark outlines, muted limited palette,
the same clean cel-shaded look as the rest of the game's terrain.
