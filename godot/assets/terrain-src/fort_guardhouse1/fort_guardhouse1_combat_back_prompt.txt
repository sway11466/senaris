SUBJECT: The town's GUARD POST, standing behind the fighting in a battle window —
ONE building, drawn whole, seen close from the front. The soldiers stand in the
street in front of it.

THE BUILDING — one single-storey building of rough grey fieldstone #8C8A82 laid
without courses, with exposed timber framing in weathered brown #6E5A42 in its
gable. A stout timber double door in the middle of its front wall, one small
shuttered window beside it, and a timber rack with three or four spears leaning
against the wall by the door. Its roof is one steep gabled slate roof, the biggest
shape in the picture, and its ridge and both slopes are fully in view. Neutral
slate: base tone #6E6D6B, catch-light #9B9995, shadow #4D4C4A — hold those numbers,
the game recolours this roof to show who holds the post. One flat colour per slope:
no stripes, no chequer, no tile picked out, nothing sitting on it.

NOTHING IS CUT EXCEPT THE BOTTOM — this is the rule that keeps being broken, so
read it twice. The building is drawn COMPLETE, standing well inside the frame with
empty white to the left of it and empty white to the right of it. It must NOT be
stretched to reach the sides. Only the BOTTOM edge cuts it: the front wall runs
down to the very bottom edge and is cut flat by it, with no strip of white beneath.

NOTHING ELSE STANDS IN THE PICTURE — no second building, no cottages, no yard
wall, no boundary wall, no fence, no gate, no lane, no street furniture, no
people, animals, carts, barrels, flags or smoke. If a run of stone appears with no
roof above it, the picture is wrong.

SHAPE AND PLACE IN THE FRAME — the picture is square. The building sits in the
BOTTOM HALF: it is about half the width of the picture across, its ridge reaches
near the middle of the picture, and it stands slightly right of centre. The whole
top half, and both side margins, stay empty white.

VIEWING ANGLE — seen from a slight high three-quarter angle, close: the front wall,
the door and the window are clearly readable, the roof is fully in view above them,
and the building's right corner turns just enough to show a hand's breadth of side
wall. Draw no ground anywhere.

THE BACKGROUND — everything around the building is PURE FLAT WHITE #FFFFFF, with no
texture, no shading, no gradient, no sky, no clouds. This white is deleted, so
every outline against it must be hard, clean and crisp. Nothing in the building may
be white or near-white.

NO LIGHT DIRECTION and no cast shadows: none under the eaves, none from the spear
rack, none on the ground. The band is mirrored left to right when the game lays it
down. The two slopes of the roof may differ slightly; that is all the shading.

This is a near view, so a little more detail than the map piece is welcome — the
courses of the stonework, the boards of the door, the ridge line of the roof — but
keep it in bold flat shapes, with no fine texture that would fight with the figures
standing in front of it.
