SUBJECT: The low stone WALL of the guard post's yard, lying across the FOREGROUND
of a battle scene — a band that runs along the very bottom of the battle window.
The soldiers stand behind it and it hides their feet, so this is a frame at the
very front, not scenery in the distance.

THE WALL — waist-high, built of the same rough grey fieldstone as the guard post,
#8C8A82, laid without courses, with a flat coping of squared stones along its top.
It is a plain run of wall: no gate, no posts, no railing, no plants, no moss, no
lettering, and nothing standing on it or leaning against it.

LAYOUT — in a 1792 x 1024 picture the wall is a band about 320 pixels tall resting
on the BOTTOM edge. Everything above it is EMPTY WHITE. The coping is a straight,
clearly readable horizontal line — that line is the top of the band, and the only
strong line in it.

THE TWO ENDS ARE DIFFERENT.
- LEFT end: the wall runs straight off the left edge of the picture and is cut by
  it. Nothing tapers and nothing is rounded off there.
- RIGHT end: the wall STOPS well inside the picture, a FIFTH of the picture's width
  in from the right edge — in a 1024-pixel picture that is about 200 pixels of
  empty white to the right of it, a plainly visible gap and not a sliver. The end
  is plain and squared off: the coping stops with it and the end face of the
  stonework shows. Do not let it fade out and do not run it off the right edge.

KEEP THE FACE QUIET — the stonework below the coping is flat and even: small
stones, thin dark joints, no large stones, no pattern, no cracks, no holes, no
shading and no dirt. Numbers and bars are drawn on top of this face in the game,
so it must stay calm enough to read them against.

NO LIGHT DIRECTION and no cast shadows: no shadow under the coping, none along the
bottom, none on the ground (no ground is drawn at all). The band is mirrored left
to right when the game lays it down.

THE BACKGROUND — everything above the wall is PURE FLAT WHITE #FFFFFF, with no
texture, no shading, no gradient. The white is deleted, so the coping line must be
a hard, clean, crisp edge against it. Nothing in the wall may be white or
near-white.

STYLE NOTE — bold flat shapes, clear dark outlines, muted limited palette, the same
clean cel-shaded look as the rest of the game's terrain.
