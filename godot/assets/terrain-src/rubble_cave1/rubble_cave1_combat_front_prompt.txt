SUBJECT: A NEW composition, not a crop or extension of any earlier picture:
a long, DENSE heap of broken cave rock lying across the floor in the
FOREGROUND of a battle scene, seen from the front at eye level, for a wide
landscape picture (16:9). Draw it as ONE SELF-CONTAINED object: the heap has
a clear left end and a clear right end, both well inside the picture, with
wide empty white on either side. The most important rule: NOTHING is cut by
the frame — the heap tapers down to loose chunks at both ends and STOPS, and
no rock touches the left edge, the right edge or the top edge.

LAYOUT — in a 1792 x 1024 picture, the heap lies in a LOW STRIP about 240
pixels tall resting on the bottom edge, about 1150 pixels long and CENTRED:
its left end stops about 320 pixels in from the left edge and its right end
stops about 320 pixels in from the right edge. Those margins stay EMPTY WHITE,
as does everything above the strip. The rock may rest on the bottom edge. No
rock may rise above the strip.

THE RUBBLE — this is a ROCKFALL, not a few stones on a floor: broken boulders,
slabs and chunks piled two or three deep along the whole length of the heap,
wedged against and on top of each other at every angle, with smaller rubble
and grit filling every gap. There is NO bare floor anywhere along the heap,
NO path through it, NO flat footing — a soldier on foot could not cross it;
only something that flies could pass. The heap is uneven: higher in two or
three places where big boulders stack, lower between them, ragged along the
top, never a smooth bank and never a row. Plain fallen rock only: NOT
stalagmites, NOT worked stone, no columns, no bricks, no wall.

STYLE — the same clean stylized cel-shaded look as the game's terrain and unit
art: bold flat shading, clear dark outlines, a mature, slightly muted, limited
color palette (NOT bright saturated, NOT painterly photorealism). Because this
rubble sits CLOSE to the camera, in front of the soldiers' feet, draw it a
shade DARKER and SIMPLER than the map version — big plain shapes, little
inner detail, dim in shadow — so it frames the scene rather than competing
with the figures behind it.

COLOR — dull grey-brown cave rock, hex #5E564C (darker than the map version
because it is in shadow), each piece a slightly different flat tone between
#6A6156 and #4E473F. NO moss, NO crystals, NO glow, NO water, NO strong red,
blue or green anywhere.

THE BACKGROUND — everything that is not rock is PURE FLAT WHITE, #FFFFFF: NO
texture, NO shading, NO gradient, NO ground, NO floor, NO cave wall, NO drop
shadow on the white. This white is going to be deleted and the strip laid over
the game's own battle scene, so every outline must be a hard, clean, crisp edge
against it. Nothing in the rock may be white or near-white.

LIGHT — from directly above only, short soft shadows that fall only on the
rock itself; NOTHING casts a shadow onto the white, and NO light from the left
or the right.

Nothing else: no creature, no bones, no plants, no torches, no treasure, no
lettering. NO border, NO frame around the picture itself, NO vignette.
Landscape 16:9.
