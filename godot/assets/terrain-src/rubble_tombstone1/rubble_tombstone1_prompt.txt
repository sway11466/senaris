SUBJECT: A HEAP OF COLLAPSED MASONRY in an old graveyard — the stonework of a
burial vault that has come down, piled together with the gravestones it took
with it — as drawn on the world map of a classic 2D role-playing game: seen
from the front and slightly above, standing upright and facing the bottom of
the frame — nothing lying flat, no perspective.

ONE heap, wider than it is tall, made mostly of DRESSED BUILDING STONE: squared
ashlar blocks, broken roof slabs and cracked lintels stacked two or three deep,
wedged against and on top of each other at every angle, with smaller chips and
loose grit filling every gap. Jammed into the pile at wrong angles are THREE OR
FOUR old GRAVESTONES — slabs with rounded tops, one snapped in half, one buried
to its shoulders — so the heap plainly reads as a graveyard's, not a
mountainside's.

IT IS A WALL, NOT A PATH: the pieces sit unstable and interlocked, with NO gap
through it, NO ledge, NO step, NO flat footing — a soldier on foot could not
climb over it. It must NOT read as a few markers standing in open ground: this
is a solid MASS of stone, packed from end to end, with no ground showing
anywhere inside it. It is fallen rubble, NOT a cliff, NOT a standing wall, NOT
an intact building, NOT a doorway: no arch left standing, no roof, no column.

SIZE — the heap sits in the MIDDLE of the picture. In a 1024-pixel picture it
is about 640 pixels wide and about 460 pixels tall, leaving at least 190 pixels
of white on every side. It must NOT touch any edge and must NOT be cut by any
edge.

COLOR — dull grey building stone, hex #7F7B6E, each block a slightly different
flat tone between #918D80 and #67635A so the pieces separate from one another
by tone alone. The gravestones caught in the heap are the same weathered
grey-green as the markers standing elsewhere in this graveyard, hex #8A8676.
The game draws its own dry khaki graveyard grass, hex #B0A888, behind this
picture, and the heap must stand out plainly against it, so keep the whole
thing CLEARLY DARKER and cooler than that grass. Do NOT make the stone yellow,
warm, golden, pale or brighter than #918D80. Everything muted, in the same
clean stylized cel-shaded look as the game's terrain and unit art: bold flat
shading, clear dark outlines, a mature, slightly muted, limited color palette
(NOT bright saturated, NOT painterly photorealism). A little dull grey lichen
is welcome; NO bright green moss, NO crystals, NO glow, NO water, NO strong
red, blue or green anywhere.

THE BACKGROUND — everything around the heap is PURE FLAT WHITE, #FFFFFF, with
NO texture, NO shading, NO gradient, NO ground, NO grass, NO soil, NO drop
shadow. This white is going to be deleted and the game will draw its own
graveyard grass there, so the outline of the heap must be a hard, clean, crisp
edge against it. Nothing in the stone may be white or near-white.

LIGHT — dim light from above and slightly in front, short soft shadows that
fall only on the stone itself; NOTHING casts a shadow onto the white.

Nothing else: no creature, no people, no bones, no skulls, no coffins, no urns,
no flowers, no candles, no plants, no torches, no treasure, no lettering and no
inscriptions. NO glow, NO mist, NO magic. Read at thumbnail size: a few large
legible shapes, no fine detail. NO border, NO frame around the picture itself,
NO vignette. Square 1:1.
