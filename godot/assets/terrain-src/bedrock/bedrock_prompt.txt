SUBJECT: The bare rocky SLOPE of a mountain, seen straight from directly overhead — steep broken ground that a climber can get up, slowly. This is ONE CONTINUOUS BODY OF EXPOSED BEDROCK, never an assembly of separate stones: the rock has split along its own lines into broad shelves and terraces that step up and down against each other, and the shelves run on unbroken off all four edges of the frame. Each shelf is a large flat plane — the biggest about 1/5 of the frame across (about 200px in a 1000px image) — and where two shelves meet, a short step or riser separates them, drawn as a narrow darker band, not as an outline around a rock. Shallow ridges and gullies wander across the slope, branching and petering out, running in NO single direction and never parallel. Loose scree and rubble collect ONLY in the hollows and at the foot of the steps, in a few small drifts, never spread evenly over the frame.

CRITICAL, and the easiest thing to get wrong: this must NOT look like loose stones. NO rounded boulders, NO cobbles, NO gravel field, NO pebble pavement, NO dry-stone wall, NO tightly packed same-sized rocks each ringed by its own thick dark contour. Lines appear only where two rock faces meet, and they are thin.

Pale warm grey stone, hex #BCAF9B — use this exact stone colour and keep it consistent across all variants. Do NOT drift cooler, bluer, more purple or more saturated than it, and do NOT make it darker than it either: the stone stays pale. Shade every plane as ONE FLAT TONE — a lighter shelf, a mid shelf, a darker riser — flat cel shading of the rock's own faces, NOT a cast shadow, NOT a light direction, and no face near-black.

Keep the large-scale colour even and low-contrast so it tiles without an obvious repeat, with NO single summit, peak, boulder or crag dominating the frame — this is a piece of a mountainside, not a picture of a mountain. The rock must read as climbable steep ground, NOT as a wall of spikes. NO soil, NO grass, NO moss, NO snow, NO trees, NO water, NO path, NO steps cut by hand, NO buildings, NO ruins, NO bones, NO props of any kind.
