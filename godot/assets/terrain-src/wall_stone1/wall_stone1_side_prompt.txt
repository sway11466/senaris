STYLE: A side-on wall-face texture for a fantasy tactics game, in the same clean
stylized cel-shaded look as the game's terrain and unit art: bold flat shading, a
mature, slightly muted, limited color palette (NOT bright saturated, NOT
painterly photorealism). Viewed straight from the FRONT, face-on — a flat lay
with NO perspective, NO horizon, NO sky, NO ground line, NO floor. The texture
fills the ENTIRE square frame edge to edge, with NO border, NO frame, NO
vignette, and no single big focal object. It must tile SEAMLESSLY LEFT TO RIGHT:
the left and right edges continue into each other. CRITICAL: vary and scatter
the rock RANDOMLY and UNEVENLY — never a repeated motif, never the same shape
twice, never in rows or a regular grid. Avoid large, high-contrast patches (they
repeat visibly when tiled); keep large-scale color even and low-contrast.
Square 1:1.

SUBJECT: The raw ROCK FACE of a wall of solid stone, seen face-on. The face is
made entirely of broken rock: chunky irregular blocks of stone of many different
sizes, packed tightly against each other with no gaps of anything else, each
with blunt facets and rounded worn edges, some jutting forward and some set
back. Deep narrow crevices between the blocks fall into shadow. Dark cold
grey-brown stone, base color around #5F584F, with the stone itself varying
subtly from block to block. Blocks read slightly taller than wide so the face
has a faint downward grain. Lit dimly from above: the upper facets catch a
little light, the undercuts stay dark. Raw, heavy, geological. NO tool marks,
NO pick or chisel gouges, NO scratches, NO carved shapes. NO vegetation of any
kind — NO grass, NO moss, NO lichen, NO plants, NO soil, NO puddles, NO snow.
NO objects of any kind — NO torches, NO bones, NO ore, NO crystals, NO props.
Natural broken rock, NOT masonry — NO stacked bricks, NO mortar lines, NO worked
edges. This is only the flat rock face — NO top surface, NO floor, NO arch, NO
opening, NO cast shadow onto anything else.
