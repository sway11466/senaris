SUBJECT: One small rural stone chapel, standing alone on a flat pure white
background (#FFFFFF) — no ground, no grass, no path, no cast shadow on the
ground, no other objects. Seen from slightly above eye level. The front
gable wall with its door faces the viewer almost square-on, the building
turned only a LITTLE to one side — much closer to a straight front view
than to a corner view: the right side wall with its windows is visible
but strongly compressed, taking up far less of the picture than the front
wall. Keep the turn small.

DESIGN (follow exactly, every count and position):
- One rectangular nave only. Strictly symmetrical building. NO tower, NO
  transept, NO side annex, NO porch, NO chimney.
- One steep gabled saddle roof covering the whole nave.
- Front gable wall: one pointed-arch double door centered at ground level,
  and one round window centered directly above the door. Nothing else on
  this wall.
- On the roof ridge, at its front end: one small stone bell-cote (an open
  stone frame) holding one bell, topped by one cross.
- Each side wall: three pointed-arch windows evenly spaced, and four
  buttresses at equal intervals — one at each corner of the wall and one
  between each pair of windows.
- Walls: rough-cut stone, base colour #B1AC9B, shaded side #7A776C.
- Roof: flat stone slabs in one uniform colour — base #97948A, shaded
  areas #7C796F, lit areas #B7B2A4. Every slab is the SAME colour as its
  neighbours — do NOT alternate lighter and darker slabs, NO patchwork,
  NO stripes, NO checker pattern, NO decorative bands, and NO part of the
  roof darker than #7C796F. Shading may follow only the slope of the
  roof, never individual slabs.

Square 1:1.
