SUBJECT: A small stone CHAPEL standing in an old graveyard, as drawn on the
world map of a classic 2D role-playing game: the ground seen from directly
overhead, but the building drawn from the front and slightly above, standing up
and facing the bottom of the frame — nothing lying flat, no perspective.

One small country chapel of pale grey dressed stone, sitting a little off
centre. It is a single hall — one nave, no side wings, no cloister, no tower
block — with a steep pitched roof, and it stands noticeably TALLER than it is
wide so it reads as a chapel and not as a house. On the roof ridge above the
front wall sits a small open bell-gable, and on top of that a plain stone CROSS.
That cross is the most important thing in the picture: it must be simple, thick,
clearly upright and unmistakable as a cross even at thumbnail size, because it
is what tells the player this is a holy building and on their side. It stands
straight and whole — NOT tilted, NOT broken, NOT fallen, NOT desecrated.

In the front wall, facing the bottom of the frame, is a tall arched TIMBER DOOR,
plainly centred and firmly SHUT — a solid boarded door with heavy iron hinges,
with no opening, no gap and no darkness showing through. Nothing comes out of
here. Two or three worn steps run down from it to the ground. High on either
side of the door is one narrow arched window with plain leaded glass, dark and
unlit. Arched openings and the pitched roof are what carry the shape; keep them
big and simple.

Around the chapel, a handful of old gravestones — plain weathered slabs with
rounded tops, small, standing at slightly different angles, scattered
irregularly and never in a row. NO statues, NO urns, NO iron railings, NO gate,
NO grave crosses among them: the only cross in the picture is the one on the
bell-gable, so that it stays the single clear mark.

The chapel stands in open ground with NOTHING marking off a plot around it.
NO kerb, NO low wall, NO stone border, NO fence, NO hedge, NO paving, NO
platform, NO plinth, NO raised base, NO steps up to the site, NO enclosure of
any kind. There is no boundary anywhere in the picture: the graveyard simply
continues off all four edges, because identical graveyard ground is drawn on the
tiles next to this one and the two must run together with no seam.

The stone and the roof are plain grey with almost no colour in them — neither
warm nor cool — and only a little weathered brown in the door timber. NO ochre,
NO warm tan, and NO strong red, blue or green anywhere (those colors mark who
owns the place).

The ground is the same neglected dying grassland as the surrounding graveyard
tiles: dry yellowed grass, hex #B2B28E, with pale dusty soil, hex #A89877,
showing through in only a handful of small openings. Use these exact colors,
carry them out to all four edges, keep the ground even and low-contrast, and
scatter only a FEW small tufts and pale pebbles over it, randomly and unevenly.

CRITICAL — the ground is seen from straight above and is FLAT. Draw it as a
plain overhead field of grass with no depth to it at all: NO perspective, NO
receding or tilted ground plane, NO vanishing point, NO isometric or
three-quarter view of the site, NO horizon. Only the chapel itself is drawn
standing up and facing the bottom of the frame. Everything lying on the ground —
grass, soil, pebbles — is seen flat from overhead.

Composition: the chapel and every gravestone sit together in the middle of the
picture, within an imaginary circle covering about four fifths of the frame,
with a clear margin of plain empty grass all the way round them. Nothing but
grass reaches the outer band or the corners of the picture. Inside that circle
the chapel is the subject and sits large and legible.

The picture is an ordinary full square image of the ground, filled edge to edge:
NO frame, NO border, NO outline, NO vignette, NO drawn shape around the scene, NO
blank or white area anywhere. The grass runs right off all four edges.

Read at thumbnail size, so a FEW large legible shapes and no fine detail: no
picked-out carvings, no inscriptions, no lettering, no stained-glass pictures,
no banners, no bells drawn in detail, no lanterns, no people, no bones, no
skulls, no cobwebs. It must NOT read as a cluster of houses or a walled town —
it is ONE building.
The mood is quiet, weathered and kept, NOT ruined and NOT haunted: NO glow of
any kind, NO lit windows, NO mist, NO fire, NO floating lights, NO magic
circles. Flat shading, dim light from above and slightly in front, short soft
shadows. Square 1:1.
