SUBJECT: A CROWDED GROUP OF OLD GRAVE MARKERS standing in a neglected
graveyard — five or six weathered headstones packed close together and nothing
else — as drawn on the world map of a classic 2D role-playing game: seen from
the front and slightly above, standing upright and facing the bottom of the
frame — nothing lying flat, no perspective.

THE ARRANGEMENT — the stones stand in TWO RANKS. Three or four of them stand in
front, and two more stand BEHIND and BETWEEN them, drawn a little smaller and
partly hidden by the front ones, so the group has depth and the eye cannot see
a clear way through it. The stones are CLOSE together: the gaps between them
are narrower than the stones themselves. One or two lean over until they nearly
touch their neighbour at the top, while their bases stay apart.

THIS IS BAD FOOTING, and the picture has to say so: stones everywhere, hardly
anywhere left to put a foot down. But every stone still STANDS ON THE FLAT
GROUND at its own base — nothing is stacked on anything else, nothing rests on
top of another stone, and there is NO mound, NO heap, NO pile, NO rubble, NO
raised earth, NO kerb, NO plinth and NO wall. This must read as a thicket of
upright stones, never as a heap of stone.

THE STONES — give every one a different shape and a different lean: a slab with
a rounded top, a squared-off slab with a chamfered head, a short stone cross
with thick stubby arms, a stub whose top has snapped off, one half sunk into the
ground up to its shoulders. Vary the heights, but NOT in order: do NOT arrange
them as an even row, and do NOT step the heights up or down across the picture
like a staircase. The tallest should not stand at either end.

SIZE — the group sits in the MIDDLE of the picture and is TALLER THAN IT IS
WIDE. In a 1024-pixel picture it is about 520 pixels wide and about 600 pixels
tall, the tallest stone about 560 pixels, leaving at least 200 pixels of white
on every side. It must NOT touch any edge and must NOT be cut by any edge. Do
NOT spread the stones out sideways into a wide low row: keep them packed
together and let the group stand UP. The stones are tall slabs, not squat
blocks — the tallest is about twice as tall as it is wide.

COLOR — weathered dark grey-green stone, hex #8A8676, each marker a slightly
different flat tone between #9A9686 and #6E6A5A so the stones separate from one
another by tone alone — this matters most where one stone stands in front of
another. The game draws its own dry khaki graveyard grass, hex #B0A888, behind
this picture, and the stones must stand out plainly against it, so keep them
CLEARLY DARKER and cooler than that grass — about as much darker as wet slate
is against dry straw. Do NOT make the stone yellow, warm, golden, pale or
brighter than #9A9686, and do NOT make it darker than #6E6A5A. A little dull
grey lichen at the base of a stone is welcome; NO bright green moss.

Everything muted, in the same clean stylized cel-shaded look as the game's
terrain and unit art: bold flat shading, clear dark outlines, a mature,
slightly muted, limited color palette (NOT bright saturated, NOT painterly
photorealism).

THE BACKGROUND — everything around the markers is PURE FLAT WHITE, #FFFFFF,
with NO texture, NO shading, NO gradient, NO ground, NO grass, NO soil, NO drop
shadow. This white is going to be deleted and the game will draw its own
graveyard grass there, so the outline of every stone must be a hard, clean,
crisp edge against it. Nothing in the stones may be white or near-white.

LIGHT — dim light from above and slightly in front, short soft shadows that
fall only on the stones themselves; NOTHING casts a shadow onto the white.

Nothing else: no building, no tomb, no mausoleum, no doorway, no fence, no
gate, no path, no statues, no urns, no flowers, no candles, no bones, no
skulls, no open graves, no creature, no people, no lettering and no
inscriptions. NO glow, NO mist, NO magic. Read at thumbnail size: a few large
legible shapes, no fine detail. NO border, NO frame around the picture itself,
NO vignette. Square 1:1.
