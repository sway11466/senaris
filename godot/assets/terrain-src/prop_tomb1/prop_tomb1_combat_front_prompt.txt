SUBJECT: A NEW composition, not a crop or extension of any earlier picture:
a CROWDED THICKET OF OLD GRAVE MARKERS standing on the ground in the FOREGROUND
of a battle scene, seen from the front at eye level, for a wide landscape
picture (16:9). Draw it as ONE SELF-CONTAINED band of stones that sits well
inside the frame with empty white at both ends. The most important rule of this
picture: NOTHING is cut by the frame. Every stone is drawn COMPLETE and WHOLE
with white beyond it; no stone touches the left edge, the right edge or the top
edge; nothing continues off the picture.

The markers are the same weathered kind as the game's map version — slabs with
rounded tops, squared-off slabs with chamfered heads, short stone crosses with
thick stubby arms, snapped stubs, some half sunk into the ground up to their
shoulders — each leaning at its own angle, never straight.

LAYOUT — in a 1792 x 1024 picture, the stones stand in a LOW STRIP about 340
pixels tall resting on the bottom edge, running the whole length of the strip
with NO gap anywhere along it: it starts about 120 pixels in from the left edge
and stops about 120 pixels in from the right edge, about 1550 pixels long. At
each end the outermost stones are the shortest, and the band simply STOPS —
nothing is cut, nothing continues off the frame. The margins at both ends and
everything above the strip stay EMPTY WHITE. The stones rest on the bottom
edge. NO stone may rise above the strip.

DEPTH — the stones stand in TWO OR THREE RANKS, not a single line. The front
rank is the tallest and its bases sit on the bottom edge. Behind it, a second
rank is drawn smaller with its bases HIGHER up inside the strip, showing
between and above the front stones; where a third rank appears, only the tops
of those stones show. Ranks overlap so the eye cannot see a clear way through
the band at any point. Fifteen to twenty stones in all.

CROWDING — the gaps between neighbouring stones are narrower than the stones
themselves, and some lean until they nearly touch a neighbour at the top while
their bases stay apart. This is BAD FOOTING — stones everywhere, hardly
anywhere left to put a foot down. But every stone STANDS ON THE FLAT GROUND at
its own base: nothing is stacked on anything else, nothing rests on top of
another stone, and there is NO mound, NO heap, NO pile, NO rubble, NO raised
earth, NO kerb and NO wall. A thicket of upright stones, never a heap of stone.
Vary the heights along the band, but NOT in order — no even row, no staircase
of heights, no regular spacing, and the tallest stone somewhere in the middle
stretch rather than at either end.

STYLE — the same clean stylized cel-shaded look as the game's terrain and unit
art: bold flat shading, clear dark outlines, a mature, slightly muted, limited
color palette (NOT bright saturated, NOT painterly photorealism). Because these
stones sit CLOSE to the camera, in front of the soldiers' feet, draw them a
shade DARKER and SIMPLER than the map version — big plain shapes, little inner
detail, dim in shadow — so they frame the scene rather than competing with the
figures behind them.

COLOR — weathered grey-green stone, hex #8E8A76 (darker than the map version
because it is in shadow), each stone a slightly different flat tone between
#9C9884 and #6E6A5A so the stones separate from one another by tone alone —
this matters most where one stone stands in front of another. Draw the rear
ranks a shade darker and flatter than the front rank so the depth reads. A
little dull grey lichen low on a stone is welcome; NO bright green moss, NO
strong red, blue or green anywhere.

THE BACKGROUND — everything that is not stone is PURE FLAT WHITE, #FFFFFF: NO
texture, NO shading, NO gradient, NO ground, NO grass, NO soil, NO drop shadow
on the white. This white is going to be deleted and the strip laid over the
game's own battle scene, so every outline must be a hard, clean, crisp edge
against it. Nothing in the stones may be white or near-white.

LIGHT — from directly above only, short soft shadows that fall only on the
stones themselves; NOTHING casts a shadow onto the white, and NO light from the
left or the right.

Nothing else: no building, no doorway, no fence, no gate, no flowers, no
candles, no bones, no skulls, no open graves, no creature, no people, no
lettering and no inscriptions. NO glow, NO mist, NO magic. NO border, NO frame
around the picture itself, NO vignette. Landscape 16:9.
