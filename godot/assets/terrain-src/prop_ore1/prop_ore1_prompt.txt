DESIGN — a dwarven mine's ore dump. This block is repeated word for word in
every slot of this skin, so that the pile on the board and the band in the
battle window are plainly the same place.

- The ORE: rough, angular chunks of dark grey-brown rock, hex #5C554D, fist-
  sized to head-sized, with pale dull pewter streaks and specks on the broken
  faces, hex #B8B2A4 — never bright silver, never gold.
- The MINE CART: a squat wooden box on four small iron wheels, weathered dull-
  brown planks, hex #6E5238, bound with dark iron straps, heaped over the brim
  with the same ore.
- The PICK: a miner's pick with a long timber handle in the same brown and a
  dark iron head, always standing with the head UP so it reads at a glance.
- Everything muted, in the game's clean stylized cel-shaded look: bold flat
  shading, clear dark outlines, a limited palette. NO strong red, blue or
  green anywhere. NO gold, NO gems, NO lanterns, NO fire, NO people.

SUBJECT: The ore dump as ONE compact group, as drawn on the world map of a
classic 2D role-playing game: seen from the front and slightly above, standing
upright and facing the bottom of the frame — nothing lying flat, no
perspective.

THE THREE THINGS, all of them large and plainly legible: on the left a heap
of ore; on the right the MINE CART, drawn nearly as tall as the heap and about
as wide, its wheels clearly visible, heaped with ore; and the PICK leaning
against the cart, its head up above the cart's rim and its handle nearly as
long as the cart is tall. The cart and the pick are the things a player
should recognise first; the heap is the ground they sit in.

SIZE — the group sits in the MIDDLE of the picture, wider than it is tall. In
a 1024-pixel picture it is about 660 pixels wide and about 440 pixels tall,
leaving at least 180 pixels of white on every side. It must NOT touch any edge
and must NOT be cut by any edge.

THE BACKGROUND — everything around the group is PURE FLAT WHITE, #FFFFFF,
with NO texture, NO shading, NO gradient, NO ground, NO floor, NO rails, NO
drop shadow. This white is going to be deleted and the game will draw its own
ground there, so the outline of the group must be a hard, clean, crisp edge
against it. Nothing in the group itself may be white or near-white.

LIGHT — soft light from above and slightly in front, short soft shadows that
fall only on the objects themselves; NOTHING casts a shadow onto the white.

Read at thumbnail size: a few large legible shapes, no fine detail. NO
border, NO frame around the picture itself, NO vignette. Square 1:1.
