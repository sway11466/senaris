SUBJECT: A NEW composition, not a crop or extension of any earlier picture:
dug-out ORE lying on the ground, drawn as ONE SELF-CONTAINED GROUP that sits
well inside the frame with wide empty white margins on BOTH sides. The most
important rule of this picture: NOTHING is cut by the frame. Every chunk of
ore, every tool is drawn COMPLETE and WHOLE with white space beyond it; no
object touches the left edge, the right edge or the top edge; nothing
continues off the picture.

The ore is the same kind as the game's map version — rough, angular chunks of
dark rock with pale metallic streaks on the broken faces — SCATTERED LOOSELY
along the ground in the FOREGROUND of a battle scene, seen from the front at
eye level, for a wide landscape picture (16:9).

LAYOUT — in a 1792 x 1024 picture, the ore lies in a LOW STRIP about 220
pixels tall resting on the bottom edge, split into TWO SEPARATE GROUPS with
EMPTY GROUND between them: a LEFT group about 600 pixels wide whose outer end
stops about 80 pixels in from the left edge, and a RIGHT group about 600
pixels wide whose outer end stops about 80 pixels in from the right edge. The
middle of the picture — roughly the central 400 pixels — holds NOTHING but
white. The two groups are different from each other, not mirror copies. The
margins at both ends and everything above the strip stay EMPTY WHITE. The
objects may rest on the bottom edge. No object may rise above the strip.

THE CLUTTER — a low spill of ore chunks, some in small heaps, some lying
alone, a miner's pick dropped flat on the ground, a wooden bucket tipped over
with ore spilling out, an empty gap of bare ground, then more. Vary the
spacing: some pieces touching, some apart, NEVER evenly spaced, NEVER in a row.
Keep everything LOW.

STYLE — the same clean stylized cel-shaded look as the game's terrain and unit
art: bold flat shading, clear dark outlines, a mature, slightly muted, limited
color palette (NOT bright saturated, NOT painterly photorealism). Because this
clutter sits CLOSE to the camera, in front of the soldiers' feet, draw it a
shade DARKER and SIMPLER than the map version — big plain shapes, little inner
detail, dim in shadow — so it frames the scene rather than competing with the
figures behind it.

COLOR — the ore dark grey-brown rock, hex #4F4942 (darker than the map version
because it is in shadow), its metallic streaks a dull pewter, hex #A8A295,
never bright silver and never gold; timber weathered mid-brown, hex #6B4E32;
iron dark and dull. NO strong red, blue or green anywhere.

THE BACKGROUND — everything that is not an object is PURE FLAT WHITE, #FFFFFF:
NO texture, NO shading, NO gradient, NO ground, NO floor, NO drop shadow on the
white. This white is going to be deleted and the strip laid over the game's
own battle scene, so every outline must be a hard, clean, crisp edge against
it. Nothing in the objects may be white or near-white.

LIGHT — from directly above only, short soft shadows that fall only on the
objects themselves; NOTHING casts a shadow onto the white, and NO light from
the left or the right.

Nothing else: no dwarf, no people, no animals, no lantern, no fire, no
lettering, no signs. NO border, NO frame around the picture itself, NO
vignette. Landscape 16:9.
