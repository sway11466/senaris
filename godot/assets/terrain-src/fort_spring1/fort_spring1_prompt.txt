SUBJECT: One stone FOUNTAIN, set in a cave — an octagonal basin of water with a
pillar at its centre carrying two round bowls, water falling from bowl to bowl
and into the basin. Drawn as ONE object standing alone on a flat pure white
background (#FFFFFF) — no ground, no floor, no cave wall, no cast shadow on the
ground, no other objects.

VIEWING ANGLE — seen from the front and moderately above, about 35 degrees down
from the horizon: high enough that the water in the basin shows as a wide
ellipse, low enough that the pillar and its bowls stand clearly above it.

SQUARE TO THE VIEWER — the fountain is set straight in front of the viewer, not
turned: one flat side of the octagonal basin faces the viewer head-on, its near
edge running horizontally across the bottom of the object, and the left and
right halves are mirror images of each other. Do NOT rotate the basin so that a
corner points at the viewer. This is NOT an isometric view and NOT a
three-quarter view.

DESIGN (follow exactly, every count and position):
- One built stone fountain. NOT a natural spring, NOT a well, NOT a water
  trough. NO roof, NO canopy, NO arch, NO columns around it, NO steps, NO
  railing, NO statue of a person or an animal, NO animal-head spout, NO
  gargoyle, NO carved faces.
- THE BASIN: one octagonal basin. Its wall is low — one course of dressed stone,
  its height about one sixth of the basin's width — and is topped by eight flat
  coping slabs, one to each side, with a visible joint at every corner. Still,
  clear water fills the basin to just below the coping.
- THE PAVING: one narrow ring of flat paving slabs laid around the foot of the
  basin wall, level with the ground and about one fifth of the basin's width
  wide. It follows the eight sides of the basin. Nothing else is on it.
- THE PILLAR: one straight octagonal stone pillar rising from the centre of the
  basin, its height about three quarters of the basin's width, plain and
  untapered, with no carving on it other than the shield described below.
- THE TWO BOWLS: one round bowl on the pillar at about two thirds of its height,
  its width about half the basin's width; and one smaller round bowl at the very
  top of the pillar, its width about a quarter of the basin's width. Both are
  shallow, with a plain rounded rim.
- THE WATER: a low rounded dome of water wells up at the centre of the top bowl,
  no higher than the depth of that bowl. Water spills evenly over the rim of the
  top bowl in an unbroken sheet all the way round, falls into the lower bowl,
  and spills from the lower bowl's rim in the same way into the basin. Both
  sheets fall clear of the pillar, well outside its faces. NO tall jet, NO
  spout, NO arcing streams, NO spray, NO foam, NO mist, NO steam, NO droplets in
  the air.
- THE SHIELD: one carved stone shield fixed flat to the FRONT face of the
  pillar, in the clear stretch between the lower bowl and the surface of the
  basin water, facing the viewer square-on. It is about one quarter of the
  basin's width across and a quarter taller than it is wide. It has a plain
  raised stone rim and a FLAT, EMPTY FIELD inside it — NO device, NO charge, NO
  emblem, NO cross, NO animal, NO lettering, NO crest, NO ribbon, NO wreath, and
  the field is not divided. The field is ONE UNIFORM COLOUR — the game repaints
  it to show who holds the fountain — so no pattern of any kind, and its shading
  is flat. Nothing overlaps the shield: no falling water passes in front of it.
- COLOURS: the basin, coping, paving, pillar, bowls and the shield's rim are a
  cool pale grey dressed stone, hex #9B9A93, each piece a slightly different
  flat tone between #ACABA3 and #7E7D76. The field of the shield is bare
  unpainted stone of the same family, a touch lighter than the pillar, hex
  #A8A79F. The water is clear and muted blue-green, base #5E8E8A, deepest under
  the falling sheets #3C5E5C, catch-light #9BC2B9 — NOT bright cyan, NOT
  turquoise, NOT saturated.
- THE GROUND IT WILL SIT ON: the game draws its own cave floor, a warm packed
  dirt of hex #8A7C6A, behind this picture. The stonework must read clearly
  against it by being COOLER and GREYER — do NOT make the stone warm, brown,
  orange, rust, golden or sandy, and do not let it take the colour of that
  floor.

SIZE — the object sits in the MIDDLE of the picture. In a 1024-pixel picture the
basin including its paving is about 620 pixels wide, and the whole fountain from
the paving to the rim of the top bowl is about 700 pixels tall, leaving at least
140 pixels of white on every side. It must NOT touch any edge and must NOT be
cut by any edge.

LIGHT — dim light from above, short soft shadows that fall only on the fountain
itself. NOTHING casts a shadow onto the background. NO glow, NO shine, NO light
of any colour anywhere in this picture, and the water does not light up.

THE BACKGROUND — everything around the fountain is PURE FLAT WHITE, #FFFFFF,
with NO texture, NO shading, NO gradient, NO ground, NO floor, NO cave wall, NO
drop shadow. This white is going to be deleted and the game will draw its own
cave floor there, so the outline of the fountain must be a hard, clean, crisp
edge against it. Nothing in the fountain itself may be white or near-white.

Everything muted, in the same clean stylized cel-shaded look as the game's
terrain and unit art: bold flat shading, clear dark outlines, a mature, slightly
muted, limited color palette (NOT bright saturated, NOT painterly photorealism).

NOTHING ELSE — no people, no creature, no fish, no birds, no coins in the water,
no cup, no ladle, no jar, no bucket, no rope, no torch, no lantern, no candle,
no crystal, no mushroom, no moss, no ivy, no grass, no plants, no flowers, no
bones, no treasure, no stalactites, no cave wall, no path, no banner, no flag,
no lettering, NO magic, NO particles. Read at thumbnail size: a few large
