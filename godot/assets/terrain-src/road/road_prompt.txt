stylized cel-shaded look as the game's terrain and unit art: bold flat shading,
clear dark outlines, a mature, slightly muted, limited color palette (NOT bright

BACKGROUND — IMPORTANT: this is a cut-out element, not a scene. Everything that
is not the road itself is PLAIN PURE WHITE (#FFFFFF), flat and empty, right out
to all four edges. NO grass, NO scenery, NO texture, NO gradient, NO shadow, NO
border, NO frame. Nothing at all on the white.

SUBJECT: A single straight, wide DIRT ROAD running away from the viewer,
straight up the frame. A broad, well-travelled country highway of bare packed
earth, beaten pale and smooth by carts and feet.

Composition, exactly: the road is one straight vertical band, dead centre
left-to-right, crossing the full height of the frame and continuing off BOTH the
top edge and the bottom edge — never stopping, never bending, never leaning,
never curving, no fork, no junction, no crossing, no end. It is identical in
width and character along its whole length, so the top edge and the bottom edge

The road is WIDE: about three fifths of the frame across, leaving a strip of
white of about one fifth of the frame on the left and the same on the right.
Both edges run straight up the frame and are only very slightly irregular, the
way trodden earth meets a verge — small, shallow waver only, never a bulge or a
bite that would eat into the white strips.

Each edge of the road carries a clear dark outline, the same weight of outline
as the rest of the game's map art, so the band reads as a defined shape against
the white.

COLOR: warm pale sandy earth, roughly hex #D8C39A — a light, slightly greyed
tan. Inside the band keep it calm and almost flat, with only faint, soft mottling

Lit dimly and evenly from straight above, with NO cast shadow at all: the road
