DESIGN — a wayside rest hut. This block is repeated word for word in every slot
of this skin (the board object, the two captured colours, the combat band), so
that the small piece on the board and the big building in the battle window are
plainly the same place.

- ONE single-storey hut of WOOD, no more. Walls of upright weathered planks,
  base #8A7355, shaded #5F4D39, lit #A68B68, with one stout squared corner post
  at each of the two front corners. NO stone walling anywhere except the chimney.
- Its ROOF is the biggest shape in the picture: one broad gabled roof of
  weathered wooden shingles, unbroken from ridge to eaves, overhanging the walls
  on all sides. Neutral weathered grey: base tone #6E6D6B, catch-light #9B9995
  on the lit slope, shadow #4D4C4A. Hold those numbers exactly — the game
  recolours this roof to show who holds the hut, and the later pictures in this
  set have to match.
- The roof is ONE flat colour per slope: NO stripes, NO chequer, NO shingle
  picked out light and dark, NO patch of another material, NO moss, and nothing
  lying on it. The only shading allowed is the two slopes differing slightly.
- The front wall stands OPEN: one wide doorway with NO door leaf in it, set in
  the middle of the front wall, reaching from the ground up to just under the
  eaves and wide enough for two people side by side. Inside it is one plain flat
  dark brown #3A3026 and nothing else — an empty shelter, not a furnished room.
  This open front is what says the hut is free for anyone to rest in.
- To the LEFT of the doorway, standing against the front wall: ONE plain timber
  bench — a single board on two short legs, about knee high. Nothing on it.
- To the RIGHT of the doorway, standing against the front wall: ONE stack of
  split firewood, the logs seen end-on, stacked neatly to about waist height. NO
  loose logs lying on the ground, NO axe.
- ONE chimney of rough grey fieldstone #8C8A82 rises through the LEFT slope of
  the roof close to the ridge and stops a little above it. It is stone, not
  shingle, and it keeps its own colour when the roof is repainted. NO smoke, NO
  sparks, NO fire.
- NEVER: a door leaf, a lock, a porch, an upper storey, a loft, a tower, a
  second building, a lean-to, a fence, a wall, a gate, a well, a signboard,
  lettering, a lantern, a cart, barrels, tools, banners, flags, people, animals,
  smoke.
- It must be tellable apart from the game's other single buildings at a glance:
  the town's GUARD POST is grey stone with a low walled yard and a rack of
  spears. This is bare WOOD, it has no yard and no wall at all, and it stands
  open with a bench and a woodpile.

SUBJECT: That rest hut, drawn as a single object that stands upright the way a
piece stands on a game board, cut out and set down on the game's own ground.

VIEWING ANGLE — CRITICAL. The hut is seen from the front and moderately ABOVE,
about 35 degrees down from the horizon: high enough that the ridge and BOTH
SLOPES of the roof are fully in view and read as two sloping surfaces falling
away from the ridge, low enough that the front wall with its open doorway, the
bench and the woodpile all stand clearly. The hut is set straight in front of
the viewer and NOT turned: the gable end faces the viewer as an upright
triangle, square to the picture, and the ridge runs straight away from the
viewer with one roof slope to the left of it and one to the right. NO corner
points at you, and no side wall is seen edge-on. This is NOT an isometric view
and NOT a three-quarter view. The roof must never flatten into a mere band above
the wall — the two slopes are what gives the piece its depth.

THE FOOT — the bottom of the front wall, the legs of the bench and the bottom of
the woodpile all stop on ONE straight level line across the bottom of the
picture. That line is where the game sets the hut on the ground. Draw nothing
below it: no ground, no grass, no paving, no step, no shadow.

SIZE AND SHAPE — about 1.2 times as wide as it is tall, counting the bench and
the woodpile. The hut is the only object in the picture, sits in the middle, and
touches no edge. In a 1024-pixel picture leave at least 120 pixels of white on
every side.

THE BACKGROUND is PURE FLAT WHITE, #FFFFFF, with NO texture, NO shading, NO
gradient, NO speckle, NO grey, NO drop shadow. The white is deleted and the game
draws its own ground under the hut, so the edge against the white must be hard
and clean. Nothing in the hut itself may be white or near-white.

NO LIGHT DIRECTION — no wall has a bright side and a dark side, and nothing casts
a shadow onto anything else: none under the eaves, none from the bench or the
woodpile onto the wall, none on the ground. Shading stays inside a surface.

Everything muted, in the same clean stylized cel-shaded look as the game's
terrain and unit art: bold flat shading, clear dark outlines, a mature, slightly
muted, limited colour palette (NOT bright saturated, NOT painterly
photorealism). Read at thumbnail size: a few large legible shapes, no fine
detail, no picked-out planks and no picked-out shingles. NO border, NO frame
around the picture itself, NO vignette. Square 1:1.
