SUBJECT: A HOLE IN THE GROUND — the mouth of an underground burial vault, broken
open — drawn as ONE object seen from ABOVE at a steep angle, about 50 degrees down
from the horizon (much closer to looking straight down than to looking from the
side). The opening lies FLAT IN THE GROUND and faces the sky. The viewer looks
down into it.

SQUARE TO THE VIEWER — the hole is a rectangle set straight in front of the viewer,
not turned. Its FRONT edge runs horizontally across the picture, parallel to the
bottom of the frame, and its BACK edge runs horizontally too, directly behind it.
The left and right edges are mirror images of each other and lean inward by the same
amount. Do NOT rotate the hole so that a corner points toward the viewer. This is NOT
an isometric view, NOT a three-quarter view and NOT a diamond shape: it is a
head-on, symmetrical view of a rectangular pit.

THIS IS NOT A BUILDING AND NOT A DOORWAY IN A WALL. There is NO mound, NO hill, NO
heap, NO raised earth, NO barrow, NO wall, NO upright door frame, NO jamb stones
standing on end, NO lintel, NO roof, NO tomb standing above the ground. Nothing in
this picture stands up. If any part of it reads as a structure built on top of the
ground, it is wrong.

THE OPENING — a rectangular hole cut down into flat earth, wider than it is deep
from front to back. Because of the steep viewing angle its shape is a wide
rectangle whose height on the page is about four fifths of its width. The inside
is BLACK, hex #16191C, and reads as depth, not as a flat painted panel.

THE KERB — the four edges of the hole are lined with flat slabs of weathered
stone laid INTO the ground, level with the earth around them, framing the mouth
like the rim of a well. The slabs are chipped and uneven, and one or two have
shifted out of line. They lie flat: none of them stands upright.

THE STEPS — worn stone steps drop away from the FRONT edge of the opening and go
down into it. Only the first two or three treads are visible before the darkness
swallows them. The rest of the hole is black.

THE DISTURBED EARTH — a low ring of loose, freshly turned soil is heaped around
the outside of the kerb, thinning out to nothing a short way from the stones, with
a few small broken stones and clods lying in it, scattered irregularly and never in
rows. This ring is what tells the viewer the hole is cut into ground; it is LOW and
FLAT, never a mound, and it must not rise higher than the thickness of the kerb
slabs.

THE PUSHED-ASIDE LID — one large flat stone slab, plainly the lid that once covered
the hole, lies FLAT ON THE GROUND beside the opening, shoved off to one side. Its
edges stay roughly parallel to the edges of the hole — it has slid sideways off the
mouth, not spun around — and only one corner has dropped out of line, riding up onto
the heaped soil. It is clearly the right size and shape to cover the opening. It
lies down: it does NOT lean, and it does NOT stand.

SIZE AND SHAPE — WIDE AND LOW. In a 1024-pixel picture the whole object is about
720 pixels wide and about 340 pixels tall, leaving at least 140 pixels of white on
every side. It must NOT touch any edge and must NOT be cut by any edge. It must be
clearly wider than it is tall.

COLOR — the kerb slabs, the lid and the loose stones are weathered grey-green
stone, hex #8A8676, each piece a slightly different flat tone between #9A9686 and
#6E6A5A so the stones separate from one another by tone alone. The turned soil is a
dull, desaturated brown-grey, base #8A7F6A, with #6B6255 in the hollows and #9C9179
where the light catches it. The game draws its own dry khaki graveyard grass, hex
#B2B28E, behind this picture, and the whole object must sit CLEARLY DARKER and less
colourful than that grass so its outline reads against it. Do NOT make the soil
warm, orange, rust, golden or sandy, and do NOT make it brighter than #9C9179.

LIGHT — dim light from above, short soft shadows that fall only on the object
itself. NOTHING casts a shadow onto the background. NO glow, NO light of any colour
anywhere in this picture.

THE BACKGROUND — everything around the object is PURE FLAT WHITE, #FFFFFF, with NO
texture, NO shading, NO gradient, NO ground, NO grass, NO soil, NO drop shadow. This
white is going to be deleted and the game will draw its own graveyard grass there,
so the outline of the object must be a hard, clean, crisp edge against it. Nothing
in the object may be white or near-white.

Everything muted, in the same clean stylized cel-shaded look as the game's terrain
and unit art: bold flat shading, clear dark outlines, a mature, slightly muted,
limited color palette (NOT bright saturated, NOT painterly photorealism).

NOTHING ELSE — no gravestones, no headstones, no grave markers, no crosses, no
statues, no urns, no coffins, no bones, no skulls, no fence, no gate, no path, no
ladder, no rope, no flowers, no candles, no lanterns, no creature, no people, no
lettering and NO inscriptions of any kind. NO mist, NO magic, NO particles. Read at
thumbnail size: a few large legible shapes, no fine detail. NO border, NO frame
around the picture itself, NO vignette. Square 1:1.
