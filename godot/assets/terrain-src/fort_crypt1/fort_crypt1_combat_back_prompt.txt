SUBJECT: The same BROKEN-OPEN CRYPT as the map piece, seen from FAR AWAY and
almost EDGE-ON — a very thin, very wide BAND of ground that lies behind the
fighting in a battle window. It is not a single object floating in the middle of
the frame; it is a stretch of the graveyard seen from a distance, running off the
LEFT edge of the picture.

VIEWING ANGLE — this is the whole point of the picture. The ground is seen from
the SIDE at a very low, grazing angle, almost level with it. NOTHING is seen from
above. The ground reads as a nearly flat line, and everything on it is squashed
down into that line. This is the opposite of the map piece, which looked down into
the hole; here the hole is far away and nearly edge-on, so its mouth is only a
thin dark slot.

WHAT IS IN IT, from left to right along the strip:
- A long stretch of bare, trodden graveyard earth, low and level.
- A little LEFT of the middle, the mouth of the crypt: a low line of flat kerb
  slabs laid in the ground, and immediately above and behind them a THIN
  HORIZONTAL BLACK SLOT — the opening, seen almost edge-on. The slot is the
  darkest thing in the picture and the widest single feature in it, but it stays
  low: it must never become a tall rectangle or a doorway.
- Beside the mouth, the pushed-aside stone lid lying flat on the ground, seen
  edge-on as a thin pale stone bar with a shadowed side.
- A scattering of small broken stones, clods and low stubs of stone along the
  strip, irregularly placed, never in a row, all of them low.

NOTHING STANDS UP. There is NO mound, NO hill, NO bank, NO raised earth, NO wall,
NO doorway, NO door frame, NO gravestone, NO headstone, NO cross, NO post and NO
building anywhere in this picture. The tallest thing in it is a broken stone stub
no higher than about a fifth of the strip's height.

THE TWO ENDS ARE DIFFERENT — this matters.
- LEFT end: the ground runs straight off the edge of the picture and is cut by it.
  Whatever lies nearest the left edge is cut through by the frame. Nothing tapers,
  nothing ends, nothing is rounded off there.
- RIGHT end: the ground has to finish inside the picture. Finish it honestly: past
  the crypt the earth thins out and the last stones fade away as the ground
  recedes. Do NOT stop it with a raw broken end, and do NOT put a wall, fence,
  hedge, kerb or gate there — there is no boundary anywhere in this picture.

SHAPE AND PLACE IN THE FRAME — the picture is square, and almost all of it is
empty. The strip of ground lies along the very BOTTOM edge, running edge to edge
from left to right. In a 1024-pixel picture the strip is about 130 pixels tall —
roughly one eighth as tall as it is wide — and everything above it is empty white.
The strip touches the left edge, the right edge and the bottom edge.

THE BOTTOM EDGE — the earth and the stones are cut flat by the bottom edge of the
picture. Draw nothing below them: no grass, no soil, no path, no ground, no cast
shadow, no strip of background along the bottom. Soldiers will stand in front of
this and will overlap it.

COLOR — the kerb slabs, the lid and the loose stones are weathered grey-green
stone, hex #8A8676, each piece a slightly different flat tone between #9A9686 and
#6E6A5A. The earth is a dull, desaturated brown-grey, base #8A7F6A, with #6B6255
in the hollows and #9C9179 where the light catches it. The slot of the opening is
BLACK, hex #16191C. Match those numbers: this and the map piece must read as the
same place. Do NOT make the earth warm, orange, rust, golden or sandy, and do NOT
make it brighter than #9C9179.

THE BACKGROUND — everything above the strip is PURE FLAT WHITE, #FFFFFF, with NO
texture, NO shading, NO gradient, NO sky, NO clouds, NO horizon line. This white is
going to be deleted, so the top edge of the strip must be a hard, clean, crisp edge
against it. Nothing in the picture may be white or near-white.

LIGHT — dim light from above. Short soft shadows that fall only on the stones and
the earth themselves. NOTHING casts a shadow onto the white. NO glow, NO light of
any colour anywhere in this picture.

NOTHING ELSE — no people, no animals, no birds, no bones, no skulls, no coffins,
no urns, no flowers, no candles, no lanterns, no banners, no smoke, no fire, no
lettering and no inscriptions. NO mist, NO magic, NO particles.

Keep it in bold flat shapes, in the same clean stylized cel-shaded look as the
game's terrain and unit art, with no fine texture that would fight with the figures
standing in front of it. It will be seen very small and very thin, so a few large
legible shapes only. Square 1:1.
