SUBJECT: Victory art for "Goblin Raid". Everyone stands on the same ground, the same distance from the camera. The halfling is the only small one — his head reaches the soldiers' chest.

Bright daylight, clear blue sky, no sun in the sky. The little town's dirt square, half-timbered houses and a low stone wall behind. The fight is over and won: no enemies, no goblins, no weapons raised in anger.

The young militia captain laughs with his sword lowered — short brown hair, bareheaded, steel plate over a blue tunic, no shield. Beside him the rookie grins with relief — a boyish teenager with a smooth young face, dark hair, leather armor, a short sword at his belt; he is the youngest here, not a grown man. With them a slim, wiry halfling scout with a round young face and a sling — small and light on his feet, lean, not plump — and two archers in steel-blue and muted green.

Around them townsfolk cheer and clap — ordinary villagers, a few children, one carrying a basket. Warm, happy, grateful.

NOT night. No gore.
