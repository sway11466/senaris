SUBJECT: Cover art for "Goblin Raid". The goblins are nearly as tall as the humans — a goblin's head reaches the soldier's shoulder — and everyone stands on the same ground, the same distance from the camera.

Bright daylight, clear blue sky, no sun in the sky. Green farmland outside a small half-timbered town. From the left, a young militia captain charges: short brown hair, bareheaded, steel plate over a blue tunic, one straight sword, no shield. A leaner rookie with dark hair and leather armor runs a step behind him, a short sword in both hands, not a spear.

Five goblins give way to the right across the open field — wiry, olive-green, long pointed ears, crude chipped blades and a bent short bow — with one bigger, broader goblin among them — the same olive-green skin as the rest, just taller and thick with muscle — swinging a two-handed axe.

NOT night. NOT a horde. No gore.
