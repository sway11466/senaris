SUBJECT: Cover art for "Dragon Hunt", using the attached reference sheet for the characters — keep their faces, hair, outfits, weapons and relative sizes exactly as shown.

Late afternoon in high mountains: bare grey rock, a stony trail along a ridge, thin pines, a deep valley below, warm low sunlight.

Six characters climb the path, left to right: the HUNTER lowest on the trail with his crossbow, looking up at the sky; the VANGUARD leading in heavy plate, his greatsword shouldered; the THIEF crouched on a rock ahead of them, pointing; the BISHOP and the WIZARD walking behind; the PIXIE hovering by the vanguard's shoulder. The fairy is tiny — about the size of his forearm.

Far over the valley, small in the sky above a distant volcanic peak, a RED DRAGON wheels — deep red scales, broad membranous wings, no bigger in the frame than a hand. It has not noticed them yet.

NOT night.
