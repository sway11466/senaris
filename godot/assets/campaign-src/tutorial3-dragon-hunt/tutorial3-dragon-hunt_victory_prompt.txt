SUBJECT: Victory art for "Dragon Hunt". Use the attached reference sheet for the characters — same faces, hair, outfits, weapons and relative sizes. All SIX of them must appear, each clearly a different person.

Dawn at a volcanic crater: black rock, cooling lava channels glowing dull orange, thin smoke, the first pale gold light coming over the rim.

The RED DRAGON fills the middle of the picture, ASLEEP — a huge four-legged wyrm with deep red scales curled on the crater floor, wings folded, eyes closed, breathing quietly, its head resting on the stone. It is unharmed and at peace: no blood, no wounds, no broken body. It is sleeping off its rage, not dead.

The six stand small in front of it, quiet and relieved rather than cheering, weapons lowered:
- VANGUARD: a broad, thick-set man in heavy steel-blue plate with big pauldrons and a dark cape, short brown hair, his two-handed sword point-down and both hands resting on the pommel.
- HUNTER: a rugged older man with a short brown beard, fur-trimmed leather gear, his crossbow lowered at his side, looking up at the sleeping head.
- THIEF: a young blonde WOMAN with a chin-length bob and a headband, dark sleeveless top and shorts, sitting down on a rock with a tired grin.
- BISHOP: a stout OLDER man, bald on top, in white and gold vestments over steel-blue, both hands on his cross-topped scepter, head bowed toward the dragon as if saying goodnight. He must be in the picture.
- WIZARD: a young clean-shaven man in a tall pointed blue-grey hat and long blue-grey robe, his crystal staff dark and quiet, wiping his brow.
- PIXIE: a tiny fairy girl with a pale blonde bob and translucent insect wings, hovering close to the dragon's nose, peering at it.

Warm, calm, grateful. NOT a triumph over a corpse. No gore.
