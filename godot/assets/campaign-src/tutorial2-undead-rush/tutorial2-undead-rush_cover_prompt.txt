SUBJECT: Cover art for "Undead Rush". The undead are the same height as the humans, and everyone stands on the same ground, the same distance from the camera.

Night in an old town graveyard: crooked headstones, a bare dead tree, a rusty iron fence, pale moonlight, low fog. Three heroes hold the line together, shoulder to shoulder, facing the viewer's right.

A young knight braces a large kite shield forward — full steel-blue plate, big pauldrons, bareheaded, a sword in his other hand. A priest prays beside him — a man in layered steel-blue and off-white vestments with gold trim, bareheaded, holding up a metal rod topped with a bold cross, a soft warm light gathering around him. A witch levels a long staff past them — a young woman with semi-long brown hair under a wide-brimmed dark purple hat, black bodice and short black skirt, the staff's crystal glowing cold blue.

Eight undead press in from the right: rotting grey-green corpses in tattered rags and pale bare skeletons with rusty blades, some climbing out of open graves. Far behind them, dim in the purple haze, a tall hooded figure with a staff watches — only a silhouette with cold glowing eyes.
