SUBJECT: A glowing magic SIGIL carved into the stone floor of an ancient ruin,
as drawn on the world map of a classic 2D role-playing game. The floor itself
is NOT drawn — only the sigil's lines are drawn, lying flat on the ground and
seen from the front and slightly above, so the round sigil appears as a
horizontal ELLIPSE: about 4/5 as tall as it is wide.

THE SIGIL — ONE bold outer ring. Inside the ring, ONE large jagged LIGHTNING
BOLT runs from the top of the ring to the bottom, clearly readable as
lightning. Around the bolt, a few simple alchemical marks: short straight
strokes and small crescents set just inside the ring. All lines are THICK and
bold so the shape reads at thumbnail size; no fine detail, no tiny lettering.
Everything is foreshortened the same way as the ellipse — it lies on the
floor.

SIZE — the sigil sits in the MIDDLE of the picture. In a 1024-pixel picture,
the ellipse is about 600 pixels wide and 480 pixels tall, leaving at least 200
pixels of white on every side. It must NOT touch any edge and must NOT be cut
by any edge.

COLOR — the lines glow a bright warm RED, around #E0443A, with a lighter,
brighter core along the middle of each line, so the sigil reads as active and
dangerous. The glow stays INSIDE the lines: NO halo, NO glow, NO light spills
onto the white around them. Same clean stylized cel-shaded look as the game's
terrain and unit art: bold flat shading, crisp edges, a mature, slightly
muted, limited color palette. NO blue, NO yellow, NO white lightning.

THE BACKGROUND — everything around the lines, and inside the ring between the
lines, is PURE FLAT WHITE, #FFFFFF, with NO texture, NO shading, NO gradient,
NO floor, NO stone, NO drop shadow. This white is going to be deleted and the
game will draw its own floor there, so every line must have a hard, clean,
crisp edge against it.

Nothing else: no stone slab, no pedestal, no pillar, no creature, no people,
no sparks flying, no lettering, no signs. NO border, NO frame around the
picture itself, NO vignette. Square 1:1.
