REFERENCE IMAGE: thunder_sigil_found_03_master.png (the sigil as it appears on
the game board). Use it for the sigil's design and color only.

SUBJECT: A magic trap goes off. On the stone floor of a dark ancient ruin, the
red SIGIL from the reference image blazes, and ONE huge branching bolt of
blue-white LIGHTNING crashes straight down onto its center.

THE SIGIL — the same design as the reference: one bold ring with a jagged
lightning-bolt mark inside and a few simple alchemical marks, glowing warm
red, around #E0443A. It is carved into the floor and lies flat, seen from a
low camera close to the ground, so it appears as a wide ellipse in the lower
half of the picture.

THE LIGHTNING — one thick main bolt with a few thinner branches, blue-white,
running from the top edge of the picture down to the middle of the sigil.
Where it hits, a burst of sparks and small chips of stone fly up. The bolt is
the brightest thing in the picture; its cold light falls across the floor and
mixes with the red glow of the sigil.

THE PLACE — worn stone floor tiles and, behind, broken pillars and walls
fading into darkness. Kept simple and dark so the sigil and the bolt stand
out.

NO people, NO creatures, NO characters of any kind, NO weapons, NO footprints.
Only the sigil, the lightning and the ruin.
