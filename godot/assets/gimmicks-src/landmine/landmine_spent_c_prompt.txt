SUBJECT: The scattered FRAGMENTS of an exploded mine, lying on the stone floor
of an ancient ruin, as drawn on the world map of a classic 2D role-playing
game. The floor itself is NOT drawn — only the fragments are drawn, lying on
the ground and seen from the front and slightly above. The pieces are spread
over a roughly round area that appears as a horizontal ELLIPSE about 4/5 as
tall as it is wide.

THE FRAGMENTS — the round metal shell of the mine has burst into about fifteen
to twenty-five pieces thrown outward from an empty center: curved, jagged
SHARDS of thick dark iron casing, some showing a row of rivets, plus a few
small bent strips and bolts. Pieces are of different sizes, larger ones near
the center and smaller ones toward the edge, with plenty of pure white
between them. The center is EMPTY white — no hole, no pit, no crater, no dark
patch, no soot stain. Nothing stands up tall: no pile, no smoke, no fire, no
embers, no sparks. Bold, simple shapes that read at thumbnail size.

SIZE — the fragments sit in the MIDDLE of the picture. In a 1024-pixel
picture, the area the pieces reach is about 600 pixels wide and 480 pixels
tall, leaving at least 200 pixels of white on every side. No piece may touch
any edge or be cut by any edge.

COLOR — dark iron, around #3A3836, with a slightly lighter edge where the
metal broke, around #6A6560, and a few dull bronze rivets, around #9C7A4A.
Same clean stylized cel-shaded look as the game's terrain and unit art: bold
flat shading, clear dark outlines, a mature, slightly muted, limited color
palette. NO white or very light grey on the pieces, NO orange, NO red, NO
glow.

THE BACKGROUND — everything around and between the pieces is PURE FLAT WHITE,
#FFFFFF, with NO texture, NO shading, NO gradient, NO floor, NO drop shadow.
This white is going to be deleted and the game will draw its own floor there,
so every piece must have a hard, clean, crisp edge against it.

Nothing else: no whole bomb, no sign, no creature, no people, no lettering. NO
border, NO frame around the picture itself, NO vignette. Square 1:1.
