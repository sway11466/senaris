SUBJECT: A SCORCH MARK left on the stone floor of an ancient ruin by a small
explosion, as drawn on the world map of a classic 2D role-playing game. The
floor itself is NOT drawn — only the mark is drawn, lying flat on the ground
and seen from the front and slightly above, so the roughly round mark appears
as a horizontal ELLIPSE: about 4/5 as tall as it is wide.

THE MARK — ONE blackened burn patch: a dark charred center, sooty black-brown,
fading out to a ragged, splotchy edge. A few short CRACKS radiate outward from
the center like a star, thick enough to read at thumbnail size. It is flat on
the ground — nothing stands up: no crater rim, no rubble, no stones, no smoke,
no fire, no embers.

SIZE — the mark sits in the MIDDLE of the picture. In a 1024-pixel picture,
the ellipse is about 600 pixels wide and 480 pixels tall, leaving at least 200
pixels of white on every side. It must NOT touch any edge and must NOT be cut
by any edge.

COLOR — dark charcoal black and sooty brown, around #2B2622 at the center and
#5A4A3E toward the edge. Same clean stylized cel-shaded look as the game's
terrain and unit art: bold flat shading, clear shapes, a mature, slightly
muted, limited color palette. NO white or light grey anywhere inside the mark,
NO orange, NO red, NO glow.

THE BACKGROUND — everything around the mark is PURE FLAT WHITE, #FFFFFF, with
NO texture, NO shading, NO gradient, NO floor, NO stone, NO drop shadow. This
white is going to be deleted and the game will draw its own floor there, so
the mark must have a clear edge against it.

Nothing else: no bomb, no sign, no creature, no people, no lettering. NO
border, NO frame around the picture itself, NO vignette. Square 1:1.
