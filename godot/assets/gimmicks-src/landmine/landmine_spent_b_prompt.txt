SUBJECT: A BLAST MARK left on the stone floor of an ancient ruin by a small
explosion, as drawn on the world map of a classic 2D role-playing game. The
floor itself is NOT drawn — only the soot is drawn, lying flat on the ground
and seen from the front and slightly above, so the round mark appears as a
horizontal ELLIPSE: about 4/5 as tall as it is wide — only slightly squashed,
NOT a thin flat oval.

THE MARK — a STARBURST of SOOT STREAKS: about ten to fourteen dark streaks
shoot straight outward from the center in every direction, like the spokes of
a splash, each one thick near the center and tapering to a ragged point at
the outside. Between the streaks there are wide GAPS of pure white, all the
way in to the center. The center is NOT a solid dark blob: only a small
scattered speckle of soot sits there, with white showing through it. It must
read as soot sprayed over a flat floor, NOT as a hole, NOT as a pit, NOT as
a crater. Nothing stands up: no rim, no rubble, no stones, no smoke, no fire,
no embers. Bold, simple shapes that read at thumbnail size.

SIZE — the mark sits in the MIDDLE of the picture. In a 1024-pixel picture,
the ellipse the streaks reach is about 600 pixels wide and 480 pixels tall,
leaving at least 200 pixels of white on every side. It must NOT touch any
edge and must NOT be cut by any edge.

COLOR — dark charcoal black and sooty brown, around #2B2622 to #5A4A3E. Flat,
with no gradient inside a streak. Same clean stylized cel-shaded look as the
game's terrain and unit art: bold flat shapes, a mature, slightly muted,
limited color palette. NO white or light grey inside the streaks, NO orange,
NO red, NO glow.

THE BACKGROUND — everything around and between the streaks is PURE FLAT
WHITE, #FFFFFF, with NO texture, NO shading, NO gradient, NO floor, NO stone,
NO drop shadow. This white is going to be deleted and the game will draw its
own floor there, so every streak must have a clear edge against it.

Nothing else: no bomb, no sign, no creature, no people, no lettering. NO
border, NO frame around the picture itself, NO vignette. Square 1:1.
