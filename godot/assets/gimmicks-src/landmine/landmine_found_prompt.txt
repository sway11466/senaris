SUBJECT: A WARNING SIGN painted flat on the floor, as drawn on the world map of
a classic 2D role-playing game. The floor itself is NOT drawn — only the sign
is drawn, lying flat on the ground and seen from the front and slightly above,
so it is squashed vertically: about 4/5 as tall as it would be seen straight
on.

THE SIGN — ONE equilateral TRIANGLE pointing up, filled with flat bright
safety YELLOW, around #F2C200, with a thick solid BLACK border. In the middle
of the triangle, ONE simple black BOMB icon: a round bomb with a short fuse
and a small spark at the fuse tip. It looks like a modern road hazard sign or
an interface warning icon — a clean flat graphic symbol, deliberately NOT
fantasy, NOT hand-painted, NOT carved, NOT old, NOT weathered. All shapes are
bold and simple so it reads at thumbnail size. Everything is foreshortened the
same way — it lies on the floor.

SIZE — the sign sits in the MIDDLE of the picture. In a 1024-pixel picture,
the triangle is about 600 pixels wide and 420 pixels tall, leaving at least
200 pixels of white on every side. It must NOT touch any edge and must NOT be
cut by any edge.

COLOR — only flat safety yellow and solid black. No gradients, no shading, no
texture, no glow, no highlights.

THE BACKGROUND — everything around the sign is PURE FLAT WHITE, #FFFFFF, with
NO texture, NO shading, NO gradient, NO floor, NO drop shadow. This white is
going to be deleted and the game will draw its own floor there, so the sign
must have a hard, clean, crisp edge against it.

Nothing else: no post, no stand, no flag, no lettering, no exclamation mark,
no second sign. NO border, NO frame around the picture itself, NO vignette.
Square 1:1.
