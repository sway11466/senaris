SUBJECT: A buried LANDMINE goes off. On the stone floor of a dark ancient
ruin, an EXPLOSION bursts up out of the ground, seen from a low camera close
to the floor.

THE EXPLOSION — one big blast in the middle of the picture, rising from the
floor: a bright yellow-white core at the bottom, a billowing orange fireball
above it, and dark smoke curling at its edges. The floor tiles around it are
blown apart, with broken slabs of stone and jagged shards of the mine's iron
casing flying outward in all directions.

THE PLACE — worn stone floor tiles and, behind, broken pillars and walls
fading into darkness. Kept simple and dark so the blast stands out; its warm
light falls across the floor and the nearest pillars.

NO people, NO creatures, NO characters of any kind, NO weapons, NO warning
sign, NO lightning. Only the explosion, the flying debris and the ruin.
