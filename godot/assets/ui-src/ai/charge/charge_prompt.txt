SUBJECT: A charge mark — ONE spearhead pointing to the LEFT, level and horizontal (no upward or downward tilt), seen flat from the side and mirrored top-and-bottom about that horizontal axis. A broad leaf-shaped blade, thick and full-bellied, narrowing to a sharp point at the left; behind it a short socket and only a stub of shaft — NOT a long thin spear. From that socket, above and below, spread two SHORT bat-like membranes: leathery skin stretched between two or three straight ribs, with a small hooked claw at the outer tip, held folded close to the shaft. They are bat wings, NEVER feathered bird wings, and they stay clearly smaller and shorter than the blade — they break up the outline, they do not carry the mark. The blade itself is whole and sharp: NOT chipped, NOT rusted, NOT rotting; the menace comes from the heavy blunt shape and the membranes, not from decay. DRAW IT BIG: the mark spans the full width of the square canvas, its tip and its shaft end touching or nearly touching the left and right edges, and the membranes reaching close to the top and bottom edges. Leave only a thin margin, a few percent of the canvas — do NOT float a small mark in a large empty background. Blunt, heavy and driving forward, an emblem of pushing ahead without hesitation. Warm amber-gold, one light tone with at most one slightly darker tone of the same amber inside the blade and the membranes, hard flat edges — a branding-iron mark, NOT a rendered metal spear, NO steel sheen, NO glow. No hand, no banner, no ground. Pure black background (single flat color, for easy cutout).
