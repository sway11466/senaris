A single wizard's wooden staff, drawn as one SOLID BLACK SILHOUETTE on a plain pure-white
background. A filled shape only — no outline, no contour line, no grey, no gradient, no
shading, no highlight, no texture, no drop shadow, no background scenery, no ground, no
frame, no lettering of any kind.

The staff stands VERTICAL: tip pointing straight down, head at the top. Its overall axis is
upright — do not tilt or rotate the whole staff.

THE HEAD: the top quarter of the staff curls into an open SPIRAL CROOK, like a shepherd's
crook, bending to the left and curling inward on itself, ending in a small rounded tip
inside the curl. The opening inside the curl is a white gap and must stay clearly open and
generous — never so tight that it fills in and becomes a solid knob.

THE SHAFT: long and SLENDER, a piece of natural wood. Its sides are not ruler-straight:
they waver very gently and carry two or three subtle knots or bumps, but the waviness stays
small — the shaft must still read as one slim clean line, never lumpy or crooked. It tapers
gradually toward the bottom and ends in a blunt worn point.

No ornaments of any kind: no ribbons, no leaves, no jewels, no wrappings, no runes.

Everything is one connected silhouette. The only white inside the staff is the opening of
the spiral crook at the top. Keep that opening large enough to stay open and not fill in.
