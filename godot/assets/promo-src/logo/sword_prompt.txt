A single sword, drawn as one SOLID BLACK SILHOUETTE on a plain pure-white background.
A filled shape only — no outline, no contour line, no grey, no gradient, no shading, no
highlight, no texture, no drop shadow, no background scenery, no ground, no frame, no
lettering of any kind.

The sword stands PERFECTLY VERTICAL and upright: point straight down, pommel straight up.
Do not tilt, rotate or lean it. It is seen flat from the front, face-on to the blade, so it
is exactly left-right symmetrical — the same shape on both sides of its centre line.

Proportions: the blade takes roughly two thirds of the total height, the hilt the remaining
third. The whole weapon is SLENDER and elegant rather than broad and heavy.

THE BLADE: straight and double-edged, narrow, with a long gradual taper that runs most of
its length and finishes in a sharp needle-like point. The edges are clean straight lines,
not curved.

Down the centre of the blade runs a FULLER — a single very narrow white groove, drawn as a
thin white gap through the black silhouette, following the centre line. It starts just below
the crossguard and runs almost the whole way to the point, narrowing with the blade and
closing before the tip. It is a hairline: thin enough to read as an engraved channel, never
so wide that the blade looks split into two. No other engraving and no lettering on the
blade.

THE CROSSGUARD: slim and narrow — only a little wider than the grip is long. Its two arms
sweep gently DOWNWARD toward the blade, like a shallow bow, and each arm ends in a small
round knob. Nothing sits at its centre: no ring, no jewel, no boss — the blade simply passes
into the guard.

THE GRIP: bound in wrapping, shown as five or six narrow horizontal white gaps that cross
the grip evenly from side to side, leaving black bands between them. The grip is a simple
straight column, slightly narrower than the crossguard is thick.

THE POMMEL: an OPEN RING at the very top — a circle with a white hole through its middle,
like a finger ring, joined to the bottom of the grip.

Everything is one connected silhouette. The only white inside the sword is the fuller down
the blade, the hole in the pommel ring, and the narrow gaps of the grip wrapping. Keep those
openings large enough to stay open and not fill in.
