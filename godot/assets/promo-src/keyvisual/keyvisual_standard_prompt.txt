SUBJECT: A key-art illustration for the game's store page, 4:3.

CHARACTERS — five reference sheets are attached. They are, in this order:
  sheet 1 = FIGHTER, sheet 2 = ELF, sheet 3 = WIZARD, sheet 4 = THIEF,
  sheet 5 = the RED DRAGON.
Reproduce these exact designs: same faces, same hair, same armor and clothing
colors, same weapons, same dragon anatomy and coloring. Do not redesign them, do
not age them up, do not restyle them. Each character appears exactly ONCE in the
picture — never draw the same character twice.

The ELF and the THIEF are DIFFERENT people and must not be confused. They are
both blonde, so keep every one of these apart:
  - ELF (sheet 2): tall slim woman, POINTED EARS, long blonde hair in a side
    braid, a dark GREEN hooded cloak, a QUIVER OF ARROWS on her back, a pale
    blue-grey long-sleeved tunic with a belt, brown boots, and a longbow. Fully
    covered — no bare midriff.
  - THIEF (sheet 4): short blonde bob held by a black headband, ROUND ears, a
    blue short-sleeved shirt over a black crop top with a BARE MIDRIFF, denim
    shorts, a black glove on one hand, pouches on her belt, and a small DAGGER.
    She has NO cloak, NO quiver and NO bow.

SCENE: dusk at a volcanic rift. THE GROUND IS SPLIT IN TWO: a clifftop on the
left, a clifftop on the right, and between them a deep open CHASM — empty air
falling away to glowing lava far below. The two clifftops do not touch. A huge
red dragon is FLYING in the gap between them, hovering over the chasm with
nothing at all beneath it. The party of four has it SURROUNDED — two heroes on
the left clifftop, two on the right clifftop, closing in from both rims. The
dragon cannot face both sides at once. It has NOT breathed yet.

TWO RULES THAT OVERRIDE EVERYTHING ELSE IN THIS PROMPT:

  (a) THE DRAGON IS IN THE AIR. Its legs are tucked up under its body, its wings
      are raised and beating, and the dark chasm is visible BELOW it — between
      its body and the ground. Do NOT draw rock, ground, ledge, pillar, rubble or
      debris under its feet. It must not stand, perch, crouch, sit or rest on
      anything. Nothing touches it.

  (b) THE MIDDLE OF THE PICTURE IS OPEN AIR OVER A CHASM. The rock is broken into
      two separate clifftops, one at the left and one at the right, with a clear
      empty gap between them. The gap runs from the bottom edge of the picture up
      to the horizon: dark in its depths, lit by orange lava far down. Do NOT
      close the gap, do NOT bridge it, and do NOT draw a continuous floor across
      the bottom of the picture.

LAYOUT — this describes WHERE things sit in the frame. It is placement only: do
NOT draw any dividing line, seam, edge, wedge, shaft or beam across the picture.
The sky is one smooth, even gradient with no hard edges anywhere.

TOP MARGIN — keep the top 22% of the picture as open sky. Nothing may enter it:
no wingtip, no horn, no tail, no smoke, no cloud, no rock, no character, not even
a wisp. Draw the dragon LOW enough and SMALL enough that its wingtips stay well
clear of it. This is a placement rule only — there is no line, no edge, no band
and no boundary to draw anywhere. The sky must read as ONE continuous gradient
from the very top of the picture down to the horizon, with nothing at all marking
where this margin ends.

SAFE MARGINS: the picture will be trimmed slightly on the LEFT and RIGHT edges.
Keep every character, every weapon and every important shape at least 5% of the
width away from the left and right edges. Only sky, distant mountains and ground
may run to those edges.

CAMERA: a level view from the side, at about the height of the heroes' heads. We
are watching the encirclement from outside, not from above and not from below.
Nobody looks at the camera.
