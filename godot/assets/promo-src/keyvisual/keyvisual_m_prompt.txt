SUBJECT: A key-art illustration for the game's store page, wide 16:9.

CHARACTERS — five reference sheets are attached. They are, in this order:
  sheet 1 = FIGHTER, sheet 2 = ELF, sheet 3 = WIZARD, sheet 4 = THIEF,
  sheet 5 = the RED DRAGON.
Reproduce these exact designs: same faces, same hair, same armor and clothing
colors, same weapons, same dragon anatomy and coloring. Do not redesign them, do
not age them up, do not restyle them. Each character appears exactly ONCE in the
picture — never draw the same character twice.

The ELF and the THIEF are DIFFERENT people and must not be confused. They are
both blonde, so keep every one of these apart:
  - ELF (sheet 2): tall slim woman, POINTED EARS, long blonde hair in a side
    braid, a dark GREEN hooded cloak, a QUIVER OF ARROWS on her back, a pale
    blue-grey long-sleeved tunic with a belt, brown boots, and a longbow. Fully
    covered — no bare midriff.
  - THIEF (sheet 4): short blonde bob held by a black headband, ROUND ears, a
    blue short-sleeved shirt over a black crop top with a BARE MIDRIFF, denim
    shorts, a black glove on one hand, pouches on her belt, and a small DAGGER.
    She has NO cloak, NO quiver and NO bow.

SCENE: dusk on a volcanic ridge. A huge red dragon dives at a party of four from
the upper right. The dragon has NOT breathed yet.

LAYOUT — this describes WHERE things sit in the frame. It is placement only: do
NOT draw any dividing line, seam, edge, band, wedge, shaft or beam across the
picture. The sky is one smooth, even gradient with no hard edges anywhere.

RESERVED AREA — the single most important rule of this layout. The rectangle
covering the LEFT 40% OF THE WIDTH and the TOP HALF OF THE HEIGHT is reserved
and must contain NOTHING but plain empty sky. No smoke, no smoke column, no
lightning, no arrow, no wing, no rock, no mountain, no ember, no bright cloud, no
character, no part of any character. Not even a wisp. Everything in the picture
is arranged around this empty rectangle: if an element would reach into it, move
that element down or to the right until it is clear of it. Treat it as a solid
block of sky that the composition is built around.

1. UPPER LEFT — the reserved area above. Deep slate-blue dusk sky, flat and
   almost featureless, blending softly with no visible boundaries. Any volcanic
   smoke belongs on the RIGHT side of the picture, low and behind the dragon —
   never on the left.

2. UPPER RIGHT — the red dragon, very large, filling the right side of the frame.
   Wings spread wide, body angled head-down as it descends toward the lower left,
   claws forward. Its jaws are open and a deep ember-red glow is building in its
   throat — that glow is the only fire in the picture. There is NO stream of
   flame, NO fireball, NO burning breath anywhere.

3. LOWER LEFT — the four heroes on black volcanic rock, small against the dragon
   so the size difference reads at a glance. They stand low in the frame: the top
   of the tallest hero's head, and the tip of every raised weapon, stays BELOW the
   halfway line of the picture.
   - FIGHTER: front-most, nearest the dragon, feet planted, braced low, sword
     raised across his body to shield the others behind him. He is NOT attacking
     the dragon.
   - ELF: just behind the fighter, bow drawn and loosed upward to the right at the
     dragon. Her arrow is an ordinary arrow — a plain dark wooden shaft with grey
     feather fletching and a steel head, with NO glow, NO light trail and NO
     sparks. It flies on a low, shallow line toward the dragon's chest.
   - WIZARD: behind and left of the elf, staff raised in both hands. From the
     crystal on his staff a bolt of pale blue-white lightning leaps out — a
     jagged, branching arc of electricity, clearly magic and clearly NOT an
     arrow. It leaves the staff heading RIGHT toward the dragon's head, climbing
     above the arrow's path. The bolt travels only to the right — it never
     reaches up or back to the left, and never enters the reserved area.
   - THIEF: at the far left edge of the frame, crouched low behind a rock with her
     daggers ready, watching the sky. She is NOT attacking.
   The arrow and the lightning are two separate things travelling on two clearly
   separated paths. They never touch, cross, overlap or merge into one streak,
   and neither one turns into the other.

4. FOREGROUND — cracked black volcanic rock with thin veins of glowing orange
   lava and low smoke.

LIGHT: the scene is dark, cool and dim overall. The only warm light is the
dragon's throat glow and the lava veins underfoot. The wizard's lightning is the
one cool bright accent in the sky; the arrow stays dark and unlit.

FORBIDDEN: no barrier, no dome, no shield bubble, no protective magic circle or
wall of any kind — nothing is blocking the dragon. No straight-edged shadow,
wedge or light shaft cutting across the sky or across the dragon. Nothing at all
in the reserved upper-left rectangle. No text, no title, no logo.
