SUBJECT: A bomb blast — the moment a dropped iron bomb bursts on its target, drawn as a flat graphic stamp in the cartoon "explosion" style: a ring of big, chunky, jagged flame lobes bursting outward from a hollow center, like a comic-book explosion outline. The lobes are bold rounded-and-pointed tongues of uneven size, packed all the way around the circle, each pushing straight outward; the ragged outer silhouette is roughly circular. Keep the lobes THICK and chunky, not hairline, so the burst still reads when the whole graphic is shrunk very small. The middle of the burst is EMPTY: plain background color showing through, NO white flash disc, NO filled core, NO starburst in the center — the ring of flame lobes frames a hollow hole. Muted orange flame with a darker brick-red inner edge, two or three tones only, hard flat edges — NOT bright neon yellow. This is a flat graphic stamp, NOT a soft glow, NOT a lens flare, NOT smoke clouds, NOT debris, shrapnel or particles. The burst fills the frame with only a thin margin. No bomb, no machine, no ground. Pure black background (single flat color, for easy cutout).
