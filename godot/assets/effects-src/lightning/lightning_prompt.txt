SUBJECT: A BRANCHING LIGHTNING BOLT striking straight DOWN from the top of the
frame to the bottom, like lightning hitting a target from the sky. ONE main
jagged trunk runs down the vertical center with sharp zigzag turns, and many

and right. The main trunk is thick and solid; the branches get thinner as
they split, but every stroke stays a bold, clear graphic shape, never a
hairline. At the bottom tip of the trunk, a small sharp starburst flash where
it strikes. The bolt fills the frame from top to bottom.
Colors: a pale blue-white core, around #E8F6FF, inside a bright sky-blue
edge, around #6FBFFF, outlined with a thin deeper blue line, around #2F6FC0.
