SUBJECT: A shriek mark — the air itself CRACKING under a magical scream, drawn as jagged lightning-shaped fissures. Draw six to eight sharp zigzag cracks, each one a bold angular bolt with hard straight segments and abrupt kinks (lightning, NOT smooth curves, NOT wavy lines, NOT spirals). They all spring from a single point at the LEFT-CENTRE of the frame and tear OUTWARD, fanning UP and DOWN as they travel to the RIGHT: the topmost bolts rake up toward the top-right corner, the bottom ones rake down toward the bottom-right corner, and the whole fan widens the further right it goes, so the burst clearly opens to the RIGHT. Each crack is thick and solid where it starts and splits into thinner forked branches as it spreads, with a few short splinter shards flung off the forks. The CENTRE of the fan is EMPTY — leave a wide open gap between the upper and lower bolts, with nothing drawn in it, so the unit underneath stays visible; the cracks must never cross the middle or close into a ring. Three tones only: a pale bone-white core down the middle of each bolt, a dull violet body around it, and a dark plum outline — flat hard-edged cut-out strokes, NOT a glow, NOT smoke, NOT particles, NOT a gradient, NOT semi-transparent. Keep it a clean graphic stamp with a strong readable silhouette even when shrunk. NO mouth, NO face, NO creature, NO hands, NO musical notes, NO sound rings, NO concentric circles, NO text, NO ground. The fan of cracks fills the frame with only a thin margin. Pure black background (single flat color, for easy cutout).
