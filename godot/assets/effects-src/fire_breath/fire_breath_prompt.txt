SUBJECT: A DRAGON'S FIRE BREATH — a huge, violent, roaring torrent of flame blasting down diagonally, as if a dragon in the air at the upper left were unloading its whole breath onto a target below to the right. Composition: the narrow throat of the jet is SHORT and stays in the TOP-LEFT CORNER, taking up no more than a fifth of the frame; almost immediately it ERUPTS into an enormous fan of fire that fills the rest of the frame, spreading very wide — the outermost tongues reach the right edge, the bottom edge, and curl back up toward the top right. This is a wall of fire, not a streak. Build the torrent from many overlapping flame tongues at DIFFERENT angles and lengths: the core tongues drive down-right, the outer ones peel away and curl back on themselves like breaking waves, and a few detached licks and torn-off flame fragments fly ahead of the front edge. Layer them so nearer tongues overlap and partly hide the ones behind, giving real depth. Leave CLEAR EMPTY GAPS OF BACKGROUND between the tongues, so the burned figure stays visible through the fire. Colors: hot white-yellow at the throat and along the spines of the biggest tongues, fading out through bright golden-orange, then a strong orange-red, to a deep blood-red at the outer edges and tips — five or six distinct flat tones with hard, clean, crisp edges (stepped like layered cel painting, NOT airbrushed, NO soft gradients, NO glow, NO bloom, NO haze). Bold, chunky, powerful flame shapes, NOT wispy, NOT feathery, NOT a ball. NO dragon, NO head, NO mouth, NO teeth, NO wings, NO target, NO ground, NO smoke. The torrent fills the frame corner to corner with only a thin margin. Pure black background (single flat color, for easy cutout).
