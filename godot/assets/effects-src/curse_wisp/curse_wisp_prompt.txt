SUBJECT: A creeping curse — a loose flight of small WAILING SPIRIT WISPS swirling around the victim, as if a few lost souls had risen off the grave and closed in on them. Each wisp is a slender teardrop of cold flame, rounded at its head and tapering to a fine trailing tail, and inside the rounded head a faint hollow-eyed face is just suggested — two small dark eye pits and a small open dark mouth, drawn as simple flat holes, no skull, no bone, no jaw, no teeth. The faces are barely there: soft, sunken and mournful, the kind you half-see and doubt. Five or six wisps only, small and thin, scattered around the edge of the frame at different sizes and angles with their tails streaming outward and their faces turned inward toward the centre. They do not touch and are NOT evenly spaced or arranged in a ring pattern, a flower or a spiral — the scatter is irregular. A few tiny flecks of the same flame drift in the gaps.

CRITICAL — KEEP IT FAINT AND SPARSE. This is the weakest attack in the faction, not a boss spell: the wisps are small, thin and widely separated, and most of the frame is empty background. Do NOT mass them into a dense blazing swarm, a torrent, a wall of fire or a thick plume; do NOT let them overlap into one solid shape; do NOT make the faces large, screaming, snarling or detailed.

CRITICAL — THE CENTRE IS EMPTY: the middle of the frame is completely open background, a wide clear hole with the wisps only around its edge, so the figure underneath stays fully visible. NOTHING is drawn in the centre — no skull, no face, no rune, no sigil, no filled shape, no haze across the middle.

Same faction palette and the same flat cut-out treatment as the game's curse mark: a pale sickly green core with a mid-tone teal shadow and a darker teal outline, three tones only, hard flat edges. Cold blue-green only — NOT purple, NOT magenta, NOT pink, NOT orange. NOT a soft glow, NOT smoke, NOT a gradient, NOT particles or sparkles, NO bloom spilling off the shapes — every wisp is a solid flat shape with a crisp edge.

It must read as a curse rather than a cut or a blow: NO blade arc, NO slash line, NO impact star, NO shockwave rings, NO cracks. It must also not be mistaken for the necromancer's curse mark — that one is a single large solid skull, this is a scatter of small faint soul-flames with no skull anywhere in it.

No character, no hands, no weapon, no ground, no text. Pure black background (single flat color, for easy cutout).
