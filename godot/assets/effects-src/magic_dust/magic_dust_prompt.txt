SUBJECT: ONE burst of magic dust in flight, flying to the RIGHT — the sparkling powder a fairy flicks off the tip of a small wand. Do NOT draw it as a soft powder or a haze: draw it as a scattered swarm of about twenty-five DISCRETE little shapes — small filled circles and simple four-pointed stars in a few different sizes, each one a hard-edged flat cut-out shape with a clean dark outline, big enough to still read when shrunk. Two tones only: pale ice-blue for the shapes and a brighter near-white core on the largest few. The swarm is arranged as a comet: densest and largest at the RIGHT-hand leading edge where it strikes, thinning out and spreading into a wider, sparser tail of smaller dots toward the LEFT, so the direction of travel is unmistakable. Leave gaps between the shapes — this is a spray of separate specks, not a solid mass, and the middle of the swarm must stay open enough to see through. NO wand, NO hand, NO fairy, NO wings, NO target, NO ground. NO soft glow, NO bloom, NO gradients, NO blur, NO smoke, NO trailing ribbons. The swarm spans the frame with only a thin margin. Pure black background (single flat color, for easy cutout).
