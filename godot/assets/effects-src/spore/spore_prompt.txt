SUBJECT: ONE cloud of fungal spore dust drifting to the RIGHT — the fine powder a giant mushroom coughs out of its cap into an enemy's face. IMPORTANT — this subject overrides the style block's ban on soft edges and gradients: draw it as a genuinely SOFT, airy, airbrushed haze. NO outlines anywhere, NO hard cel-shaded edges, NO flat cut-out shapes, NO circles with rims, NO bubbles, NO pebbles, NO seeds — every edge of the cloud must fade out gradually into the black background. The cloud is a directional gust: a thin wisp at the LEFT where it leaves the mushroom, swelling as it travels right into a broad, billowing, ragged front on the RIGHT side of the frame, with a few tongues and streaks torn off the leading edge so the direction of travel is unmistakable. Inside the haze, sprinkle a fine grain of tiny specks of dust, no bigger than pinpricks, dense in the thick part and sparse at the fringes — grain, not dots. The middle of the cloud is thin enough to see through; only the leading edge builds up to full density. Colour: a dull sickly yellow-green, dim and desaturated, brightest just inside the leading edge and falling away to nothing at the fringes — no other hue, no glow, no rim light, no sparkle, no smoke curls, no ribbons. Pure BLACK background, and the cloud must fade smoothly into that black with no visible boundary, because the black will be keyed out to alpha afterwards. NO mushroom, NO cap, NO creature, NO hand, NO target, NO ground, NO skull, NO text. The cloud spans the frame with only a thin margin.
