SUBJECT: A towering wall of volcanic rock, the nest of a red dragon, seen
close up from straight ahead. The rock fills the picture edge to edge and
continues past the bottom edge, which cuts straight through the middle of the
rock face: at the bottom of the picture we are still looking at the wall
itself, not at what lies at its feet. Along the top the wall breaks into a
ragged ridge of blunt spires and notches, none of them centered and none of
them reaching below the middle, with a flat haze of grey-brown smoke behind
them. The wall is a skin of cooled lava crust, dark warm grey-brown, hex
#3A322D, broken into irregular plates in flat tones between #463C35 and
#2E2824 with clear dark outlines between them, stacked into broad blocky
ledges and cracked buttresses. The cracks between the plates glow the same
orange as molten lava, hex #E0702A, but seen from far off: thin, dim,
scattered evenly over the whole wall, never widening into a pool, never
gathering in one spot, and never reaching the hot pale yellow-orange #F2B04A.
The crust stays by far the larger area, so the wall reads as DARK rock with
faint warm lines - do NOT flood it with orange and do NOT push the glow toward
pink or red. Every part of the picture below the ridge is rough vertical rock,
broken and irregular all the way down to the bottom edge, growing a little
darker toward the bottom. No smooth or level surface anywhere, no ash, no
rubble, no lava river, no fountain of fire, no sparks, no sun, no sky, no cave
mouth, no bones and no treasure.
