A game title logo, drawn as flat solid shapes on a plain white background. This is a LOGO,
not an illustration or a scene. No texture, no glow, no drop shadow, no frame or border, no
background scenery. Nothing but the mark itself on empty white.

NO OUTLINES ANYWHERE. Every shape is a filled silhouette. Do not draw contour lines around
the hexagons, around the sword, or around the letters. Shapes are read by their filled form
alone, never by a stroke.

COMPOSITION: the emblem and the wordmark OVERLAP. The emblem sits above and its lower edge
descends into the wordmark, passing behind the letters so the two read as one locked
composition rather than two stacked pieces. Keep the overlap SHALLOW: only the very bottom
of the emblem crosses the tops of the letters. Every letter of the word stays fully readable
— nothing may cover a letter enough to break its shape. The whole logo is wider than it is
tall.

THE EMBLEM: seven flat hexagonal tiles lying on the ground, seen from a low camera looking
down, so each hexagon is squashed vertically to about 80% of its height. The hexagons are
FLAT-TOP: a flat horizontal edge along the top and bottom, a pointed corner at the left and
the right. One hexagon sits in the centre and the other six surround it in a ring, so the
six enclose the one. The arrangement is left-right symmetrical. Each tile is a solid filled
shape with a narrow clean gap of white between it and its neighbours — the gaps are what
separate the tiles, not outlines.

THE COLOUR: one single gradient runs ACROSS THE WHOLE CLUSTER from left to right, not within
each tile. The tiles on the left side are a muted steel blue, the tiles in the middle are a
neutral slate that belongs to neither side, and the tiles on the right side are a muted deep
crimson. Each individual tile is filled with one flat colour taken from its place along that
sweep, so the seven tiles together read as a single blue-to-red transition. Keep both
colours desaturated and grown-up — dusty steel blue and dark blood crimson, never bright
primary blue or fire-engine red.

THE SWORD: a single straight sword is driven point-down into the CENTRE hexagon — exactly
where the blue side meets the red side — LEANING at a slight angle rather than standing
perfectly upright, tilted maybe fifteen degrees off vertical, as though it was thrust into
the ground and left there. The blade is partly buried in that tile; the crossguard and the
grip rise above the cluster. The sword is a solid silhouette in dark neutral steel grey,
carrying NO colour from the gradient — it is the one uncoloured thing in the emblem. It is
plain and heavy, a simple soldier's arming sword, no ornament, no jewels, no engraving, no
wings, no ribbons. Make it broad enough to stay visible when the logo is small.

The emblem must read as one single figure: a sword planted in the middle of a ring of six
tiles, standing on the line where two sides meet.

THE WORDMARK: the single word SENARIS, set in a slender elegant serif typeface with the
letters spaced widely apart, drawn as solid filled letterforms in the same dark neutral steel
grey as the sword. No colour in the letters, no outline, no bevel, no metallic effect, no
banner behind it.

The whole logo must stay legible when shrunk to a strip only 87 pixels tall, so keep the
shapes large and simple, keep the gaps between tiles wide enough not to close up, and do not
add any small detail that would disappear at that size.
