A reference sheet of FOUR sword silhouettes, side by side in a single row, evenly spaced,
on a plain pure-white background.

Each sword is a SOLID BLACK SILHOUETTE — a filled shape only. No outline, no contour line, no
grey, no gradient, no shading, no highlight, no texture, no drop shadow, no reflection, no
background scenery, no ground, no frame, no labels, no numbers, no text of any kind.

Each sword stands PERFECTLY VERTICAL and upright, point straight down, pommel straight up.
Do not tilt, rotate or lean any of them. Each is seen flat from the front, face-on to the
blade, so it is exactly left-right symmetrical: the same shape on both sides of its centre
line.

Every sword is a plain, heavy, practical soldier's weapon — a straight double-edged arming
sword. Simple crossguard, plain grip, plain round or disc pommel. NO ornament, no jewels, no
engraving, no runes, no wings, no wrapped ribbons, no wire binding, no fuller lines, no
lettering on the blade. Nothing but the outline of the weapon itself.

Make every sword BROAD and heavy in proportion — a thick blade and a substantial guard — so
the shape survives when it is shrunk down very small. Thin, delicate, elegant blades are
wrong here.

Vary the four only in these ways, keeping all of them simple:
  1. a short broad blade with a straight narrow crossguard
  2. a longer blade with a heavier straight crossguard and a larger disc pommel
  3. a blade that tapers more sharply to the point, with a slightly downswept crossguard
  4. a wide blade with a thick straight crossguard and a compact round pommel

All four are the same height and sit on the same baseline, filling the frame vertically.
