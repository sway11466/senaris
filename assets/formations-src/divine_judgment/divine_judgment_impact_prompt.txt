（divine_judgment_prompt.txt）とは別物で、絵柄も違う＝人物・背景を描かず、痕跡だけを描く。
向きは下向き固定（盤では回転させない）。置き場と扱い → doc/art/keyvisual.md §3、

聖職の一撃と陣形の裁きが同じ色・同じ十字の記号でつながる。

STYLE: A single fantasy tactics-game attack effect, drawn as ONE isolated graphic:
NO character, NO hands, NO weapon owner, NO ground, NO scenery, NO text, NO hex
grid, NO UI. Clean stylized vector-like shapes with bold flat cel-shading in only
two or three tones — the same flat, slightly muted look as the game's unit pieces.
NOT photorealistic, NO glowing bloom, NO soft gradients, NO sparkle particles, NO
smoke, NO motion-blur haze, NO lens flare, NO volumetric god-rays. A crisp, bold,
readable silhouette that still reads when shrunk very small. The graphic is drawn
as a blow travelling straight DOWN, vertical and level (no sideways tilt). It fills
the frame edge to edge with only a thin margin. Plain single flat background color
(given in the subject) for easy cutout. TALL portrait composition, roughly 1:2.5
(clearly taller than wide).

SUBJECT: A divine judgment falling from the sky — ONE straight vertical shaft of
light coming DOWN from the top edge of the frame and striking a point at the
bottom. Left-right symmetrical.

The shaft is NARROW and hard-edged, like a cut-out ribbon of light, not a cone and
not a spotlight: it starts thin at the very top edge, stays slender for most of its
length, and flares only slightly as it nears the bottom. Warm white core with a
pale gold outline edge, two tones only, straight parallel sides and clean corners.
Flanking the shaft, two or three short straight gold speed lines fall alongside it,
thinner and shorter than the shaft, to say that the light is descending fast.

Where it lands at the bottom, draw the point of impact as a FLAT RING seen almost
edge-on — a thin gold ellipse lying on the ground with a few short straight spikes
of light stabbing outward from its rim, and a small pale gold cross resting at the
center of the ring (the cross motif the game's clerics carry). The ring must be
HOLLOW: the area inside it is empty background, not filled with light, so the
figure standing there is never hidden. Keep the whole bottom cluster compact — a
sharp point of impact, NOT a wide explosion, NOT a shockwave dome, NOT a crater.

This is used only by the player's paladin and clergy, so warm white and gold is
correct — do not use blue or red. Pure black background (single flat color, for
easy cutout).
