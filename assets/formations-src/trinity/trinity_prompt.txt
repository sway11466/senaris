SUBJECT: Three chibi spellcasters deep in incantation — TWO wizards and ONE witch — seen from a LOW camera almost at ground level, looking UP at them so they stand tall against the sky. They are spread across the foreground at the three corners of a triangle.

All three are CHANTING, not fighting. Every one of them has their EYES CLOSED and their mouth open in a small round "o", as if singing a sustained note — serene, absorbed, perfectly still. Do NOT draw them shouting, roaring, gritting their teeth, grimacing, or bracing against anything; no wide staring eyes, no angry brows, no straining. Their calm stillness against the violence of the spell behind them is the whole point of the picture.

The two wizards wear the same uniform as each other: a slate blue-grey pointed hat with a wide brim and a floppy bent tip banded in darker grey, a matching slate blue-grey full-length robe with a hooded collar, and brown boots. They are YOUNG and CLEAN-SHAVEN — no beard, no moustache, no old-man wizard; short dark hair. Each carries a dark wooden staff capped with a pale ice-blue crystal. Keep their build, hair and face shape the same; separate them by POSE only:

- The first wizard holds his staff upright in both hands close to his chest, head bowed, chin tucked — quiet and inward.
- The second wizard holds his staff aloft in one hand, face tilted up toward the sky, the other hand open at his side, palm up — offering the chant upward.

The witch is a young woman with long brown hair, in a deep purple wide-brimmed pointed hat with a bent tip and a blue-grey hatband with a small silver buckle, a black sleeveless top with a blue-grey belt, a short black skirt, and brown knee-high boots. Do NOT put a crystal, blade or glow on both ends — it is a one-ended staff. The same rule applies to the two wizards' staves: crystal at the top, bare wood at the bottom. It must read as an ACTIVE INCANTATION still being sustained.
