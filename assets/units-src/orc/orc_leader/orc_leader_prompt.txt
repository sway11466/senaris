SUBJECT: An orc leader — the war-boss who drives the horde's rank-and-file forward. He is the SAME kind of creature as the orc grunt, one rank up, not a different monster and not a demon lord: same thick ash-grey hide with a brown-earth cast (definitely NOT green skin — green belongs to the goblins), same heavy hunched build with a bull neck, sloping shoulders, long thick arms and short bowed legs, same blunt jutting jaw with two tusks pushing up from the lower lip, same flat broad nose and small deep-set dull-red eyes. He is a head bulkier than the grunt and visibly older and harder: old scars across the muzzle and chest, a torn ear, coarse black hair pulled back and bound with cord. Better war-gear, all of it looted and mismatched: overlapping plates of blackened iron strapped across the chest and one shoulder, thick studded bracers, a broad iron-buckled belt, heavy shin wrappings — clearly heavier and more layered than the grunt's roped-on scraps. His rank is shown by ONE marking: a deep-crimson war-cloth, torn and weathered, slung over one shoulder and hanging at his back like a short cape. NO helmet (the tusks and jaw must stay visible), NO crown, NO banner. He grips a big two-handed chopping blade upright in front of him with both hands — the same crude pitted dark-iron cleaver shape as the grunt's but noticeably longer and broader, with a long wrapped haft, chunky and oversized. NO bow, NO magic. Hard, commanding and mean; a brute the other brutes obey. Keep the mature, muted, limited palette: sooty blacks, ash-grey hide and dark iron, with crimson only in the war-cloth.
