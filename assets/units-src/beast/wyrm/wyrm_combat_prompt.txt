POSE (bite): A lunging bite — the body coils back and then whips forward so the head lances out toward the enemy on the left, jaws wide open and fangs bared, neck extended at full reach, the rest of the body trailing behind the strike. For beasts that attack with their mouth and have no arms to swing.

SUBJECT: The SAME wyrm as the wyrm board piece — a serpent-dragon of the badlands (same broad draconic skull, same backswept horns, same spined dorsal fin, same large membrane wings, same charcoal-and-bronze scale plates; this must read as the exact same animal). Body plan: one long, heavy, snake-like trunk with NO legs, NO arms, NO claws and NO hands — do NOT draw it as a lizard, a crocodile or a four-legged drake. The only limbs are a pair of large ribbed membrane wings on the shoulders behind the head, pale translucent membrane between dark spars, here swept BACK along the body as it strikes rather than stretched flat out to the sides. It is a DRAGON, not a mere snake: broad blunt draconic skull, heavy hinged jaw, rows of thick fangs, short backswept horns, spiny whiskers at the jaw, and a ridged fin of dark webbing down the spine; the trunk is python-thick, far heavier than the small rock serpent. It wears NOTHING — no saddle, no harness, no collar, no armour, no ornament. Seen from the side, striking: the back half of the heavy body is still heaped in a coil ON THE GROUND at the right of the frame while the front whips out to the LEFT, neck extended at full reach, jaws wide open with the fangs bared. It is a GROUND creature — the coil stays down on the earth and it does NOT hover or fly. Hide of bold overlapping scale plates in dark charcoal and slate with a deep bronze-olive sheen along the back and a paler ash belly. The eyes are dull red. NO fire, NO smoke — never the great red dragon. Keep the whole animal compact in the frame, the coil stacked and the wings swept back rather than spread wide, so the piece is not much wider than it is tall. Keep the mature, muted, limited palette: charcoal, slate and bronze-olive, with red only in the eyes.

NOTE: the STYLE block's instructions about a person's head-to-body proportions, arms, hands and "both feet visible" do NOT apply to this piece — it is a limbless winged serpent with no feet. Keep only the STYLE's rendering, the three-quarter overhead angle facing LEFT, the centered composition and the plain pure-white background with no ground and no shadow.
