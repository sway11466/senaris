SUBJECT: A harpy — a shrieking bird-woman of the monster side that comes in flocks, small and light and dangerous in numbers. She is a MONSTER, not a person with wings: her ARMS ARE WINGS — long ragged feathered wings grow straight out of her shoulders where arms would be, ending in feather tips, so she has NO hands and NO fingers anywhere. From the knee DOWN her legs are a bird's: thin scaly shanks with heavy black talons, held under her as she flies. Everything else is a young woman's: head, neck, shoulders, a human torso with a normal waist and belly, and human thighs, in smooth warm-tan human skin — so that her human face sits on a human body, NOT on a bird's feathered barrel. Chunky chibi proportions with a big oversized head and a small slender body. Her face is an ordinary young woman's face, drawn in exactly the same friendly chibi style as the game's other unit pieces: a rounded face with large clear eyes in warm amber-brown, a small nose and a small closed mouth. Keep the face plain and unglamorous but NOT frightening — NO gaunt hollow cheeks, NO sunken or glowing eyes, NO heavy scowling brow, NO hooked witch's nose, NO bared fangs or pointed teeth, NO snarl or grimace, nothing ghoulish or corpse-like. Instead of hair she has a mane of pale straw-blonde feathers swept back off her head. Her feathers grow as her clothing: a thick feathered collar and mantle covering the chest and shoulders, and a short feathered skirt around the hips and upper thighs. Between them the human midriff and the human thighs are bare skin. She must be decently covered by that plumage and NOT sexualised at all — chibi proportions, a flat childlike build, no cleavage, no exposed chest, no exposed hips, nothing suggestive. Feather colour: deep muted rust-red along the long wing feathers, warming to brick and dusty rose toward the tips, with paler sand-tan on the collar and skirt. She is a WARM-coloured bird, not a black one: NO sooty black plumage, NO crow or raven colouring. Apart from her own plumage she wears NOTHING — no cloth, no rags, no armour, no jewellery, no ornament of any kind — and she carries NO weapon and NO tool: she fights with talons and voice alone. She is AIRBORNE — the whole body floats clear of the ground with the taloned feet hanging beneath her and the wings spread WIDE and open on both sides so they fill out the silhouette, and a small soft oval ground shadow is cast on the ground directly under her with a clear empty gap between the talons and that shadow; she is plainly hovering, NOT standing. Wild and alert, like a wary wild bird. She reads as a monster because of the wings-for-arms and the taloned bird legs — NOT because of her face or body, which stay human and ordinary. Not seductive and not a cute fairy, but not scary-looking either. Keep the mature, muted, limited palette: muted rust-red and brick feathers over warm sand-tan, with darker rust in the shadows and black only in the talons. Rich and warm, but still desaturated and flat cel-shaded — NOT a bright hot-pink cartoon bird, NOT glossy, NOT painterly or photorealistic.
