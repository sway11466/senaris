POSE (drift): A floating attack pose — the body hovers clear of the ground with no weight on any foot, drifting toward the enemy on the left with both hands (or the weapon) reaching ahead, the trailing hem or tail streaming out behind. For flying units.

SUBJECT: The SAME griffon as the griffon board piece — a wild predator beast of the monster side (same eagle head, same sooty-and-ash plumage, same lion hindquarters, same black beak and talons; this must read as the exact same animal). It is an ANIMAL, not a person: an eagle's head, an eagle's broad feathered wings and eagle forelegs with talons, joined to a lion's hindquarters with heavy paws and a tufted tail. It has NO hands and never stands upright like a man. Chunky, stocky proportions with a big oversized head and short thick limbs. Plumage on the head, chest and wings in sooty black shading to ash-grey and earth-brown, with a coarse dusty-brown lion coat behind; the hooked beak and every talon are black; the eyes are dull red; a few pale feather bars break up the wings. It wears NOTHING — no saddle, no harness, no collar, no reins, no armour, no ornament. It is diving: the body hurtles toward the enemy on the left with both taloned forelegs thrown forward at full reach and the claws spread to strike, the beak open, the wings swept back and up behind it and the lion tail streaming out behind. No foot touches anything — it is entirely airborne. Keep the whole animal compact within the frame: wings swept BACK along the body rather than stretched out flat to the sides, so the piece is taller than it is wide. Fierce, alert and predatory. Keep the mature, muted, limited palette: sooty blacks, ash-grey and dusty browns, with red only in the eyes.
