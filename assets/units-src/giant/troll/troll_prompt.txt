SUBJECT: A troll — a huge, slow, thick-hided brute that has climbed up out of a cave under the badlands. It is the heaviest thing on the board: it hits hard, it is very tough, and it barely moves. It is a MONSTER BEAST, not a person and not an orc: build it big and lumpen with a heavy stooping back, a tiny head sunk low between enormous rounded shoulders, gigantic thick arms that hang down past its knees with heavy knuckles resting near the ground, a bloated barrel belly and short bowed legs with broad flat feet. Its hide is its armour: thick, knotted grey-green skin, mottled and leathery, with patches of hard warty plates and a crust of grey stone-dust and lichen across the shoulders and back, as if it were part of the cave wall. Tiny dull red eyes sit deep under a heavy brow; the mouth is a wide flat gash with a few blunt tusks poking up; the ears are small and torn. It carries NO weapon and NO tool: it fights with its fists. It wears NOTHING — no clothing, no armour, no belt, no collar, no ornament of any kind; keep the whole body covered by that knotted hide with no anatomical detail, smooth and simple like the other pieces. Seen head-on and symmetrical, standing heavy and planted with both arms hanging. Slow, stupid and immovable — NOT quick, NOT lean, NOT clever; the silhouette should read as a boulder with arms. Keep the mature, muted, limited palette: grey-greens, stone greys and dusty browns, with red only in the eyes.

NOTE: the STYLE block's instruction about held weapons does not apply — this piece carries nothing. Keep everything else in the STYLE, including the straight-on symmetrical front view, the centered composition with the feet near the lower third and the small soft ground shadow.
