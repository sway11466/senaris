SUBJECT: A rogue axeman — the muscle of the outlaw gang that traps monsters and sells them, the one who breaks open cages and cuts through fences. He wears the SAME black-and-crimson bandit gear family as the rogue and must clearly read as one of the same gang, but he ranks a step above the skinny rank-and-file rogue and well below the gang's boss. He is an ordinary HUMAN: normal human skin, NO tusks, NO fangs, NO horns, NO claws. Unlike the scrawny rogue, build him HEAVY and thick-set — a broad barrel chest, a thick short neck, heavy sloping shoulders and big forearms — so his strength reads from his BODY, not from armour. His clothes are still cheap and plain but perfectly INTACT and reasonably kept: a dark charcoal-brown sleeveless tunic over grey-brown trousers, a broad plain belt, plain worn boots. NO rips, NO tatters, NO patches, NO frayed hems, NO holes, NO bandage-like rag wrappings. His only upgrade over the mook is ONE piece of black leather plus thick leather work gloves: a plain black leather apron over the chest with just two or three simple buckles. NO plate armour, NO helmet, NO mail, NO fitted full leather suit, NO rows of studs, NO polished metal, NO uniform, NO cloak — a thug, not a knight, and his kit must stay visibly poorer than the gang boss's tailored black leather. He hides his face like the other underlings, but NOT with a hood: a deep-crimson cloth is wound tight around his head like a bandana and a second deep-crimson cloth covers his nose and mouth, leaving only the eyes visible — giving him a compact, rounded head silhouette clearly different from the rogue's pointed cloth hood. His weapon is a big TWO-HANDED AXE, a woodcutter's axe pressed into war: one thick heavy single-bit iron head, wide and blunt-backed, on a long stout wooden haft with leather wound around the grip. The iron is dull, dark and unpolished, chipped and nicked from use — a crude heavy working tool, NOT a fine engraved battle-axe, NOT a halberd, NOT a double-bit axe, NO gold, NO gems, NO polish, NO glow. He holds it upright in front of him with both hands, head up and close to the body. He carries the gang's trade tool: a thick coil of heavy rope slung across his chest. No priestly sign anywhere: no cross, no robe, no holy symbol. EXPRESSION — play him as a proper villain, not a raging monster: the eyes above the mask are narrowed to a hard, sly glare with the brows angled down and inward, smug and mean, plainly pleased with how much bigger he is than you. He is NOT frightened, NOT snarling, NOT baring teeth; NO gaunt cheeks, NO sunken eyes, NO dark shadow across the eyes. He stands upright and insolent. Keep the mature, muted, limited palette: charcoal, dark browns and greys, black leather, with the deep-crimson head cloth and mask as the only colour.
