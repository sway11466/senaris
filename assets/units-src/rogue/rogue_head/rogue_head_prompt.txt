SUBJECT: The rogue head — the boss of the outlaw gang that traps monsters and sells them, and the man behind everything that has gone wrong. He is an ordinary HUMAN: normal human skin, NO tusks, NO fangs, NO horns, NO claws. Unlike his masked underlings he shows his FACE and wears NO hood and NO mask: a composed, even-featured man in his forties with a soft rounded chibi face like the game's other pieces, close-cropped dark hair swept back, a neatly trimmed short beard, a small faint old scar on one cheek, and calm level eyes. Build him solid and capable — broader in the shoulders and a head taller than the skinny rank-and-file rogue, standing straight and at ease, plainly used to being obeyed. His gear is the same black-and-crimson outlaw kit as his men, but everything is a grade better and well kept: fitted black leather with a reinforced shoulder piece and studded bracers, a good broad belt with brass buckles, sturdy boots, and a deep-crimson scarf worn loose around his NECK (not over his face) as the gang's colour. Still NO plate armour, NO helmet, NO uniform — an outlaw, not a knight. He holds one good curved sabre upright in front of him with both hands, blade up and close to the body: clean polished steel with a simple guard and a wrapped grip, clearly a real weapon and a step above the mook's little rusty knife. At his belt hangs the trade tool of this gang: a short iron chain ending in a heavy hook, for dragging what he catches. No priestly sign anywhere: no cross, no robe, no holy symbol. EXPRESSION — keep it PLEASANT on the surface, not frightening: a relaxed, businesslike look with the faint easy half-smile of a merchant closing a deal, eyes calm and untroubled. Eyebrows relaxed and level — NO furrowed or angry brows, NO scowl, NO glare, NO grim downturned mouth, NO snarl, NO bared teeth, NO dark shadow across the eyes, nothing menacing or intimidating in the face itself. He is unsettling precisely because he looks so unbothered and ordinary; the villainy is in what he does, not in a fierce expression. Keep the mature, muted, limited palette: blacks and charcoal leather with deep crimson in the scarf, brass fittings and one line of polished steel.
