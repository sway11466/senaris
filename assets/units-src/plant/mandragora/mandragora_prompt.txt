SUBJECT: A mandragora — an uprooted mandrake root that has pulled itself out of the forest floor and walks, killing at a distance with its magical shriek. It is a PLANT, not an animal and not a person in a costume: the whole creature IS one gnarled root that happens to be shaped like a little man — a knotted burl for the head and torso, two thin woody roots for arms, and a forked pair of thick roots for legs, with fine root hairs and crumbs of dark soil still clinging to the lower ends. NO fur, NO scales, NO claws, NO animal limbs, NO clothing, NO armour, NO weapon, NO staff, NO wand, NO bow, NO tools or ornaments of any kind — it throws nothing and holds nothing. Chibi proportions: a big knobbly head on a small stubby root body, short and slight so it reads as a small piece that comes in numbers, clearly smaller and lighter than the towering treant. From the crown of its head sprouts a big spreading fan of five or six broad, pointed leaves with clear central veins, standing up and out like a crown — fresh leaf green, the one strong colour on the whole piece, bright enough to sit clearly above the dark green forest ground tiles it stands on, but held a touch below neon so it stays in the game's muted register. Colours: pale cream and bone-coloured root flesh with soft grey-brown shading in the grooves, darker soil at the root tips — a dry, papery root surface, NOT the cracked grey bark of a tree and NOT soft mushroom flesh. Its face is not drawn on the root, it IS the root: two dark rounded hollows for eyes and a third larger hollow below them held open in a round O of a mouth, like knot-holes in the wood — simple, flat, dark openings. Keep it plainly odd rather than horrific: NO fangs, NO teeth, NO torn or jagged edges, NO eyeballs, NO veins, NO blood. Keep the mature, muted, limited palette: pale cream root, earth brown, dark knot-hole face, and the green leaf crown as the single strong colour.
