SUBJECT: A red dragon — the great wyrm of the volcano, the final boss of this campaign and the largest piece on the board. It is a proper DRAGON and must outclass every other monster at a glance: a heavy four-legged reptilian body with a deep barrel chest, thick pillar legs ending in huge black talons, a long heavy tail, a powerful neck, and a pair of membrane wings that are clearly LARGER than its own body. A crown of thick, backswept horns sweeps off the skull, with smaller spikes along the jaw and a ridge of hard spines running down the neck, spine and tail. Its scales are big, thick, overlapping plates — deep blood-crimson across the back and flanks, darkening to black-rust in the shadows and at the plate edges, with a duller ash-charcoal belly and dark charcoal wing membrane between crimson spars, so the red never becomes one flat mass. The eyes are hot amber-gold, the horns, talons and jaw spikes black. It wears NOTHING — no saddle, no harness, no collar, no armour, no ornament of any kind. Because fire is what it is, but not what we draw: the only hint of it is a deep ember-red glow deep inside the parted jaws. There is NO flame, NO fireball, NO burning breath, NO sparks, NO smoke or wisps of any kind anywhere — not at the nostrils, not at the mouth, not around the body — and NO glow spilling onto the body or the background. It is AIRBORNE — the whole body hangs clear of the ground with the four legs tucked and hanging beneath it, the tail curling down, and a small soft oval ground shadow is cast on the ground directly below with a clear empty gap between the talons and that shadow; it is plainly hovering, NOT standing. Seen head-on and symmetrical, the head thrust forward and level at the viewer, jaws parted. Keep the piece TALL rather than wide: the wings are raised HIGH and swept back like a canopy behind the shoulders rather than stretched out flat to the sides, so the silhouette stays inside a squarish frame. Furious and imperious — a disaster to be calmed, not a villain to be sneered at: proud and wrathful, NOT comic, NOT sly, NOT grinning. Keep the mature, muted, limited palette: deep crimson and black-rust with charcoal, and amber only in the eyes and the ember glow in its throat.

NOTE: the STYLE block's instructions about a person's head-to-body proportions, arms, hands and held weapons do NOT apply to this piece — it is a four-legged winged beast. Keep only the STYLE's rendering, the straight-on symmetrical front view, and the centered composition with the small soft ground shadow beneath the hovering body.
