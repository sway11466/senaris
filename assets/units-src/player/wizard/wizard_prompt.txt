CRITICAL: an ENORMOUS round oversized chibi head — the head alone is roughly 40% of the whole figure's height (about 2 to 2.5 heads tall total), NOT 3 heads tall; do NOT shrink the head or elongate the body, the limbs stay short and thick. Calm, scholarly, composed. TWO THINGS SET HIM APART from the apprentice, and nothing else changes. FIRST, the decoration of his robes: a BOLD ORNAMENTAL PATTERN band running around both SLEEVE CUFFS and around the HEM of the robe — a chunky repeating geometric arcane motif in pale gold and pale arcane blue, wide and simple enough to read clearly even when the piece is shrunk very small (NOT fine embroidery, NOT busy filigree). The apprentice's robes are plain; these bands are the mark of the trained wizard. SECOND, his staff is a grade grander: a LONGER and THICKER staff, taller than he is, its shaft wrapped by a few THICK curling vines of carved silver-grey metal that spiral up from the grip and open out at the top into a CAGE OF VINES that encircles and holds a LARGE blue arcane crystal, the crystal clearly visible inside the ring and giving a gentle soft blue-white glow. Keep the vines few, thick and boldly shaped so the head of the staff still reads as a ring around a glowing stone when the piece is shrunk very small — NOT delicate filigree, NOT a dense thicket of tiny scrollwork. Keep everything else identical to the apprentice in build and palette: no pattern on the hat, no cape, no extra props. NO holy cross (that is the clergy), NO bow, NO skull or bone motifs (that is the necromancer) — the pointed hat plus the crystal-topped staff is his role sign. Keep the mature, muted, limited palette.
