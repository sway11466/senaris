SUBJECT: One straight run of the low stone parapet of an old footbridge, seen
from DIRECTLY OVERHEAD -- looking straight down onto the flat top of the
parapet wall, so the picture shows a narrow horizontal BAND of coping stones
crossing the whole frame from the left edge to the right edge.

THE BAND: the parapet runs perfectly straight and level across the middle of
the frame, about one eighth of the frame tall, and runs off both the left and
right edges -- no ends, no posts at the ends, no gate, no gap, no stairs, the
same wall all the way. The OUTSIDE of the bridge (the drop to the water) is
toward the TOP of the frame, the walking surface of the bridge toward the
BOTTOM. Above and below the band, leave a plain flat single-colour background
-- NO water, NO stone deck, NO grass, NO shadow on the ground.

THE STONES: heavy weathered coping blocks laid end to end along the run, each
block a slightly different length, their joints thin and dark. The band will
be shrunk very small in the game, so keep the blocks BIG and SIMPLE -- roughly
one twelfth of the frame long each, no carving, no ornament, no moss, no
fine texture.

COLOR: the same dry grey stone as the bridge deck, roughly hex #8C8A82, but a
touch lighter on top so the parapet reads as raised above the deck. NO block
may be white or near-white, none near-black.

Lit dimly and evenly from straight above, with NO cast shadow and no light
direction: any shading stays inside each block, the same along the whole run,
so the band repeats seamlessly left to right.
