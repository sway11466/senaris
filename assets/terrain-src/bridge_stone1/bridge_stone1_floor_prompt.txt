SUBJECT: The stone deck of an old footbridge, seen from straight above --
just the walking surface, filling the whole picture.

FILL THE FRAME -- IMPORTANT: the stonework covers the ENTIRE square, edge to
edge, corner to corner. This is a texture swatch, not a scene and not an
object. NO background, NO grass, NO water, NO river, NO bank, NO parapet, NO
railing, NO kerb, NO edge, NO border, NO frame, NO vignette, NO rounded
corners. The deck runs off all four sides. Nothing stands on it and nothing
lies on it.

THE STONES: worn rectangular slabs and irregular flagstones fitted together by
a mason, each one a slightly different size and shape. A stone is roughly a
twelfth of the frame across, and none is bigger than an eighth of the frame.

NO PATTERN -- CRITICAL: this same texture is cut into many map tiles and also
rotated by 60 degrees, so any regularity shows up immediately as a repeat.
NO rows, NO courses, NO grid, NO brickwork or running bond, NO herringbone,
and NO joint line that runs straight across more than about a fifth of the
frame. The stones sit at all angles and their joints wander. Nothing in the
picture is distinctive enough to be recognised as a landmark when the square
is laid down many times side by side.

THE JOINTS: thin and dark -- narrow dark lines, a little darker than the stone
itself. NO white or pale joints, NO wide mortar beds, NO bright grout.

COLOR: dry grey stone, roughly hex #8C8A82 -- a calm, slightly warm mid grey,
matching the game's stone-paved road so a road running onto the bridge reads
as one surface. Each stone is a slightly different shade of that grey, some a
touch warmer, some a touch cooler, scattered unevenly. NO stone may be white
or near-white, and none may be near-black. NO moss, NO puddles, NO cracks big
enough to notice, NO carved marks.

Lit dimly and evenly from straight above, with NO cast shadow at all and no
light direction: the stones are flat paving and throw no shadows. Shading
stays inside each stone -- very slight, just enough to tell one stone from
the next.
