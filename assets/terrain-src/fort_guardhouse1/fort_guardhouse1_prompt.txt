SUBJECT: A small GUARDHOUSE beside a town gate, as drawn on the world map of a
classic 2D role-playing game: the ground seen from directly overhead, but the
building drawn from the front and slightly above, standing upright and facing
the bottom of the frame — nothing lying flat, no perspective.

ONE squat single-storey guardhouse of rough grey fieldstone with a steep
slate-and-timber roof and a plain timber door in the middle of its front wall.
There is no second building. It stands at the BACK of a small square yard. A low
drystone wall runs all the way round the yard, with a simple timber gate in the
middle of the front side. The yard is cobbled.

IN THE YARD — exactly two things, both large enough to read: a wooden weapon
rack standing against the wall with three or four spears in it, and a
two-wheeled hand cart parked to one side. Nothing else: no people, no animals,
no barrels, no fire, no laundry, no crates, no flags, no signage, no smoke.

SIZE — the whole compound is a chunky block, roughly as wide as it is tall,
sitting in the MIDDLE of the picture. In a 1024-pixel picture it is about 700
pixels across, leaving about 160 pixels of white on all four sides. It must NOT
touch any edge and must NOT be cut by any edge. This is a guardhouse, not a
town: one building, one yard.

COLOR — the stone and the slate are plain grey with almost no colour in them,
neither warm nor cool; only the door, the gate, the rack and the cart are
weathered brown timber. NO ochre, NO warm tan, and NO strong red, blue or green
anywhere — those colours mark who holds the post.

THE BACKGROUND — everything outside the drystone wall is PURE FLAT WHITE,
#FFFFFF, with NO texture, NO shading, NO gradient, NO grass, NO soil, NO road,
NO paving. This white is going to be deleted and the game will draw its own
street there, so the outside of the wall must be a hard, clean, crisp edge
against it. Nothing inside the compound may be white or near-white.

LIGHT — dim light from above and slightly in front, with short soft shadows.
Every shadow falls INSIDE the yard or against the wall; NOTHING casts a shadow
out onto the white.

Read at thumbnail size: a few large legible shapes, no fine detail, no
picked-out windows. Square 1:1.
