SUBJECT: An old graveyard gone to seed. The ground is dry yellowed grass, hex
#B2B28E, with pale dusty soil, hex #A89877, showing through in a handful of
small openings. Hold that grass colour precisely — it is a greyed-out khaki,
NOT a warm yellow. Do NOT make it more yellow, more golden, more saturated or
brighter than #B2B28E. Keep the ground even and low-contrast.

Standing in the grass are FOUR or FIVE OLD GRAVE MARKERS. The ground is seen
from straight above, but the markers themselves are drawn STANDING UPRIGHT and
facing the bottom of the frame, the way buildings are drawn on the world map of
a classic 2D role-playing game, so you see the face of each one.

Give every marker a different shape: a slab with a rounded top, a squared-off
slab, a stone cross with short thick arms, a stub whose top has snapped off.
Lean some of them at their own angle so none stand quite straight.

SIZE — each marker stands roughly ONE THIRD of the frame's height. If the
picture were 1000 pixels tall, each would be about 330 pixels tall. They are by
far the largest things in the picture, many times taller than a clump of grass,
chunky and solid, still readable when the whole picture is shrunk to a
thumbnail. Do NOT draw them small, thin or dainty. Their stone is dull mid-grey,
clearly paler than the grass but NOT white and NOT black-outlined.

PLACEMENT — scatter them irregularly: two close together, one off on its own,
wide empty grass between. NEVER evenly spaced, NEVER in a row or a grid, and
never centred in a way that makes one of them the subject. Keep them all in the
middle of the picture: the outer fifth of the frame on all four sides, and the
four corners, are plain empty grass. No marker may touch an edge, sit in a
corner, or be clipped by the frame. This is a rule about where things go, not
something to draw — do NOT draw a circle, ring, outline, border or guide line
of any kind.

NO SHADOWS. The markers cast nothing on the grass; there is no light direction
in this picture at all.

Nothing else stands up from the ground: no building, tomb, mausoleum, hut,
wall, doorway, steps, fence, gate or path. No statues, urns, flowers, candles,
bones, skulls, open graves or mounds. No glow, no mist, no magic.

The mood is quiet neglect — a burial ground nobody visits any more.
