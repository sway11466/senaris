SUBJECT: A heap of PLUNDER dumped in a goblin cave — food and goods carried
off from a raided farming town and thrown into a pile by goblins who do not
care for any of it — as drawn on the world map of a classic 2D role-playing
game: seen from the front and slightly above, standing upright and facing the
bottom of the frame — nothing lying flat, no perspective.

ONE compact pile, everything dumped on top of everything else into a single
sagging mound: two or three round wooden BARRELS bound with rusty iron hoops —
one upright, one tipped on its side, one with its lid gone and a stave
missing; a couple of slatted wooden CRATES, one smashed open at the corner with
broken slats sticking out; and three or four burlap SACKS, lumpy and
misshapen, one torn so that pale grain has run out down the front of the pile.
The pile is CRUDE and CARELESS: nothing is stacked straight, nothing is tied
down, things lean and slump and spill. Scraps lie in the gaps — a snapped
board, a loose lid, a bit of rope, a gnawed bone or two. These are a farm
town's stores, stolen and mistreated: NO gold, NO coins, NO treasure chest, NO
jewels, NO weapons, NO armour.

THE LOOK — battered and dirty, not neat: the timber is split, chipped, scuffed
and stained, with grain and cracks showing; the hoops are rusted and one is
sprung loose; the burlap is patched, frayed at the seams and smeared with
grime. NOT a tidy merchant's stack, NOT new barrels, NOT clean sacks.

SIZE — the pile sits in the MIDDLE of the picture, wider than it is tall. In a
1024-pixel picture it is about 620 pixels wide and about 460 pixels tall,
leaving at least 200 pixels of white on every side. It must NOT touch any edge
and must NOT be cut by any edge.

COLOR — weathered dull-brown timber, hex #6E5238, greyed with dirt; rust-brown
iron hoops; undyed grey-tan burlap, hex #9A8C74, dirtier in the creases; the
spilled grain a pale dull straw colour, never bright yellow. Everything muted,
in the same clean stylized cel-shaded look as the game's terrain and unit art:
bold flat shading, clear dark outlines, a mature, slightly muted, limited
color palette (NOT bright saturated, NOT painterly photorealism). NO strong
red, blue or green anywhere.

THE BACKGROUND — everything around the pile is PURE FLAT WHITE, #FFFFFF, with
NO texture, NO shading, NO gradient, NO ground, NO floor, NO cave wall, NO drop
shadow. This white is going to be deleted and the game will draw its own cave
floor there, so the outline of the pile must be a hard, clean, crisp edge
against it. Nothing in the pile itself may be white or near-white.

LIGHT — dim light from above and slightly in front, short soft shadows that
fall only on the pile itself; NOTHING casts a shadow onto the white.

Nothing else: no goblin, no creature, no people, no animals, no rats, no
torches, no fire, no lettering, no signs. Read at thumbnail size: a few large
legible shapes, no fine detail. NO border, NO frame around the picture itself,
NO vignette. Square 1:1.
