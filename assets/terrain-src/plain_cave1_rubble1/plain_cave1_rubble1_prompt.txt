SUBJECT: A MOUND OF FALLEN ROCK inside a cave — a heap of broken boulders and
rubble where part of the cave has come down — as drawn on the world map of a
classic 2D role-playing game: seen from the front and slightly above, standing
upright and facing the bottom of the frame — nothing lying flat, no
perspective.

ONE heap, wider than it is tall, made of rough broken cave rock: a few LARGE
angular boulders (the biggest about a third of the heap's height) piled
against and on top of each other, with smaller chunks, slabs and loose rubble
filling the gaps and spilling out around the base. The pieces sit at every
angle, wedged and unstable, so the heap reads as IMPASSABLE — no path, no
ledge, no flat footing, nothing a person on foot could walk over without
climbing. It is plain fallen rock, NOT stalagmites, NOT a cliff, NOT a wall,
NOT worked stone: no columns, no blocks, no bricks, no carving.

SIZE — the heap sits in the MIDDLE of the picture. In a 1024-pixel picture it
is about 640 pixels wide and about 460 pixels tall, leaving at least 190
pixels of white on every side. It must NOT touch any edge and must NOT be cut
by any edge.

COLOR — dull grey-brown cave rock, hex #6E655A, the same stone as the cave's
walls; each boulder takes a slightly different flat tone somewhere between
#7A7164 and #5E564C so the pieces separate from one another by tone alone.
Everything muted, in the same clean stylized cel-shaded look as the game's
terrain and unit art: bold flat shading, clear dark outlines, a mature,
slightly muted, limited color palette (NOT bright saturated, NOT painterly
photorealism). NO moss, NO crystals, NO glow, NO water, NO strong red, blue or
green anywhere.

THE BACKGROUND — everything around the heap is PURE FLAT WHITE, #FFFFFF, with
NO texture, NO shading, NO gradient, NO ground, NO floor, NO cave wall, NO drop
shadow. This white is going to be deleted and the game will draw its own cave
floor there, so the outline of the heap must be a hard, clean, crisp edge
against it. Nothing in the rock itself may be white or near-white.

LIGHT — dim light from above and slightly in front, short soft shadows that
fall only on the rock itself; NOTHING casts a shadow onto the white.

Nothing else: no creature, no bones, no mushrooms, no plants, no torches, no
treasure, no tools, no lettering. Read at thumbnail size: a few large legible
shapes, no fine detail. NO border, NO frame around the picture itself, NO
vignette. Square 1:1.
