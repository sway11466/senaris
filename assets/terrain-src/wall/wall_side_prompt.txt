STYLE: A side-on wall-face texture for a fantasy tactics game, in the same clean
stylized cel-shaded look as the game's terrain and unit art: bold flat shading, a
mature, slightly muted, limited color palette (NOT bright saturated, NOT
painterly photorealism). Viewed straight from the FRONT, face-on — a flat lay
with NO perspective, NO horizon, NO sky, NO ground line, NO floor. The texture
fills the ENTIRE square frame edge to edge, with NO border, NO frame, NO
vignette, and no single big focal object. It must tile SEAMLESSLY LEFT TO RIGHT:
the left and right edges continue into each other. CRITICAL: vary the rock
RANDOMLY and UNEVENLY — never a repeated motif, never the same shape twice,
never in rows or a regular grid. Avoid large, high-contrast patches (they repeat
visibly when tiled); keep large-scale color even and low-contrast. Square 1:1.

SUBJECT: The raw ROCK FACE where a passage has broken through a single mass of
solid stone, seen face-on. This is ONE CONTINUOUS BODY OF ROCK, never an
assembly of pieces: the surface swells forward and sinks back in broad uneven
lobes, and irregular fracture lines wander and branch across it, dividing
nothing — the rock runs on unbroken underneath them. Where slabs have sheared
away the face is left with shallow shelves and blunt overhangs, dark undercuts
beneath them, and rough conchoidal scars in between. The relief is subtly
VERTICAL: the lobes and fractures run more downward than sideways, as if the
rock split under its own weight. Dark cold grey-brown stone, base color around
#5F584F, its tone shifting gradually across the face. Lit dimly from above:
upward-facing shelves catch a little light, undercuts stay dark. Raw, heavy,
geological. CRITICAL: NO separate stones, NO cobbles, NO boulders set side by
side, NO joints or gaps between pieces, NO stones packed into a matrix — nothing
that reads as built or assembled. NOT masonry — NO stacked bricks, NO mortar
lines, NO worked edges. NO tool marks, NO pick or chisel gouges, NO scratches,
NO carved shapes. NO vegetation of any kind — NO grass, NO moss, NO lichen, NO
plants, NO soil, NO puddles, NO snow. NO objects of any kind — NO torches, NO
bones, NO ore, NO crystals, NO props. This is only the flat rock face — NO top
surface, NO floor, NO arch, NO opening, NO cast shadow onto anything else.
