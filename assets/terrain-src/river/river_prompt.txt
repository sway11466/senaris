STYLE: A map tile element for a fantasy tactics game, in the same clean
stylized cel-shaded look as the game's terrain and unit art: bold flat shading,
clear dark outlines, a mature, slightly muted, limited color palette (NOT bright
saturated, NOT painterly photorealism). Drawn like the world map of a classic 2D
role-playing game, seen from DIRECTLY OVERHEAD — flat, no perspective, no
horizon, no sky, no depth. This is ground seen from above: nothing is built on
it, nothing stands up out of it, nothing casts a shadow, and nothing is
reflected in it. Read at thumbnail size: a few large legible shapes, no fine
detail. Square 1:1.

BACKGROUND — IMPORTANT: this is a cut-out element, not a scene. Everything that
is not the water itself is PLAIN PURE WHITE (#FFFFFF), flat and empty, right out
to all four edges. NO grass, NO bank, NO shore, NO reeds, NO rocks, NO scenery,
NO texture, NO gradient, NO shadow, NO border, NO frame. Nothing at all on the
white.

SUBJECT: A single straight, wide RIVER running away from the viewer, straight up
the frame. Clean, clear, blue-green river water, calm and still. It should read
as WATER at a glance, unmistakably — the most obviously watery thing on the map.

Composition, exactly: the river is one straight vertical band, dead centre
left-to-right, crossing the full height of the frame and continuing off BOTH the
top edge and the bottom edge — never stopping, never bending, never leaning,
never curving, no fork, no junction, no crossing, no end. It is identical in
width and character along its whole length, so the top edge and the bottom edge
match up when the picture is stacked head to tail. There is nothing special
anywhere along it — no landmark, no stepping stone, no sandbar, no rock, no
island, no bridge, no boat, no patch that would stand out if this stretch were
repeated.

NO DIRECTION — CRITICAL: the surface of the water must look the same whichever
way the picture is turned, because this same water will be used for stretches of
river running in six different directions. NO current, NO flow lines, NO streaks,
NO wake, NO long wave crests, NO swirls, NO foam, and no mark of any kind that
runs along the length of the river or shows which way the water is moving.

SURFACE — the important part: a FINE, DENSE, ALL-OVER SPARKLE. The whole river,
edge to edge and end to end, is covered in many small bright ripple highlights —
the crowded net of light on the bottom of a sunlit swimming pool, seen from
directly overhead. Hundreds of them, small and close together: each highlight is
about a fortieth of the frame across (roughly 25px in a 1024px picture) and never
wider than a twentieth. They lie at all angles and are spaced unevenly, so the
pattern has no grain, no rows, and no direction. Drawn the game's way — crisp,
flat, stylized shapes of light — NOT a photograph, NOT soft glowing photoreal
caustics, NOT blurred.

NOT large soft blobs: NO big rounded patches of paler or deeper water, NO clouds,
NO spots, NO stains, NO blotches, NO puddle shapes. Anything wider than a
twentieth of the frame reads as a landmark and shows up as an obvious repeat when
this stretch of river is cut into tiles and laid end to end — a few big pale
blobs is the single worst failure for this picture.

The river is WIDE: about three fifths of the frame across, leaving a strip of
white of about one fifth of the frame on the left and the same on the right.
Both edges run straight up the frame and are only very slightly irregular, the
way a water's edge meets the land — small, shallow waver only, never a bulge or
a bite that would eat into the white strips.

Each edge of the water carries a clear dark outline, the same weight of outline
as the rest of the game's map art, so the band reads as a defined shape against
the white.

COLOR: light, clear CYAN-BLUE water, roughly hex #6FB6CC — the pale blue of
shallow sunlit water, clearly bluer and clearly LIGHTER than a greyed teal.
This is the brightest, most colourful thing on the map and it is meant to be:
it should lift the board beside the game's grass (#B4C6A0) and its dirt roads
(#D8C39A). Do not grey it down, do not darken it, do not push it toward green.
The sparkle highlights are a pale, light version of that same blue — bright
enough to glitter, but still tinted blue and kept OFF pure white, and always
small: no broad pale area anywhere. Equally: NOT neon, NOT cartoon primary blue,
NOT electric turquoise, NOT muddy brown, NOT grey, NOT near-black.
Nothing of the riverbed shows through — no stones, no gravel, no sand, no weed,
no silt.

Lit brightly and evenly from straight above, with NO cast shadow at all and NO
reflection of anything: this is flat water seen from directly overhead, and
there is no dark smear or soft dark band alongside it.
