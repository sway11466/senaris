SUBJECT: Clear, still, deep RIVER WATER seen from DIRECTLY OVERHEAD, filling the

the water, nowhere near its edges.

COLOUR: clear, bright BLUE water, exactly hex #63A0D6 — a true sky-leaning blue,
NOT a cyan and NOT a teal. Keep this exact colour: do NOT push it toward green,
do NOT grey it down, do NOT darken it, and equally do NOT lighten it and do NOT
brighten it to neon, cartoon primary blue or electric turquoise. This is the most
colourful thing on the game's map and it is meant to be — it has to lift the

SURFACE: a FINE, DENSE, ALL-OVER SPARKLE — many small bright ripple highlights,
the crowded net of light on the bottom of a sunlit swimming pool seen from
straight above. Hundreds of them, small and close together: each highlight is

wider than a twentieth. They lie at all angles and are spaced unevenly, so the
pattern has no grain, no rows, no grid and no direction. Drawn the game's way —
crisp, flat, stylized shapes of light — NOT a photograph, NOT soft glowing
photoreal caustics, NOT blurred. The highlights are a pale, light version of the
same blue: bright enough to glitter, still clearly tinted blue, never pure white,
and always small.

NO DIRECTION — CRITICAL: the surface must look the same whichever way the picture

mirrored tile by tile. NO current, NO flow lines, NO streaks, NO wake, NO long
wave crests, NO swirls, NO spray, and no mark of any kind that shows which way
the water is moving.

NO LANDMARKS — the single worst failure: anything wider than a twentieth of the
frame reads as a feature and shows up as an obvious repeat when this water is cut
into tiles and laid across the board. NO large soft blobs, NO big rounded patches
of paler or deeper water, NO clouds, NO spots, NO stains, NO blotches, NO puddle
shapes, NO single focal thing anywhere. Keep the large-scale colour even and
low-contrast across the whole frame.

Lit brightly and evenly from straight above, with NO cast shadow at all and NO
reflection of anything — no sky, no cloud, no bank, no tree. Flat water seen from
directly overhead, with no dark smear and no soft dark band anywhere.
