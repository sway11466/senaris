Simple bold shapes: a rounded cranium, two large empty eye sockets, a small triangular nose hole, and a short row of teeth. A pale sickly green core with a mid-tone teal shadow and a darker teal outline, three tones only, hard flat edges — a cut-out graphic stamp, NOT a soft glow, NOT smoke, NOT particles. Below or around the jaw, two or three short curved wisp strokes in the same teal suggest necrotic vapor — flat solid strokes, not a haze. The skull is grim and clean, like a seal pressed onto the enemy. It fills the frame with only a thin margin. No body, no hands, no candles, no ground.
