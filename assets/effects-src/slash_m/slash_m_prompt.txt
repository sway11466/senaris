SUBJECT: A small sword slash — EXACTLY ONE crescent arc and nothing else. NO horizontal stroke, NO straight sliver crossing the arc, NO second blade trail, NO impact star, NO debris: if you are about to add a second shape, do not. The single arc is a narrow curved sliver, as if a short sword swept through the air toward the RIGHT: its convex edge faces right and its two tips taper to fine points (like a closing parenthesis). Silver-white core with a pale ice-blue edge, two tones only, hard flat edges. Faction-neutral — NOT blue-tinted armor colors, NOT red; this same cut is used by both the player's soldiers and goblins. Modest and quick: a light weapon's mark, thin and clean, not a heavy gash. Pure black background (single flat color, for easy cutout).
