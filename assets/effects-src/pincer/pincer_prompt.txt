SUBJECT: A SHEAR CUT — the trail left by two blades scissoring shut, drawn as TWO thin crescent slash arcs that CROSS. The upper arc enters at the upper LEFT and sweeps down to the RIGHT; the lower arc enters at the lower LEFT and sweeps up to the RIGHT; they cross each other near the RIGHT third of the frame and each runs a little past the crossing, so the pair forms a narrow X that opens wide toward the LEFT. Each arc is a slender blade trail — a thin crescent, widest in the middle and tapering to a hairline point at both ends, with a smooth clean edge and no notches. The two arcs are the same length and mirror each other. THE FRAME IS OTHERWISE EMPTY (background shows through everywhere between and around the two arcs). Silvery blade-trail colors, two flat tones only: a pale silver-white body with a cool muted steel-blue rim along one edge, hard flat edges. NO blade, NO scissors, NO pincer, NO claw, NO hand, NO blood, NO wound, NO flesh, NO sparks, NO debris, NO third arc — just the two crossing slash trails hanging in the air. The pair fills the frame with only a thin margin. Pure black background (single flat color, for easy cutout).
