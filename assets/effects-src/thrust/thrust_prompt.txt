SUBJECT: A THRUST — the trail left by a point driven straight through, drawn as ONE single straight horizontal streak. It runs dead level from the LEFT edge of the frame to the RIGHT: a long slender spindle that starts as a hair-fine tail at the left, swells only slightly toward the middle, and narrows again to a needle-sharp point at the RIGHT end. It is perfectly STRAIGHT — NOT a crescent, NOT an arc, NOT curved, NOT tilted, NOT a cone, and there is only ONE streak, not several. At its widest the streak is no more than a fifteenth of its length. The frame is otherwise empty. Blade-trail colors, two flat tones only: a pale silver-white body with a cool muted steel-blue rim along the lower edge, hard flat edges, a crisp readable silhouette. NO blade, NO spear, NO sword, NO needle, NO stinger, NO arrow, NO hand, NO wound, NO blood, NO droplets, NO splash, NO mist, NO smoke, NO sparks, NO speed lines behind it. Just the single straight thrust trail hanging in the air. The streak spans the frame from side to side with only a thin margin. Pure black background (single flat color, for easy cutout).
