SUBJECT: A shriek mark — ONE stylized burst of sound landing on the victim, as if a scream has torn the air around them. Draw it as a ring of sound: three or four broken, concentric arcs opening to the RIGHT, each arc a bold flat stroke with hard edges, thickest at the right where the scream bites and tapering as it curls back; between the arcs sit a few short straight spikes pointing outward, like cracks in glass. The CENTRE of the ring is completely EMPTY — a wide open hole with nothing drawn in it, so the unit underneath stays visible. Three tones only: a pale bone-white core stroke, a dull violet mid-tone, and a dark plum outline — flat cut-out strokes, NOT a soft glow, NOT smoke, NOT particles, NOT a gradient. Keep it a clean graphic stamp with a strong readable silhouette even when shrunk. NO mouth, NO face, NO creature, NO hands, NO musical notes, NO text, NO ground. The ring fills the frame with only a thin margin. Pure black background (single flat color, for easy cutout).
