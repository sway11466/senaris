SUBJECT: A base-raid mark — ONE squat stone tower seen from the front with a banner planted on top, left-right symmetrical about a vertical axis except for the banner, which flies to the LEFT. The tower is a single heavy block, slightly wider at the base than at the top, crowned with three or four big square battlements; one narrow slit window sits in the middle of the block, cut in the darker tone. It is crude and thick-walled, a border keep rather than a palace: NO roof, NO spire, NO cone, NO dome, NO windows other than the one slit, NO stairs, NO gate detail, NO bricks or masonry lines. From the centre of the battlements a short thick pole rises and carries a broad pennant streaming to the left, its outer edge cut into a deep swallowtail notch; the banner is plain, with no emblem, no pattern and no folds, and it is big — about a third of the mark's width — so it still reads at tiny size. The banner is the enemy's, just driven into a place that was not theirs: the mark should feel like a claim staked, blunt and final. DRAW IT BIG: the mark fills the square canvas, the banner tip touching or nearly touching the left edge, the tower's base spanning most of the bottom edge, the battlements and the pole reaching close to the top edge. Leave only a thin margin, a few percent of the canvas — do NOT float a small mark in a large empty background. Warm amber-gold, one light tone with at most one slightly darker tone of the same amber inside the tower block, hard flat edges — a branding-iron mark, NOT a rendered building, NO texture, NO shadow, NO glow, NO ground line. Pure black background (single flat color, for easy cutout).
