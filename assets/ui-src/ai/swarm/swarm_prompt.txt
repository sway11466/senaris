SUBJECT: A pack mark — one wolf head stamped three times, every copy pointing LEFT. Arrange the three copies as a triangle: one head at the top centre, two heads side by side below it, with a thin gap of background between them so all three can be counted. The three are the same size, the same shape and the same direction, like three prints from one branding iron. Each head is a flat profile: a long wedge muzzle, a low brow, two sharp triangular ears at the back of the skull, and the neck cut off flat. In every one of the three heads the muzzle points at the left edge of the frame and the ears sit on the right side of the skull. The heads are lean and angular, wolves closing in together, patient and hungry. The group fills the square canvas: the top head's ears reach close to the top edge, and the two lower heads span from the left edge to the right edge and reach close to the bottom edge, leaving only a thin margin. Warm amber-gold, one flat light tone with at most one slightly darker tone of the same amber, hard flat edges — a branding-iron mark on a pure black background (single flat color, for easy cutout).
